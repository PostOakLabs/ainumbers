## plan_mode: shadow (D2(b) — binding until Tim flips it)

It NEVER writes the live plan.** The live `C:/dev/Claude/Projects/AINumbers/farm/FARM-PLAN.json` is written by `C:/dev/Claude/Projects/AINumbers/scripts/farm-plan-apply.mjs` ALONE — a zero-LLM FarmTicks step that validates each proposal with `C:/dev/Claude/Projects/AINumbers/scripts/lib/farm-plan.mjs` plus the per-edit step limits against the live plan currently in force, and which, while its `live_apply` constant is `false` (shadow), logs `[plan] SHADOW proposal <version> valid|invalid <reason>` and leaves the live plan byte-untouched. ## Hard rules (RULINGS 2026-09-24T23:4xZ)

- **D5(a) — no harness fields, ever.** The seat never proposes `prefer_harness`, `bus_overrides`, or `refill.bus`; any of them makes the plan INVALID (`lib/farm-plan.mjs` rejects them outright). Bus-feed owns harness choice and the 12 h failover (Tim 2026-09-18). The design-note §2 example that carried those fields is superseded.
- **D6(a) — consumers only from the allowlist or `none`.** `CONSUMER_ALLOWLIST` in `lib/farm-plan.mjs` is the only place a source's `consumer` may point: `mr → script:farm-report-rollup.mjs` · `webmcp → script:t5-harvest.mjs` · `constants → script:constants-sweep.mjs` · `smoke → script:live-smoke.mjs` · `grades`, `selector`, `doors` → `none` only. A proposal may choose `none` or an allowlisted value; anything else is INVALID. Changing the allowlist is a board row, never a plan edit.
- **Caps (design §4.4):** ≤ 3 plan edits per tick, ≤ 12 per UTC day (`edits_today` in the file is IGNORED — `loadPlan()` derives it from `git log --since=<UTC midnight> -- FARM-PLAN.json`); no single edit may raise a source's `per_tick` by more than 2 or its `per_day` by more than 6. The executor refuses a plan over these caps and falls back.
- **Channel cap:** ≤ 3 channel lines per tick (ADVERSARIAL-REVIEW F14): the one duty-5 line, plus at most two more when a failure forces it.
