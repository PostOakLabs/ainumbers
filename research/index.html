Research

The public record of our measurement work on AI models and engineering tooling: every benchmark page keeps the prompt as pasted and the reply as saved, with the grading method beside the result, so a reader can check any claim against the primary files.

This section is written for engineers and risk practitioners who want the primary record behind a published number, from the prompt that produced it to the pass that graded it.

Benchmark

Free Web AI Red-Team Benchmark

Sixteen free web chat models on ASIC physical-design and regulatory-compliance tasks, every prompt pasted by hand and every reply saved verbatim. Most of it was graded by mechanical recomputation against primary sources, across eight collection days and 663 responses, at zero spend. The evidence files live in the public data repository.
