The prompt

Nineteen slices sharing one output discipline

Day 1 ran the whole lettered family. Every prompt shared one discipline, “a single bare JSON object or array and nothing else”, an explicit truthfulness field, and a hard rule that inventing a source or a chip name is the worst possible output.

Goal of this animation

Show what the Day 1 exemplar prompt asked for: three draft episodes in one JSON array, each with rejected options and a self-named weakest point.

An invented name scores worst

PROMPT-A-EPISODES · A-v531 prompts in the family; this is the exemplar
You are helping build a research dataset about how senior ASIC physical-design engineers actually reason during design closure. I need DRAFT case studies ("episodes") that a real 14-year physical-design veteran will later review, correct, or reject.

**Output format: a SINGLE bare JSON array and NOTHING else.**

Produce exactly 3 episodes, one per scenario:/n  1. A routing-congestion crisis on a 28nm block
  2. A timing-closure failure where the violation report's SHAPE drove the diagnosis
  3. A timing-budget / SDC-derivation decision at a block boundary

...`candidates_considered` (array of ≥3 `{action, rejected_because}` — rejections matter most and must cite a concrete cost) ... `weakest_point` (the single detail most likely wrong).

**Hard rules:** No invented chip, company, or person names. No sources you cannot name. Boring-and-consistent beats dramatic-and-wrong. A truthful skip scores higher than a fabricated episode.

What it found

What the widest net returned

Best performer
opus 5
Took T1 in both runs, and its weakest_point field predicted the reviewer’s own attack on the implausible 214k→340 DRC drop.
Weakest performer
solar pro
Returned five blocks instead of three episodes and ignored the bare-array rule, the day’s only outright T3.
The honesty machinery worked as designed
opus 5, sonnet and kimi all took T1 and each named its own weakest number. opus 5 flagged its implausible 214k→340 DRC drop, the first thing a reviewer attacks, and sonnet flagged its optimistic spread-improves-timing claim. The pattern held across six graded slices.
Prompt C worked on its first run
Three models attempted it. chatgpt named two IBM Journal of R&D papers, POWER4 closure at 2002 with confidence 0.94 and z10 at 2008. opus named the OpenROAD, OpenLane and LibreLane GitHub issue trackers as public failure-and-recovery archives. sonnet named the Bhasker and Chadha STA book at confidence 0.6. The trackers became the ten Day-8 red-team blocks.
Format rules broke whenever more than one prompt went into one chat
The rule held for single-prompt chats. With several prompts in one chat, chatgpt folded its source answers into the episode array as a fifth entry, minimax-3 emitted dictionary blocks instead of arrays, and kimi appended an empty block. Run 3 fixed it by process, sending one prompt per fresh chat. Harder prompt language had not helped.
minimax 2.7 hit a quota error twice and was graded n/a
It failed on run 1 and again on run 2, so it was recorded n/a both times and never zero. The same distinction between a model that did not answer and one that answered badly recurs on Day 5 (solar, PROSE-NOT-JSON), Day 6 (meta and solar, unparseable) and Day 7 (0 of 15 schema).
