PROMPT-C1-TRID-CASES · C1-TRID-v1one of ten Day-2 prompts
This task is about the TRID fee-tolerance rules — 12 CFR §1026.19(e)(3) — the zero-tolerance, 10%-aggregate-tolerance, and no-tolerance-limit buckets for comparing Loan Estimate to Closing Disclosure charges.

Produce up to 5 worked tolerance-test cases, each with a fee table (LE amount vs CD amount per fee, bucket assignment per the rule) — and at least 2 cases MUST have mixed directions inside the 10% bucket (some fees increased, some decreased), because the 10% test compares AGGREGATE to AGGREGATE and mixed-direction cases are where implementations get it wrong.

...`computation`: step by step — per-bucket sums, the 10% threshold (Σle × 0.10), the aggregate comparison (Σcd vs Σle, NOT the sum of increases) ... `weakest_point`

Hard rules: the load-bearing rule is aggregate-vs-aggregate for the 10% bucket — a case that computes the sum-of-increases instead is itself the defect this suite exists to catch.

What it found

Three defect classes the vocabulary could not name

Best performer
kimi
Verified 20 of 20 first-batch claims, scored 12 of 12 on JCS, and was the only model to derive the canonicalization rule.
Weakest performer
mistral
Came bottom: six substantive JCS failures, WRONG-RULE on every IRB entry, and the batch’s weakest arithmetic.
Arithmetic correctness and rule correctness are independent failure modes
gemini gets the OFAC arithmetic exactly right and applies the US multiplicative treatment where the EU attribution test applies, on the one case the prompt was built to expose. grok is 20/20 on Class-1 and invents an EAR band elsewhere. Both close arithmetically, so a recomputation gate passes both.
The IRB grouping error appeared in four models under four different spellings
yuanbao multiplies where the grouping requires division. solar omits the /√(1−R) term entirely. minimax 2.7 multiplies and carries three further defects. mistral drops the /(1−1.5b) divisor from the formula itself. Four spellings of one structural error, and all four close on their own inputs.
Most honesty labels were real and 15% were boilerplate
weakest_point was populated on 55 of 55 files and on every item. About 40% name the true fault line: gemini’s OFAC entry names the exact axis on which it is wrong, and deepseek flags the rate year that is the wrong constant. About 45% are real but secondary and 15% are boilerplate. It still missed the one defect that mattered, deepseek’s TRID case 2, which does not mention its own input substitution.
minimax m3 dropped a JSON key mid-structure
In 3 of its 4 files minimax m3 loses a key mid-object: trid: {"name": …, "bucket": "ten_pct_aggregate", 500.00, "cd_amount": 350.00}, where the "le_amount": key has vanished. Recorded as damaged delivery rather than as a wrong answer, and the three recoverable entries were graded.
