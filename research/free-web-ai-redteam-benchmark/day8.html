The prompt

The literal template that replaced the abstract schema

JI-v1.2 is the whole intervention. The Day-7 pilot had shown that an abstract one-line schema was not a contract: fifteen models each invented their own verdict vocabulary. JI-v1.2 replaced the schema with a literal object to copy, and put the enum values directly against the field names.

Each model saw each of the ten drafts exactly once. The drafts were stratified by failure type rather than taken as the first ten: unmeasured post-state, resolution claimed with no fix described, a tooling policy that hides errors, a crash on degenerate input, measurement validity, nondeterminism, silent netlist corruption, a repair that took 40000 seconds on one endpoint, a misattributed root cause, and a bug closed without reproduction.

J-ISSUE-REDTEAM-V1 · JI-v1.2the fix that closed the Day-7 drift: a literal template instead of a described schema
**OUTPUT FORMAT (binding, read first):** your entire response must be exactly one JSON object matching the schema at the end of this brief. The first character of your reply must be `{` and the last must be `}`. No markdown, no headings, no commentary before or after, no code fences, no explanations — the JSON object only.

**Your review, as a single bare JSON object:** {"verdict": one of SOUND | SOUND-WITH-RESERVATIONS | SUSPECT | IMPLAUSIBLE-FOR-THIS-RECORD, "objections": [{"claim_attacked", "objection", "severity", "confidence"}], "mechanism_check", "evidence_check", "attribution_check", "missing_from_story", "most_defensible_part"}

**Hard rules:** attack the NUMBERS, the causal chain, and whether the intervention explains the outcome — not the writing. The pasted record is the WHOLE evidence base: do not browse the web or fetch any page. Do not invent thread content, commits, PRs, participants, or tool behaviour that is not in it. If the record does not settle a question, write “not-determinable-from-this-record” rather than guessing — a field-level escape beats a guessed objection. A claimed resolution with no measured post-state is an EVIDENCE defect, not a mechanism defect; classify it under `evidence_check`. If a claim is sound, say so — inventing objections is as bad as missing real ones. Fewer sharp objections beat a padded list. This review is a PRE-SCREEN for expert review, not a verdict on the underlying bug — flag, don’t convict.
Same three models as the Day-7 pilot: kimi, sonnet, deepseek. Chosen for clean records on the earlier truth-anchored instruments: arithmetic exact on 9 / 9 values, format conformance 4 / 4 on the reference canonicalizer, correct on the citation-anchor and measured browser-behaviour probes. chatgpt ran block 01 as a format pilot and was schema-compliant, but was not extended to the other nine blocks.

What it found

The contract held and the disagreement was the finding

Best performer
deepseek
Produced the most objections, 26 with 20 major, and was the strictest model on two of the ten blocks.
Weakest performer
sonnet
The least strict model: 8 major objections out of 22, and 4 blocks flagged suspect where deepseek flagged 9.
The literal template fixed the drift
Day 7 produced 0 of 15 schema compliance from a schema described in one line of prose. Day 8 produced 30 of 30 from the same three models against a literal object to copy, with no renamed or extra keys. The prompt family, the models and the chat surface were unchanged.
No model invented content and none refused
Across thirty reviews there are no fabricated quotes, no invented tool or chip names, no invented commits and no invented participants. No model browsed the web and none refused. The field-level escape not-determinable-from-this-record was available, and every model treated the pasted record as its whole evidence base.
Zero fatal objections reflects the models’ caution
The enum offers two reserved values, SUSPECT and IMPLAUSIBLE-FOR-THIS-RECORD. The second was never used by any of the three models on any of the ten blocks. Every draft carries a known defect by construction, since the set was stratified by failure type, and no model escalated past SUSPECT. The enum is wider than the models’ willingness to convict.
Unanimity tracked the record
Block 02 resolves the incident with the sentence “solved on the latest version”, no commit and no PR, and drew SUSPECT from all three models. Block 09, the STA false hold and setup record, is the best evidenced in the set and drew SOUND-WITH-RESERVATIONS from all three. Where the three agree on which records are thin, a human reviewer can start there.
No model emitted a bare SOUND on block 01
This is the check the Day-5 codebook failed. Shortest-alias-first matching collapses SOUND-WITH-RESERVATIONS into SOUND and manufactures a clean verdict. Across all thirty files no model emitted a bare SOUND, so no grader had to collapse a reserved verdict to reach one. deepseek reached SUSPECT on 9 of the 10 blocks.
Three models from one generation is a narrow basis
deepseek, kimi and sonnet share training biases we cannot see. Thirty reviews of ten drafts describe how those three read incident drafts. Three models from one generation cannot stand for the web field. The drafts are first-generation mining with no expert review yet, and if that review changes them these verdicts describe the old versions.
