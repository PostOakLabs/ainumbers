Regulatory Citations

[1]Directive (EU) 2024/1619 (CRD6) Article 76a: Prudential Transition Plans. Requires all EU credit institutions to adopt and publish transition plans integrating climate and environmental risks into strategy and governance. Effective January 11 2026.
[2]EBA Guidelines EBA/GL/2024/09: Guidelines on the management of ESG risks. Specifies requirements for identification, measurement, management and monitoring of ESG risks by credit institutions and investment firms.
[3]ECB Guide on climate and nature-related risks (2024 update): ECB supervisory expectations for banks on governance, strategy, risk management and disclosure of climate and nature-related risks.
[4]BCBS Principles for the effective management and supervision of climate-related financial risks (June 2022): Basel Committee principles covering governance, internal control, capital, liquidity and scenario analysis for climate risks.
