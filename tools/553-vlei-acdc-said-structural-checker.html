T553 · Cat-2 · Compliance · vLEI · ACDC · KERI · SAID

vLEI / ACDC SAID Structural Checker

Paste an Authentic Chained Data Container (ACDC / vLEI credential) JSON, recompute its self-addressing identifiers (SAIDs) against the CESR Blake3-256 or SHA2-256 derivation codes, and check the schema SAID against a pinned table of official GLEIF vLEI schema identifiers. Produces a per-field integrity report and an OCG check receipt.

SAID Recompute Paste-Mode GLEIF Schema Table v1.0
Scope fence – read before use Structural check – verifies the credential's internal self-addressing integrity, NOT the KERI chain of trust, NOT issuer authority, NOT revocation state. This tool recomputes SAIDs over the pasted JSON and cross-checks the declared schema SAID against a small pinned table – it never resolves a KERI key event log, never contacts a witness or watcher, and never checks whether the issuing AID is authorized to issue this credential type. Chain (edge) fields are displayed with their digests, not verified. A passing result means the credential is internally self-consistent and well-formed – it is not GLEIF-grade verification, and a reader who assumes otherwise has been misled by us.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Step 1 · Paste ACDC / vLEI Credential JSON
JSON-serialized ACDC (v, d, i, ri, s, a, e, r fields) – pasted, never fetched. CESR binary framing is out of scope; JSON only.
Step 2 · Integrity Report
Recomputed SAIDs vs. declared · schema SAID cross-check · edges displayed, not verified
Schema SAID table: 6 official GLEIF vLEI credential schemas, pinned from github.com/GLEIF-IT/vLEI-schema (main branch, fetched 2026-07-18) – QVI, Legal Entity, ECR, ECR-Auth, OOR, OOR-Auth. A schema SAID not in this table is reported as "not in pinned table", not as a failure – it may be a newer/older or non-vLEI schema version this tool does not carry.
Step 3 · Chain / Edge Fields (displayed, not verified)
The 'e' block, if present – node references and digests shown as-is
Step 4 · OCG Check Receipt
Deterministic execution hash over the check's inputs and findings
