Cat-31 · T531 · Open Templates & Agreements

DPA GDPR Art 28(3) Checker

Paste a Data Processing Agreement and run it against GDPR Article 28(3)'s 12 mandatory processor clauses. Each clause is scored present, weak, or missing. An overall completeness percentage and a Policy Mandate export give you a machine-readable compliance snapshot. Cross-references the Common Paper DPA and oneDPA open standards.

⚠ This tool performs clause selection and text analysis only. It is not legal advice and does not assess enforceability, jurisdiction, or adequacy under any supervisory authority's guidance. Consult a licensed attorney and your data protection officer before relying on any output.
