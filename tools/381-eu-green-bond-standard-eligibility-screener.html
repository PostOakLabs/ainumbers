EU Green Bond Standard Eligibility Screener

Screen a bond issuance against the EU Green Bond Standard (EU GBS, Regulation 2023/2631, applicable from December 2024). Covers taxonomy alignment of use of proceeds, pre-issuance allocation plan requirements, external reviewer obligations, ongoing reporting, and gap identification.

EU GBS Dec 2024 EU Taxonomy Client-Side
Scope & reliance — 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. EU GBS requirements are set out in Regulation (EU) 2023/2631. Technical Screening Criteria for taxonomy alignment are set by Commission Delegated Acts. Verify current TSC requirements and external reviewer registration with ESMA before issuance. Not a substitute for legal advice. Deterministic logic · no inference · zero PII · CC BY 4.0.
