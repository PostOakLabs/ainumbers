Pillar 3 Disclosure Builder

Assemble Basel III Pillar 3 disclosure tables per the BCBS consolidated Pillar 3 disclosure requirements (BCBS 309, March 2017; BCBS 400, December 2018). Generates KM1 (key metrics), OV1 (overview of risk-weighted assets), LIQ1 (LCR detail), and LIQ2 (NSFR). Enter figures from T201 (RWA), T469 (LCR), T470 (NSFR), and T471 (leverage ratio). ⚠ Verify all table row definitions against current BCBS Pillar 3 requirements and applicable jurisdiction rules (CRR3 Pillar 3 / EBA ITS for EU; PRA for UK).
