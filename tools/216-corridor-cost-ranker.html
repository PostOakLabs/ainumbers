About this tool

All-in cost = FX margin (bps × notional) + fixed fees (correspondent fee + SWIFT message fee + lifting charge + beneficiary charge). Cost components are indicative industry averages derived from World Bank RPW data and published fintech pricing as of Q1 2026.

Stablecoin costs are on-chain settlement estimates (blockchain transaction fees excluded; settlement network fee included). Legal and compliance costs of stablecoin use are not modelled.

  • BIS CPMI: Cross-border payments monitoring framework 2024
