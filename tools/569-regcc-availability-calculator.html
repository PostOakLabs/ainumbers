T569 · Banking Operations · Regulation CC · 12 CFR 229

Reg CC Funds-Availability Calculator

Look up the Regulation CC dollar thresholds frozen to their 2030 inflation-adjusted values, then enter a deposit date and deposit type to compute a funds-availability date using configurable business-day counts. The date math skips weekends only. Both the threshold table and the day-counts are reference figures, not a live regulatory feed, so re-verify against the current Federal Register notice and 12 CFR 229 Subpart B before any filing or compliance use.

2030 Thresholds Frozen Configurable Day-Counts Not A Live Feed v1.0
Scope fence, read before use This tool does not connect to the Federal Register, the CFPB, or any live data source. The threshold table below is declared reference data pinned to 2030 values per the CFPB's Regulation CC inflation-adjustment schedule, and the business-day counts in the calculator are configurable defaults we assign to general Reg CC deposit-type categories, not hardcoded citations to a specific 12 CFR 229 subsection. The date math also does not account for federal holidays; adjust the result manually for any holiday that falls in the hold window. Re-verify both the thresholds and the day-counts against the current Federal Register notice and 12 CFR 229 at consumerfinance.gov before filing or compliance use.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
