Story PIL Flavor Selector

Story Protocol's Programmable IP License (PIL) ships three ready-made "flavors" plus a parameter struct. Answer whether commercial use and derivatives are allowed, set an optional minting fee and revenue share, and this maps you to the matching flavor and emits the full PILTerms struct. Deterministic: the same answers always produce the same flavor and terms.

⚠ This tool selects among Story Protocol's published PIL flavors based on your own answers and echoes the standard PILTerms fields. It does not draft custom terms and is not legal advice. The Story PIL documentation and license text govern; consult a licensed attorney before relying on any output.
Story PIL PILTerms Zero PII Client-Side
Scope & reliance — 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. PIL flavor field values reflect Story Protocol's published defaults and may age — verify against the current Story documentation before relying on any output for commercial or legal decisions. May others use the IP commercially? No maps to the Non-Commercial Social Remixing flavor.
May others build derivative works? With commercial use: yes maps to Commercial Remix, no maps to Commercial Use.
Creator-set fee to mint a license token. Ignored for the non-commercial flavor.
Creator-set share of derivative commercial revenue. Applies only to Commercial Remix.
