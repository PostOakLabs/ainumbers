About This Tool

This tool simulates interchange qualification for Visa and Mastercard US domestic consumer and commercial transactions. It applies published qualification criteria to determine the applicable interchange tier and indicative rate, and highlights the optimisation steps required to achieve the best available rate.

Interchange rates shown are indicative US domestic rates as of the last review date. Actual interchange varies by card type, issuing bank, merchant category code (MCC), specific product programme, and acquirer agreement. Rates change periodically — always verify against the current Visa and Mastercard published interchange schedules.

⚠ This tool provides indicative interchange qualification guidance only. It does not constitute financial or legal advice. Actual interchange costs are set by the card schemes and vary by merchant agreement. Consult your acquirer or payment facilitator for your actual effective interchange rate.
