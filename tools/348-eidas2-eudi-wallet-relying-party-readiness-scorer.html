Regulatory Citations

[1]Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 (eIDAS 2.0): amending Regulation (EU) No 910/2014. Mandates EU Digital Identity Wallets available to all citizens/residents by December 2026. Regulated private-sector relying parties must accept EUDI credentials within 36 months of implementing acts.
[2]ETSI TS 119 461 v2: Policy and security requirements for trust service components providing identity proofing of trust service subjects. Required baseline for QTSP compliance with eIDAS 2.0.
[3]EU Digital Identity Wallet Architecture Reference Framework (ARF) v1.4: European Commission. Defines OID4VC, SD-JWT VC, and mdoc/ISO 18013-5 as core credential presentation protocols.
[4]EBA Opinion EBA/Op/2024/04: Use of digital identity solutions for customer due diligence under AMLD. Confirms EUDI Wallet credentials as acceptable for KYC/CDD purposes.
[5]OpenID for Verifiable Credentials (OID4VC) specification: OpenID Foundation. Core protocol for requesting and presenting credentials from EUDI Wallets to relying parties.
