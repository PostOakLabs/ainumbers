Scheme Fee Analyzer

Break down Visa and Mastercard scheme fees for acquirers and large merchants — assessment fees, FANF (Fixed Acquirer Network Fee), cross-border fees, NABU/NAPF — so finance teams can model and benchmark their total scheme cost.

Scheme fee schedules are based on published Visa/Mastercard US interchange and fee documentation (effective 2025–2026). Actual fees depend on your processor contract, programme type, and regulatory jurisdiction. Always verify with your scheme and acquirer.
