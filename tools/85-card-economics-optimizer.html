Optimization Levers
Surcharging is prohibited in some US states (CT, MA, OK, PR) and on Visa/MC debit cards. Durbin Amendment (Reg II) requires dual-network routing on debit. LCR routes to cheapest network. L2/L3 data can save 40–100bps on corporate, purchasing, and GSA card interchange.
