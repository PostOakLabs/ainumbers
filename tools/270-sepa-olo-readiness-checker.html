SEPA One-Leg-Out Readiness Checker

Evaluate your PSP's readiness for mandatory SEPA One-Leg-Out (OLO) requirements. OLO rules — mandated by EPC from October 2025 — require EU PSPs to apply ISO 20022 structured address, purpose codes, and currency conversion disclosure for all cross-border payments with a non-EEA leg.

Mandate Live — SEPA OLO requirements became mandatory for EU PSPs handling cross-border payments with non-EEA legs from October 2025. Non-compliant transactions may be rejected by CSMs or the beneficiary PSP. Key obligations: (1) ISO 20022 StructuredPostalAddress on all OLO transactions, (2) BIC mandatory on SWIFT OLO transactions, (3) currency conversion disclosure (CCD) elements required where FX applies, (4) Purpose Code mandatory for many OLO transaction types.
