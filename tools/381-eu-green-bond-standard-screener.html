EU Green Bond Standard Eligibility Screener

Screen bonds against the EU Green Bond Standard (Regulation EU 2023/2631, applicable from December 2024). Checks taxonomy alignment of use of proceeds, disclosure requirements, and external review obligations under EU GBS and the EU Taxonomy Regulation 2020/852.
