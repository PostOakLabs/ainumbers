CASS 15 Daily Safeguarding Reconciliation Calculator

Runs the CASS 15.8 internal (per-customer, floored at zero) and external (safeguarding-resource) reconciliation arithmetic for a UK payments/e-money firm on one reconciliation day, with a documented-non-standard-method toggle and an in-memory multi-day trend strip.

⚠ This tool encodes CASS 15.8 rule numbers and mechanics as documented in the FCA Handbook (pinned clause snapshot below, in force 7 May 2026 per PS25/12). A verdict here is an arithmetic finding about the figures you enter, never a determination that the firm has or has not complied with CASS 15 - that is for the firm's own records and its safeguarding auditor.
