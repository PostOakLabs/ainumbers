Regulatory Citations

[1]Regulation (EU) 2024/1624: EU Anti-Money Laundering Regulation (AMLR). Effective July 10, 2027. Establishes harmonised CDD obligations, reduces UBO threshold to 15%, and expands obliged entities to include crypto-asset providers, crowdfunding platforms, and football clubs.
[2]Directive (EU) 2024/1640: Sixth Anti-Money Laundering Directive (AMLD6). Governs national competent authorities, FIUs, and their cooperation with AMLA.
[3]Regulation (EU) 2024/1620: Establishing the AMLA (Anti-Money Laundering Authority), headquartered in Frankfurt. AMLA will directly supervise 40 high-risk cross-border financial institutions from 2028.
[4]AMLA Single Programming Document 2026: Sets out 26 RTS, ITS, and guidelines scheduled for 2026 and Q1 2027. Around 18 Level 2 measures directly impact financial institutions' CDD, monitoring, and reporting processes.
[5]Protiviti: AMLA Readiness Starts Now: Ten Practical Moves for 2026 (March 2026). Recommends baseline readiness assessment, gap remediation, and data/governance foundation-building in 2026 ahead of July 2027 AMLR application.
