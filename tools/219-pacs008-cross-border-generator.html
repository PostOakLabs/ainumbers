About this tool

pacs.008 (FIToFICstmrCdtTrf) is the ISO 20022 message for customer credit transfers between financial institutions. Under CBPR+, it replaces MT103 for cross-border SWIFT payments. SR2026 is the SWIFT Standards Release applicable from November 2026.

The UETR (Unique End-to-end Transaction Reference) is a UUID v4 generated per instruction: mandatory for CBPR+ payments and SWIFT gpi tracking. Do NOT use in production without validation against the live schema.

Cross-link: use Tool 77 for structured address batch validation (CBPR+ structured vs. unstructured address compliance).

  • ISO 20022 pacs.008.001.12: FIToFICstmrCdtTrf (SR2026)
  • SWIFT CBPR+ Usage Guidelines v2.0: November 2025
  • SWIFT gpi: UETR specification (RFC 4122 UUID v4)
