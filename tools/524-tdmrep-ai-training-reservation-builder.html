TDMRep AI-Training Reservation Builder

Declare whether text and data mining and AI training are reserved on your content. This builds the W3C TDM Reservation Protocol /.well-known/tdmrep.json plus the equivalent HTML meta tag and HTTP header, ready to drop on your site. It is the machine-readable opt-out that the EU AI Act expects general-purpose AI providers to honour, and it pairs with a content code so the reservation travels with the work.

⚠ This generates a standards-conformant TDMRep declaration from your inputs. Publishing it is a rights reservation, not a guarantee of enforcement, and is not legal advice. Whether a given crawler or model honours it depends on the operator; consult a licensed attorney for your jurisdiction before relying on it.
