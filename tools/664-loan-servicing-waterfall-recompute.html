Loan Servicing Waterfall Recompute

Independently recomputes how a single loan payment applies across escrow, fee, interest and principal buckets under the note's own declared application order, then diffs the recomputed breakdown against the core's actual applied amounts. The application order, bucket balances and payment are all your own inputs — this tool never chooses or infers a posting order, and asserts no universal statutory application order.

⚠ This is an independent recompute-and-receipt tool, not a claim that either side's figure is legally correct, not a substitute for your core, and not an audit of your core vendor. Use synthetic account identifiers only.
