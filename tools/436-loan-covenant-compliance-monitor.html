Loan Covenant Compliance Monitor

Test financial covenant compliance for up to 6 covenants simultaneously. Input actual financial figures, compare against covenant thresholds, calculate headroom, and model forward compliance under EBITDA stress and rate shock scenarios.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

Scope & reliance — Covenant definitions vary by credit agreement. Always verify thresholds, definitions, and cure mechanisms against your facility agreement's defined terms and qualified legal and financial advisers before relying on any output for compliance or enforcement decisions.
