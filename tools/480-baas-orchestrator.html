BaaS Orchestrator

Three-stage live sponsor-bank routing optimiser. Score and select a BaaS provider, construct a prefill-ready workflow chain via the AINumbers MCP server, then validate the programme against your organisation's policy budget envelope. Turns one-off BaaS due diligence into a repeatable, self-correcting algorithmic framework.

3-Stage Pipeline T152 → MCP → T320 Sponsor-Bank Selection MCP Workflow Chain Zero PII · Client-Side
How it works: Stage 1 runs a multi-dimension BaaS provider comparison and selects the optimal sponsor bank. Stage 2 calls the AINumbers MCP server's build_workflow_links to generate a prefill-ready tool chain for your programme type. Stage 3 validates the chosen routing against your organisation's AP2 policy budget envelope via T320.

Score BaaS providers across capital adequacy, product breadth (card issuing, ACH, wire, FX), API maturity, regulatory coverage, pricing, and SLA dimensions. The comparator returns a ranked matrix with a recommended primary sponsor bank and one or more alternatives.
