MiCA Market Abuse Surveillance Mapper

Maps insider dealing and market manipulation prohibitions under MiCA Title VI (Articles 88–97) to your entity type and services. Outputs required detection systems, suspicious transaction reporting (STOR) obligations, MAR regulation overlap analysis, and a surveillance programme checklist.

⚠ This tool maps obligations based on MiCA Title VI as in force from December 2024. MiCA market abuse rules apply to all crypto-assets admitted to trading on a CASP platform: not just ART/EMT. ESMA may issue additional guidance that supplements this mapping.
