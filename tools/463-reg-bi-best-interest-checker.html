Reg BI Best-Interest Checker

Assess a securities recommendation against SEC Regulation Best Interest's four component obligations. Returns a per-obligation PASS/GAP determination, overall best-interest verdict, and ordered remediation list. Applies to every US broker-dealer recommendation to a retail customer. A 2026 FINRA enforcement priority.1

T463 · v1.0 SEC Reg BI · 17 CFR 240.15l-1 US Broker-Dealer Scope 2026 FINRA Enforcement Priority Client-side · Zero PII
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

Care Obligation Inputs

Care Obligation requires: reasonable basis belief the recommendation is in the customer's best interest; consideration of reasonably available alternatives; and cost-consciousness across a series of transactions.2

Conflict of Interest Obligation

BD must establish, maintain, and enforce policies and procedures to identify, disclose, and mitigate: or eliminate: conflicts of interest.3

Compliance Obligation

BD must have a compliance program: policies and procedures: reasonably designed to achieve compliance with all four Reg BI obligations.4
