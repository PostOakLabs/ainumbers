Reg E Dispute Workflow Builder

Generate a compliant Reg E error resolution workflow for Electronic Funds Transfer disputes. Produces required investigation timelines, provisional credit rules, consumer notification requirements, and investigation steps per 12 CFR Part 1005.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope & reliance — Timelines are calculated in business days per 12 CFR Part 1005 and CFPB Official Interpretations (Supplement I). “Business days” for most Reg E purposes excludes Sundays and federal public holidays (but not Saturdays). The provisional credit and extended investigation rules for new accounts (opened within 30 days) and foreign-initiated or POS transactions apply automatically where indicated. Always apply institution-specific procedures and state law overlays.

    Regulatory Citations
    1. Electronic Fund Transfer Act (EFTA), 15 U.S.C. § 1693 et seq. — foundational statutory authority for consumer electronic funds transfer rights and error resolution.
    2. Regulation E, 12 CFR Part 1005 — CFPB implementing regulation; specifically §1005.11 (error resolution procedures), §1005.6 (liability of consumer), and §1005.7 (initial disclosures).
    3. CFPB Official Interpretations to 12 CFR Part 1005, Supplement I — authoritative commentary on provisional credit requirements, investigation timelines, new account extensions, and consumer liability rules.
    4. CFPB Prepaid Accounts Rule, 12 CFR Part 1005.18 — extended full Reg E error resolution protections to prepaid accounts (effective April 1, 2019); payroll cards covered under §1005.18(e).
    5. NACHA Operating Rules — ACH network error resolution procedures, return codes (R05, R07, R10, R29), and ODFI/RDFI obligations in parallel with Reg E requirements.
