CFPB §1033 Institution Classifier

Determine your institution's tier under the CFPB Personal Financial Data Rights rule (12 CFR Part 1033). Enter total assets and institution type to receive your Tier 1–4 or TPPP classification, exact compliance deadline, full obligations checklist, and a Policy Mandate for audit purposes.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ This tool reflects the CFPB §1033 final rule as published October 22, 2024 (89 FR 88638). Compliance dates are subject to regulatory and judicial developments. Consult qualified legal counsel before relying on this output for compliance planning.
