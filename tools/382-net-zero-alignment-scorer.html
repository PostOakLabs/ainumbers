Net Zero Alignment Scorer

Assess portfolio or firm alignment with three leading net zero frameworks: SBTi (Science Based Targets initiative), NZAM (Net Zero Asset Managers initiative), and the Paris Agreement 1.5°C pathway. Identifies framework gaps and recommends prioritised decarbonisation actions.
