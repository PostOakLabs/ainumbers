UDAAP Risk Assessor

Evaluate products and practices against the CFPB’s UDAAP framework (Unfair, Deceptive, Abusive Acts or Practices) under Dodd-Frank Section 1031. Scores Unfairness, Deception, and Abusiveness dimensions with weighted risk flags and recommended controls.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope & reliance — Scoring logic is based on published CFPB examination procedures, enforcement patterns, and supervisory guidance current as of 2025. Risk scores are illustrative outputs — they do not replace a formal legal or compliance review. Verify thresholds and guidance citations against current primary sources before relying on outputs for regulatory submissions or enforcement defense.

Marketing & Communications (Deception Factors)

Marketing deception analysis incorporates pricing and fee transparency signals above. A claim that omits material cost information can meet the CFPB’s deceptive standard even if technically accurate.

Consent & Consumer Protection (Abusiveness Factors)

Abusiveness under CFPB’s April 2023 Policy Statement includes “unreasonably tak[ing] advantage of” a consumer’s lack of understanding, inability to protect their own interests, or reasonable reliance on the covered person.

    Regulatory Citations
    1. Dodd-Frank Wall Street Reform and Consumer Protection Act, Section 1031 (12 U.S.C. § 5531) — CFPB authority to prohibit unfair, deceptive, or abusive acts or practices by covered persons and service providers.
    2. CFPB Examination Procedures: UDAAP, March 2022 — examiner guidance defining substantial injury, reasonable avoidability, and countervailing benefits tests for unfairness; material representation tests for deception.
    3. CFPB Supervisory Highlights, various editions (2012–2024) — documented UDAAP enforcement patterns across deposit accounts, credit cards, prepaid products, and digital payment services.
    4. FTC Act Section 5 (15 U.S.C. § 45) — unfair or deceptive acts or practices; parallel authority applied by the FTC and referenced by CFPB for non-bank covered persons.
    5. CFPB Policy Statement on Abusive Acts or Practices, April 2023 — clarified the “unreasonably takes advantage of” standard and enforcement posture, including stand-alone abusiveness claims.
