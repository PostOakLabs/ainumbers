Under GENIUS Act, reserves must consist exclusively of: (1) US coins and currency / demand deposits at insured depository institutions; (2) Federal Reserve deposits; (3) US Treasury bills with remaining maturity ≤93 days; (4) Treasury-collateralised repo (≤93 days). Allocations must total 100%.
