GloBE ETR Jurisdiction Calculator

OECD Pillar Two Global Minimum Tax: GloBE Effective Tax Rate (ETR) per jurisdiction. GloBE ETR = Adjusted Covered Taxes ÷ GloBE Income per OECD GloBE Model Rules Art. 5.1.1. Minimum rate: 15%. Substance-based income exclusion (SBIE): 5% × qualifying payroll + 5% × qualifying tangible assets (transitional rates 2024–2032 per Art. 5.3). Jurisdictions below 15% ETR are subject to top-up tax (computed in T474).

⚠ US Headquarter Exemption: As of January 2026, US-headquartered MNE groups are exempt from IIR and UTPR under the OECD "side-by-side" package (US Treasury announcement, January 2026). The top-up tax computed by this tool and T474 applies to non-US-parented groups and to QDMTT exposure for all groups (including US-parented) in GloBE-implementing jurisdictions. Do not apply IIR/UTPR to US-HQ groups. Verify current US position at oecd.org.
