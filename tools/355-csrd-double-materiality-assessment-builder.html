Regulatory Citations

[1]Directive (EU) 2022/2464 (CSRD): Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive. Requires large EU companies and listed SMEs to report sustainability information in accordance with ESRS. Wave 1 (large listed) reports for FY 2024.
[2]ESRS Set 1 Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772: European Sustainability Reporting Standards. 12 thematic standards (ESRS E1-E5, S1-S4, G1) plus cross-cutting ESRS 1 and ESRS 2. All subject-specific standards apply only if material.
[3]EFRAG Simplified ESRS Technical Advice December 2025: 61% reduction in mandatory datapoints for the simplified ESRS applicable to listed SMEs from FY 2026. Recommends streamlined materiality assessment process for smaller companies.
[4]EFRAG Implementation Guidance on Double Materiality Assessment (2024): practical guidance on conducting impact and financial materiality assessments, stakeholder engagement, and documentation requirements.
[5]European Commission FAQ on CSRD scope and Wave 1/2 timelines: clarifies phased application schedule, exemptions for subsidiaries of groups, and omnibus simplification package under development as of 2025.
