DORA CIF Function Identifier & Classifier

Classify your ICT functions into DORA CIF (Critical ICT Function) tiers: T1 (core client-facing / settlement), T2 (important support function), or T3 (non-CIF). Assess RTO against the 72-hour Art. 11 requirement for T1 functions. Flag TLPT scope. Generate Policy Mandate JSON. Client-side. Zero PII.
