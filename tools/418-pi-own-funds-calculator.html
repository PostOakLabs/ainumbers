PI Own Funds Calculator

Computes the own-funds requirement for Payment Institutions under PSD2 Article 9 (EU) and UK PSRs 2017. Calculates Methods A, B, and C, generates a full comparison table, and checks capital adequacy against your current holding.

PSD2 Art.9 UK PSRs 2017 EBA Guidelines Zero PII · Client-Side
Companion Tool — For E-Money Institutions, see T405 — EMI Capital Requirements Calculator (EMD2 Article 5).
Disclaimer — This tool applies published PSD2 / PSRs 2017 methodology. Actual requirements may be adjusted by your NCA or FCA. Always confirm with qualified legal counsel. All inputs are processed locally in your browser — no data is transmitted. Zero PII · deterministic logic · no inference · CC BY 4.0.
Method Selection & Inputs
UK retained PSD2 Art.9 rules post-Brexit via PSRs 2017 Reg.8. Thresholds unchanged.
Determines initial capital floor. AIS-only PIs: €50,000. All other authorised PIs: €125,000.
Method A only. Total fixed costs from prior year P&L per PSD2 Art.9(1)(a).
Methods B & C. Total value of payment transactions in the most recent 12 months.
Method C only. Volume from the year prior to the current year. Leave blank to use current year as proxy.
Method C only. Volume from two years prior. Leave blank to use current year as proxy.
If provided, the tool will calculate surplus or deficit against the requirement.
PSD2 Art.9 Formulas
Method A: own_funds = max(floor, 10% x fixed_overheads) Method B: own_funds = max(floor, tier(vol_current) x k) Method C: own_funds = max(floor, tier(avg_3yr) x k) where avg_3yr = (vol_current + vol_y1 + vol_y2) / 3 Tiers (PSD2 Art.9 / Annex I methodology): 4.00% on first EUR 5M | 2.50% on EUR 5M-10M | 1.00% on EUR 10M-100M 0.50% on EUR 100M-250M | 0.25% on above EUR 250M Floor: EUR 125,000 (AIS-only PIs: EUR 50,000)
Please provide the required input(s) for the selected method(s).
