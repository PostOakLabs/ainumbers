ICT Third-Party Contract Clause Checker

Validate ICT third-party service agreements against DORA's mandatory contractual requirements (Art. 30). Covers SLA definitions, audit rights, incident notification, business continuity, data location, exit strategy, and subcontracting disclosure. Outputs compliance %, missing clause list, model clause language stubs, and Policy Mandate JSON. Client-side. Zero PII.

v1.0
Scope & reliance — 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. Embedded rates, thresholds, and regulatory citations are static reference values that may age — verify against current primary sources and your own data before relying on any output for commercial, legal, or compliance decisions.
