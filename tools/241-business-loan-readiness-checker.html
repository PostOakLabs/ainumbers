Methodology & Citations
  • Lender criteria based on UK Finance SME Lending Standards 2024, British Business Bank Annual Review 2024, and published underwriting guidelines from Barclays, HSBC, NatWest, Starling, Tide, and Funding Circle.
  • DSCR thresholds: UK Finance standard minimum 1.25 for senior lending; government-backed schemes (Growth Guarantee) accept 1.0+. Fintech lenders typically 1.0–1.1 minimum.
  • Trading history requirements: high street banks 24+ months audited accounts; challenger banks 12+ months; fintech 6+ months bank statements; government-backed 12+ months.
  • This tool is for indicative pre-qualification assessment only. Actual lender decisions depend on additional factors not captured here. Not financial advice.
