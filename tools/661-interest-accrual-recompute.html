Interest Accrual Recompute

Recomputes per-day interest accrual over a day-count convention and compounding basis you declare, then diffs the recomputed cumulative total against your core's own posted accrual or interest-paid ledger entry. Returns MATCHES, DIVERGES with the exact divergence, or INDETERMINATE when there is nothing to diff against. An independent recompute-and-receipt over your own declared inputs, not an audit of your core, and not a claim about which convention it should use.

Core Verify Day-Count / Compounding Zero PII Client-Side
Scope & reliance: 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. Account/customer identifiers should be caller-supplied opaque tokens or hashes, never a real account number. This tool recomputes accrual arithmetic from the numbers you declare; it does not connect to any core, does not find "the vendor's bugs," and is not an audit of or endorsement/substitution for any core platform. Deterministic logic · no inference · zero PII · runs offline · CC BY 4.0.
