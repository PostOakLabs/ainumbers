Settlement Efficiency Rate Calculator & AST Benchmark

Calculate your firm's Settlement Efficiency Rate (SER) by fail cause, benchmark against the UK AST ≥98% target, and see what improvement is needed by the July–September 2027 measurement window. Deterministic · Zero PII · Client-side.

Scope & reliance — All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Regulatory thresholds (AST ≥98%, CSDR Art.7) are static reference values verified as of May 2026 — confirm against current primary sources before relying on any output for compliance or commercial decisions.
