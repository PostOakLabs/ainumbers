Cat-31 · T530 · Open Templates & Agreements

Agreement Template Linter

Paste any agreement and pick a target open standard. The linter compares the pasted text against that standard's clause set and returns which clauses are present, missing, or appear modified. A deterministic clause diff modelled on CommonAccord-style comparison. Use it to spot gaps before a review or to verify that a received agreement covers the expected terms.

⚠ This tool performs clause selection and text analysis only. It is not legal advice and does not assess enforceability or jurisdiction. Consult a licensed attorney before relying on any output for commercial or legal decisions.
