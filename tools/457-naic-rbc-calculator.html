Background — NAIC RBC Framework

The NAIC RBC framework requires every US insurer to hold capital commensurate with its risk profile. The RBC ratio = Total Adjusted Capital ÷ Authorized Control Level RBC. Four action levels trigger regulatory intervention:

  • Company Action Level (CAL): RBC < 200% — insurer must file a remediation plan.
  • Regulatory Action Level (RAL): RBC < 150% — regulator may examine and issue orders.
  • Authorized Control Level (ACL): RBC < 100% — regulator may take control.
  • Mandatory Control Level (MCL): RBC < 70% — regulator must take control.
