EU FIDA / Open Finance Readiness Scorer

Horizon-readiness tool for the draft EU Financial Data Access Regulation (FIDA) — the proposal that extends data-sharing obligations beyond payment accounts to mortgages, savings, investments, insurance, pensions, and crypto. Classify whether your entity and data categories fall in scope under the current draft texts, score your Financial Data Sharing Scheme (FDSS) and permission-dashboard readiness, and compare the draft against the US CFPB §1033 framework. For field-level data-category mapping, pair this scorer with T360 — FiDA Data Access Scope Mapper.

Draft Law — Not Adopted Open Finance FDSS · Dashboards Zero PII Client-Side
⚠ Draft legislation — status as of 6 June 2026: FIDA is not law. This tool scores readiness against the European Commission proposal COM(2023) 360 (28 June 2023) and the Council General Approach (4 December 2024). Trilogue negotiations began April 2025 and were reported paused in early 2026; adoption timing, final scope, and all obligations below may change materially. If adopted, application is expected in phases starting roughly 24+ months after entry into force. Treat every determination as horizon planning, not a compliance conclusion — re-verify against the current legislative state before relying on any output.
Scope & reliance — 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. Deterministic logic · no inference · zero PII · CC BY 4.0. Not a substitute for legal advice.
