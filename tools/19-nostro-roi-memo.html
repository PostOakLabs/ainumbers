        About This Tool
        ▾
      

      

        
Nostro/Vostro A2A ROI Memo Generator is a simulation prototype that helps bank treasury teams and fintech strategists illustrate the working capital and fee economics of migrating from traditional correspondent banking to tokenized A2A payment rails.

        
    
          
  • Trapped capital formula: Volume × (Settlement Days ÷ 30). This estimates monthly pre-funding locked in nostro/vostro accounts using the standard treasury liquidity model.
    • 
          
  • Fee savings formula: Volume × (Current CB Fee% − 0.20%). The 0.20% A2A direct fee is a fixed illustrative benchmark representing tokenized rail economics.
    • 
          
  • $27T industry reference: Refers to the estimated global value of funds trapped in correspondent banking pre-funding at any given time, per McKinsey Global Payments Report (public data).
    • 
          
  • 90-Day Pilot: A standard structured pilot framework referenced in the POL Engagement Roadmap for phased A2A corridor migration.
    • 
        

        
Target audience: Bank treasury officers, payments strategy leads, and fintech partnership teams preparing internal business cases for A2A rail migration.

        
Data privacy: This tool generates mock ROI memos using client-side JavaScript string interpolation only. No real bank data, transaction information, account numbers, or PII is processed or transmitted. All figures are illustrative and should not be used for real financial decisions without independent verification.
