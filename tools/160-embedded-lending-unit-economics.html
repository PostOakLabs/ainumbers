Embedded Lending Unit Economics

Model B2B embedded lending programme economics: origination cost, APR, default rate, loss curve, cost of capital, and servicing overhead. Outputs Net Interest Margin (NIM), Expected Loss (EL), Risk-Adjusted Return on Capital (RAROC), break-even default rate, and Markdown unit economics memo.

Embedded Lending NIM · RAROC B2B Credit Zero PII Client-Side Last Reviewed · 2026-05-12
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ Scope: B2B and commercial lending only. Consumer BNPL economics belong in Cat-16 (T184–T185). This tool does not constitute credit advice — outputs are for programme design and investor modelling purposes only.
