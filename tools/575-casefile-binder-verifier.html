Case-File Binder Verifier

Paste a case-file binder manifest to verify its signature and recompute its Merkle root over entries. For each entry, paste the referenced receipt alongside it to check its digest against what the binder committed to. The verifier never claims to independently re-verify a Helm bundle's or anchor proof's internals.

⚠ A binder is only as trustworthy as the entries it lists. This tool confirms the binder's own signature and Merkle root are internally consistent, and that any pasted referenced receipt's digest matches the committed entry. It does NOT independently prove the referenced receipt itself is correct beyond what its own dedicated verifier (547, a chain composite recompute, Helm's embedded verifier, or the anchor service) already checks.
