OCG Receipt Verifier

Upload or paste an OCG receipt, and this tool recomputes its execution_hash, checks any eddsa-jcs-2022 Ed25519 signature, and verifies RFC 6962 Merkle inclusion against an anchor binding – entirely in your browser, with zero network calls. Switch to snapshot-batch mode to check many receipts at once against one uploaded checkpoint.

execution_hash eddsa-jcs-2022 RFC 6962 Merkle Inclusion v1.0
Scope fence – read before use A PASS verdict proves: the pasted receipt's execution_hash recomputes correctly from its policy_parameters/output_payload; every attached eddsa-jcs-2022 audit_signature.proof verifies against the key it names; and any anchor_bindings[].merkle_inclusion path reconstructs the claimed root. It does not prove the timestamp authority or transparency log itself is honest or complete, that the signing key belongs to who it claims, or that the underlying RFC 3161/OpenTimestamps proof bytes are valid – this tool checks the hash, signature, and Merkle layer only, never the TSA/log's own cryptographic evidence. For that, or for witness-cosignature verification, use the Witness Cosignature Verifier. Nothing on this page ever opens a network connection – every check runs against the bytes you paste or upload. Publishing a receipt on your own page? Embed the OCG Verify Badge next to it so a reader can verify in place, or deep-link here.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
