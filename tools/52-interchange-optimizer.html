📋 Visa CEDP Product 3 — April 2026 Update
Visa launched Commercial Enhanced Data Program (CEDP) Product 3 in April 2025. Note: Visa began sunsetting the legacy Level 2 enhanced-data program in April 2026. PIN vs. Signature Debit RoutingDurbin / Reg II

Durbin Amendment (Reg II) entitles merchants to route eligible debit transactions over the lowest-cost network available. PIN debit via regional networks typically costs materially less than signature debit.
