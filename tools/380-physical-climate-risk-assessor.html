Physical Climate Risk Exposure Assessor

Maps portfolio or asset-level exposure to IPCC physical climate hazards per the TCFD physical risk pillar. Scores acute and chronic hazards across RCP scenarios and time horizons, and generates a TCFD-aligned disclosure narrative.
