DORA ICT Cascade Simulator

Monte Carlo BFS simulation of ICT failure propagation across bank-core, cloud-native, and legacy-hybrid topologies. Estimates DORA reporting probability, breach-time percentiles, and critical-path node exposure under DORA Regulation (EU) 2022/2554.

DORA Art. 10 Monte Carlo BFS ICT Risk Cascade Propagation Zero PII Client-Side
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
EDUCATIONAL: Simulation outputs are illustrative. Topology definitions are synthetic. Do not use for production incident response planning without validation by your ICT risk team.
