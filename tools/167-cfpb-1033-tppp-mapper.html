CFPB §1033 TPPP Obligation Mapper

Map your CFPB §1033 obligations as a Third-Party Payment Provider (TPPP). Select your provider type and active use cases to receive a full obligation matrix covering consumer authorization requirements, data minimization rules, purpose limitation constraints, prohibited practices, and consumer revocation handling procedures.

Scope & reliance — 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. Embedded rates, thresholds, and regulatory citations are static reference values that may age — verify against current primary sources and your own data before relying on any output for commercial, legal, or compliance decisions. Deterministic logic · no inference · zero PII · CC BY 4.0.
⚠ TPPP rulemaking under CFPB §1033 is ongoing. Obligations in this tool reflect the final rule as published (89 FR 88638) and CFPB interpretive guidance. TPPPs should monitor CFPB rulemaking for updates. Consult qualified counsel.
