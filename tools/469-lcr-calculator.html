LCR Calculator

Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) per Basel III LCR standard (BCBS 238, January 2013). LCR = High-Quality Liquid Assets (HQLA) ÷ Net Cash Outflows (NCO) over a 30-day stress period ≥ 100%. HQLA haircuts: Level 1 0%, Level 2A 15%, Level 2B 25–50%. Outflow rates per BCBS 238 Annex. Feeds Pillar 3 LIQ1 template (T472).
