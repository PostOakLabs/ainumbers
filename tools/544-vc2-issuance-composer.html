Credential Workbench · T544

VC 2.0 Issuance Composer

Compose a W3C Verifiable Credential 2.0, sign it client-side with an eddsa-jcs-2022 Data Integrity proof over an ephemeral did:key, verify the signature in-page, and export the signed credential plus an OCG Standard §23 vc-2.0 input-attestation block ready to pointer into a chain's policy_parameters.

VC 2.0 eddsa-jcs-2022 Zero PII Client-Side
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ This credential is self-issued: the signing key is generated in your browser (or pasted by you) and is not anchored to any external identity registry. A verified signature proves the credential was not altered after signing and that it was signed by whoever holds that key; it is not, by itself, identity assurance. See the credential workbench guide for what a receipt does and does not attest.
