Sources & References
  1. Council Directive 2006/112/EC: EU VAT Directive, Annex III (goods/services eligible for reduced rates) and Art. 98–105 (reduced rate provisions). Amended by Directive 2022/542/EU (effective April 2022) expanding reduced rate flexibility.
  2. European Commission, VAT Rates Applied in the Member States of the European Union (periodic publication). Rates current as at May 2026.
  3. UK HMRC, VAT Notice 700: The VAT Guide and VAT Notice 701 series (reduced/zero-rating). UK standard rate 20%, reduced rate 5% (domestic energy, children's car seats), zero rate (food, books, children's clothing).
  4. Norway Skatteetaten, MVA (Merverdiavgift): Value Added Tax in Norway. Standard 25%, reduced 15% (food), 12% (passenger transport, hotel, cinema).
  5. Swiss Federal Tax Administration (ESTV/AFC), MWST/TVA/IVA Rates. Standard 8.1% (since Jan 2024), special 3.8% (accommodation), reduced 2.6% (food, medicines, books, newspapers).
  6. Australian Taxation Office (ATO), GST: Goods and Services Tax. Standard rate 10%; broad exemptions include fresh food, medical, educational, and financial services.
  7. Inland Revenue New Zealand (IRD), GST: Goods and Services Tax. Standard rate 15%; applies to all supplies except exempt financial services and residential rents.
