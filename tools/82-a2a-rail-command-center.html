Tool 82 · Core Infrastructure

A2A Payment Rail
Optimization Command Center

Five-stage strategic rail selection framework: score FedNow, RTP, Same-Day ACH, SEPA Instant, and SWIFT gpi against your use case → model all-in costs → quantify liquidity impact → map compliance obligations → export a Nygard ADR with NPV/IRR business case. Powered by the largest A2A tool cluster in the suite (Tools 01, 09, 17, 18, 23, 39, 46, 78). Entirely client-side. Zero PII.

5 Stages · ADR Export 📊 NPV / IRR Analysis 8 Upstream Tools v1.0
Scope & reliance — 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. Embedded rates, thresholds, and regulatory citations are static reference values that may age — verify against current primary sources and your own data before relying on any output for commercial, legal, or compliance decisions. Deterministic logic · no inference · zero PII · runs offline · CC BY 4.0.
How to use this tool Work through all five stages in order. Each stage unlocks the next after required selections are made. Stage 1–4 gather your use-case inputs; Stage 5 generates a complete Nygard-format ADR with NPV/IRR business case. Use the stage bar above to navigate between completed stages. References Tool 78 (Smart Routing Advisor).
