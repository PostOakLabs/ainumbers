Open Finance Data Product Revenue Modeler

Model the commercial case for financial institutions building premium data products under the EU FIDA regulation and UK open finance expansion. Estimate API tier pricing economics, TPP revenue, 3-year P&L, and break-even: so you can assess whether open finance is a cost centre or a revenue opportunity.

FIDA Open Finance API Monetisation Client-Side
Scope & reliance: 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. Revenue projections are illustrative models based on user inputs and market assumptions. Actual FIDA implementation timelines, permitted pricing structures, and TPP ecosystem development will vary significantly. Verify against EU FIDA final text and FCA open finance consultation before making investment decisions. Deterministic logic · no inference · zero PII · runs offline · CC BY 4.0.
