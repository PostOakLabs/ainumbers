Clause Coverage Scorer

Paste any agreement and pick a named clause taxonomy. The scorer measures how many of that taxonomy's clauses appear in the document, returns a coverage percentage, highlights what is missing or present, and assigns a maturity tier (Foundational, Developing, Proficient, Advanced). Use it to benchmark a received or drafted agreement against an open industry standard before a detailed legal review.

⚠ This tool performs clause selection and text analysis only. It is not legal advice and does not assess enforceability or completeness in a legal sense. Consult a licensed attorney before relying on any output for commercial or legal decisions.
