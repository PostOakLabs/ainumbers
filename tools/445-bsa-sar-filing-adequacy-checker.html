BSA/SAR Filing Adequacy Checker

Evaluate whether a Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) meets FinCEN’s BSA filing thresholds and content adequacy requirements under 31 CFR Part 1020. Checks mandatory filing triggers, content completeness, and filing timeliness.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope & reliance — Threshold and filing rules reflect FinCEN regulations and guidance current as of mid-2025. This tool does not constitute legal advice and does not replace a qualified BSA Officer review. SAR filing determinations must be made by appropriately trained and designated compliance personnel. Verify thresholds and guidance citations against current primary sources before relying on outputs for regulatory submissions or enforcement defense.

    Regulatory Sources
    1. Bank Secrecy Act (BSA), 31 U.S.C. §§ 5311–5336 — foundational anti-money laundering framework authorising FinCEN SAR requirements and defining covered institutions.
    2. 31 CFR Part 1020 (Banks) and 31 CFR Parts 1021–1025 — FinCEN SAR filing requirements by institution type, including transaction thresholds, narrative content standards, and 30/60-day timeliness rules.
    3. FinCEN SAR Activity Review — Trends, Tips & Issues — periodic guidance on SAR narrative quality, filing best practices, and law enforcement utility of SAR content.
    4. FinCEN Advisory FIN-2007-A003 — guidance on structuring detection, transaction pattern analysis, and SAR narrative requirements for structured transactions under 31 U.S.C. §5324.
    5. 31 U.S.C. § 5318(g) — SAR confidentiality provision and anti-tipping-off requirements; criminal penalties apply for unauthorised disclosure of SAR existence or content to the subject of a SAR.
