Creative Commons License Chooser

Answer the two questions the Creative Commons chooser asks, whether others may use your work commercially and whether they may adapt it, and get the matching CC license, its SPDX identifier, canonical deed URL, and attribution requirement. A deterministic decision tree: the same answers always resolve to the same published license. Ideal as the license step before tokenizing content.

⚠ This tool selects among published, standard Creative Commons licenses based on your own answers. It does not draft custom terms and is not legal advice. The linked CC deed and legal code govern; consult a licensed attorney for your jurisdiction before relying on any output.
Creative Commons SPDX Zero PII Client-Side
Scope & reliance — 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. License mappings reflect the standard Creative Commons 4.0 family and may age — verify against the canonical CC deeds before relying on any output for commercial or legal decisions.
