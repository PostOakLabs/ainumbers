RWA Tokenized Asset Securities Jurisdiction Mapper

Maps a tokenized real-world asset (RWA) to its likely regulatory classification and applicable framework across the EU, US, UK, UAE, and Singapore. With the RWA tokenization market at $29B+ (2026), the blocking compliance question is: is your token a security (SEC), commodity (CFTC), e-money (MiCA), crypto-asset (MiCA), or payment stablecoin (GENIUS Act)? This tool provides a jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction classification matrix.
