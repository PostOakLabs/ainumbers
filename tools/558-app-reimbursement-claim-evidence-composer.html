APP-Reimbursement Claim-Evidence Composer

Assembles VoP/CoP session receipts, customer-comms attestations, and a gross-negligence / standard-of-caution assessment into a hash-chained, Merkle-rooted claim-evidence bundle: the run receipt a PSP can hand to a reimbursement-claim reviewer. Two built-in checklist definitions: UK PSR (Specific Direction 20) and an EU cross-border pack that separates the narrow IPR mismatch-liability mechanic, live today, from the broader PSD3/PSR-EU reimbursement track, which is agreed but not yet in force. Reuses the CHECKRUN-1 checklist/SOP runner pattern (definition → step receipts → Merkle run receipt), not rebuilt.

UK PSR · SD20 EU IPR · PSD3-PSR track Evidence Composer Zero PII Client-Side
Scope: evidence composer, not adjudicator. This tool assembles evidence for an APP-fraud reimbursement claim, VoP/CoP session receipts, a customer-comms attestation, and a gross-negligence / standard-of-caution assessment, into a hash-chained, Merkle-rooted bundle. Customer-comms attestations and gross-negligence-assessment steps are asserted inputs supplied by the PSP: this tool binds what the PSP asserts, it does not independently verify it, does not adjudicate it, and does not decide whether a customer was grossly negligent. It does not determine liability. It is not legal advice. Under a 50/50 shared-reimbursement regime that distinction is the whole product. Rule citations below are verified as of 2026-07-18 and versioned; re-verify against the live PSR and EU publications before relying on this for a real claim decision.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
