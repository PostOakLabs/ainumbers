Crypto Transaction Risk Profiler

Score synthetic on-chain transaction patterns against financial crime heuristics based on FATF Virtual Assets Red Flag Indicators (2021) and FinCEN CVC Guidance (2019). Covers mixer/tumbler proximity, darknet market adjacency, peel chain patterns, rapid consolidation, dormancy-break indicators. Outputs risk score (Low/Medium/High/Critical), factor breakdown, monitoring recommendation. No live blockchain queries. Client-side. Zero PII.
