Margin Call Collateral Mobilizer

Compute initial and variation margin requirements and identify eligible collateral to mobilize. Supports derivatives (UMR/BCBS d499) and repo/SFT (GMRA) workflows: correctly branched by instrument type. Canton on-chain mobilization analysis included.
