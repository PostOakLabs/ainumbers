Regulatory Citations

[1]FCA PS23/16: Sustainability Disclosure Requirements and Investment Labels (November 2023). Introduces four voluntary sustainability labels, consumer-facing disclosure document, and naming/marketing restrictions for UK-authorised investment funds.
[2]FCA GC23-3: Anti-Greenwashing Guidance (finalised January 2024, effective May 31 2024). Requires all FCA-authorised firms to ensure sustainability-related claims are fair, clear, not misleading, and substantiated by robust evidence.
[3]FCA SDR Naming and Marketing Rules: effective July 31 2024 for new products, existing products by December 2 2024. Funds using sustainability-related terms in their name must meet the naming criteria or use an SDR label.
[4]FCA Dear CEO letter on sustainability claims (November 2023): sets out FCA expectations on substance of sustainability claims, governance processes for claims review, and steps firms should take ahead of anti-greenwashing rule effective date.
