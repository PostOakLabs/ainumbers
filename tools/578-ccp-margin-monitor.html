CCP Margin Monitor

A quarterly public benchmark recomputed from each CCP's own published CPMI-IOSCO public quantitative disclosure (PQD) - backtest coverage, largest margin deficiency, default fund requirement, Cover-2 peak stress loss, and total initial margin required. Compares FICC's three divisions and ICE's three clearinghouses on the fields each entity actually discloses; a field one side does not publish is reported unavailable, never interpolated.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
