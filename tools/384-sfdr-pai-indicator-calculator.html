SFDR PAI Indicator Calculator

Calculate the 14 mandatory Principal Adverse Impact (PAI) indicators required under SFDR Article 4 / Article 8 / Article 9 fund reporting. Produces a coverage-adjusted disclosure score and PAI table for annual SFDR disclosures per Regulation EU 2019/2088 and RTS 2022.

SFDR Art.4/8/9 PAI EU 2019/2088 cat-24 Client-Side v1.0
Scope & reliance: 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data: use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. PAI indicators and thresholds are defined in SFDR Annex I (Table 1) and Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/1288 (RTS). This tool provides indicative disclosure scoring only: consult your compliance team for submission-ready PAI statements. Deterministic logic · no inference · zero PII · CC BY 4.0.
