Risk-Adjusted Loan Pricing (RAROC) Calculator

Calculate Risk-Adjusted Return on Capital (RAROC) for a corporate loan. Model expected loss (PD × LGD × EAD), economic capital, net risk-adjusted revenue, and compare RAROC vs. hurdle rate to determine whether a loan is value-creating. Generate a break-even pricing spread.

RAROC Formula
RAROC = Net Income after Tax / Economic Capital

Economic Capital approximated via simplified Basel IRB formula incorporating PD, LGD, asset correlation, and maturity adjustment at 99.9% confidence. SA approach uses risk-weight table × 8%.

Value created when RAROC > Hurdle Rate.
