CoP & VoP Scheme Readiness Self-Assessment

Score your own declared answers against two live payee-verification schemes: the EPC Verification of Payee Scheme Rulebook (EPC218-23 v1.1, published 16 March 2026, in force since 5 October 2025 with v1.1 amendments effective 20 September 2026) and Pay.UK's Confirmation of Payee obligations. A separate, clearly-flagged track covers the CoP enhancement capabilities: multi-name matching and richer mismatch metadata: reported in industry PSP pilots ahead of a formal Pay.UK v2.0 publication.

Scope: self-assessment, not certification. This tool scores answers you declare about your own PSP. It is not an audit, not a Pay.UK or EPC scheme certification, and not a determination of scheme compliance: only Pay.UK and the EPC certify scheme participants. Every finding below is asserted from your own input, not independently verified. Rule citations are verified against the source publications as of 2026-07-18 and are re-stated with their document reference and date below: re-verify against the live Pay.UK and EPC publications before relying on this for a real readiness decision.
