T551 · Cat-2 · Compliance · LEI · GLEIF · KYB Worksheet

LEI Data-Quality Grading Worksheet

Paste a GLEIF LEI record and grade it against GLEIF's published data-quality dimensions – registration status, renewal timeliness, corroboration level, entity status, Level-2 parent disclosure, and address completeness. Disposition each finding (accept / flag / escalate) to produce a tamper-evident KYB worksheet receipt.

GLEIF Data Quality Paste-Mode Disposition Chain v1.0
Scope fence – read before use This tool grades the data quality of a pasted GLEIF LEI record – how complete, current, and corroborated the record's own fields are. It says nothing about the entity's creditworthiness, sanctions status, adverse-media exposure, or whether the entity itself is legitimate. A high data-quality grade means the LEI record is well-formed and current – it is not a KYB clearance, an AML screen, or a legal opinion. Treat this as one input into a broader KYB process, never as its conclusion.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Step 1 · Paste GLEIF Record
GLEIF LEI-Record JSON (api.gleif.org/api/v1/lei-records/{lei} response shape) – pasted, never fetched
Step 2 · Data-Quality Grades
Per-dimension grades, aligned to GLEIF's published data-quality methodology
Dimensions and grading logic follow GLEIF's Global LEI Data Quality Report methodology (completeness, accuracy, consistency, timeliness of registration/renewal data) – gleif.org/en/lei-data/gleif-lei-data-quality-management – and the GLEIF Level 2 (relationship) reporting-exception taxonomy for parent-disclosure grading. Grading thresholds below are this tool's own deterministic rubric applied to those published dimensions; they are not a GLEIF-issued score.
Step 3 · Analyst Disposition
Review each finding and disposition it – builds the worksheet's tamper-evident chain
Step 4 · KYB Worksheet Receipt
Record digest · grader version · grades · hash-chained disposition chain · timestamp
