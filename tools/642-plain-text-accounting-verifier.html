Plain-Text Accounting Verifier

Paste a plain-text-accounting journal in the hledger/Beancount style, and this tool parses it, validates double-entry balance and any inline balance assertions, computes an RFC 6962 Merkle root over the transactions, and builds an AINumbers Evidence Envelope v0.1 receipt – entirely in your browser, with zero network calls. The same verification logic is published as the art-653-pta-verifier chain kernel; this page is its browser-side reference implementation.

Double-Entry Balance Assertions RFC 6962 Merkle Root Evidence Envelope v0.1 v1.0
Parser scope – read before use Transactions start on an unindented YYYY-MM-DD line; postings are indented lines of the form Account  Amount (2+ spaces or a tab between them), optionally followed by  = assertion to check the running balance after that posting. Amounts are plain decimals with no currency symbol (100.00, not $100.00). At most one posting per transaction may omit its amount (auto-balanced from the others). A blank line closes a transaction. Any other line – account/commodity/include directives, comments, tags – is parsed as an unsupported directive: it is listed explicitly below and never silently dropped or guessed at.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
