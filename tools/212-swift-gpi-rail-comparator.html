About this tool

Rail data reflects published specifications and BIS CPMI monitoring reports. Cost ranges are indicative per-transaction estimates; actual fees vary by institution, volume tier, and commercial arrangement.

G20/FSB cross-border payment targets (2027): cost <1%, speed <1hr for 75% of payments. Status per rail shown in the finality column.
