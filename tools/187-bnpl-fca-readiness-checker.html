FCA DPC Regime: Live 15 July 2026. BNPL products previously exempt under Article 60F(2) Consumer Credit Act 1974 become regulated consumer credit under the Financial Services and Markets Act 2023 (Sch. 9). Lenders and merchants must hold FCA authorisation or appointed representative status before this date.[1]

Source Notes & Regulatory Citations
  • Financial Services and Markets Act 2023, Schedule 9: removes BNPL exemption from CCA 1974 s.60F(2); FCA policy statement expected mid-2025. Effective date: 15 July 2026.
  • FCA CONC 4.2: Pre-contract disclosure requirements; CONC 5.2A: Creditworthiness assessment; CONC 6: Post-contract obligations. FCA Consumer Credit sourcebook, version in force from July 2026.
  • Consumer Credit Act 1974, s.66A: right of withdrawal for regulated credit agreements; 14-calendar-day cooling-off period from agreement date.
  • Directive 2023/2225/EU (CCD2): repeals Directive 2008/48/EC. Published OJ L 2023/2225. Transposition deadline: 20 November 2025. Member states may apply CCD2 to agreements below EUR 200.
  • CCD2 Art. 18: creditworthiness assessment; Art. 26: right of withdrawal (14 days); Art. 29: right of early repayment; Art. 31: APR calculation using actuarial method; Annex II: SECCI format.
  • 12 CFR Part 1026 (Regulation Z): Truth in Lending Act implementation. CFPB interpretive rule on BNPL (May 2024): 4-installment BNPL treated as closed-end credit per § 1026.2(a)(17)(iv).
  • CFPB Advisory Opinion on BNPL (March 2023); CFPB Supervisory Highlights Spring 2023: BNPL practices; 12 CFR 1026.13: billing error resolution; 12 CFR 1026.7: periodic statements.
  • NY DFS BNPL Supervisory Guidance, June 2023: sets expectations for disclosure, credit reporting (§3), data privacy (§4), and consumer complaints (§5) for BNPL providers operating in New York State.
  • Methodology: obligations scored Pass=1.0, Partial=0.5, Fail=0.0. N/A obligations excluded from denominator. Composite score = weighted average across all selected jurisdictions. Tier: ≥80 Compliant, 60–79 Near-Compliant, 40–59 Partial, <40 Non-Compliant.
