On-Chain Cash-Leg Finality Checker

Validate the cash leg of a Canton settlement: finality model, reserve attestation, regulatory classification (GENIUS Act / MiCA), and depeg circuit-breaker. Produces a cash-leg assurance memo for the desk accepting tokenized cash settlement.

PFMI P8 / P12 GENIUS Act MiCA Title III Zero PII Client-Side
Scope & reliance — 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. This tool provides decision-support analysis only. Verify all finality and reserve determinations with legal counsel, your prudential supervisor, and the issuer's attestation reports before accepting tokenized cash settlement.
