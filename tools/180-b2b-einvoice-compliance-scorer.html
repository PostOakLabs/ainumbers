Sources & References
  1. France DGFiP, Ordonnance n°2021-1190 du 15 septembre 2021 — mandatory B2B e-invoicing. Large enterprises from Sep 2026, mid-size from Mar 2027, small from Sep 2027. All must receive e-invoices from Sep 2026. PPF/PDP CTC platform required.
  2. Belgium FPS Finance, Loi du 6 juillet 2023 — mandatory structured e-invoicing for all Belgian VAT-registered companies from 1 January 2026. Peppol BIS 3.0 format via Peppol network (Belgium uses HERMES platform).
  3. Poland Ministerstwo Finansów, KSeF — ustawa z dnia 16 czerwca 2023 r. — KSeF mandatory from 1 February 2026 for large taxpayers (VAT turnover > PLN 200m in 2024), 1 April 2026 for all other VAT-registered businesses.
  4. Italy Agenzia delle Entrate, D.L. 119/2018 (converted by L. 136/2018) — FatturaPA via Sistema di Interscambio (SDI) mandatory for all B2B/B2C supplies since 1 January 2019. XML format (FatturaPA schema v1.2.2+).
  5. Germany BMF, Jahressteuergesetz 2024 (JStG 2024) — mandatory structured e-invoice receiving for all German VAT-registered companies from 1 January 2025. B2B issuance: large enterprises from Jan 2027, SMEs from Jan 2028. XRechnung v3.0 or Peppol BIS 3.0.
  6. Australian Taxation Office (ATO), Peppol eInvoicing Framework — PINT AU (Peppol International B2B format). ATO mandated Peppol e-invoicing for Commonwealth procurement in Jan 2022. B2B voluntary but incentivised. A-NZ Peppol BIS 3.0 NZ/AU extension (A-NZ PINT).
