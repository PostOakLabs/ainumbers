ccREL Attribution Generator

Enter the four TASL fields for a Creative Commons licensed work and get a human-readable attribution line, a machine-readable ccREL JSON-LD block, and an RDFa snippet ready to embed in HTML. Follows the Creative Commons Rights Expression Language (ccREL) specification so crawlers, agents, and NFT platforms can parse the license relationship automatically.

This tool generates standards-conformant ccREL attribution metadata from your inputs. It is a formatting and selection aid, not legal advice. Verify the output against the current CC ccREL specification before relying on it for commercial or legal purposes.
CC ccREL JSON-LD · RDFa Zero PII Client-Side
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
The title of the work being licensed. Required for TASL and ccREL.
The name of the creator or rights holder to be credited.
The URL where the work is hosted or originally published. Used as the attribution link.
The Creative Commons license applied to the work.
A canonical URL for the work itself, if different from the source URL. Used as the cc:attributionURL in ccREL. Leave blank to use the source URL.
