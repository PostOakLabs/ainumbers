Physical Climate Risk Exposure Assessor

Map portfolio and asset exposure to IPCC physical climate hazards across acute risks (flood, wildfire, storm, heatwave) and chronic risks (sea level rise, temperature increase, drought, precipitation shift). Score by geography, asset class, and climate scenario — TCFD physical risk pillar aligned.

TCFD IPCC · RCP Physical Risk Client-Side
Scope & reliance — 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. Risk scores are reference-level estimates derived from IPCC AR6 regional risk assessments and published TCFD sector guidance. They do not replace firm-specific physical risk models or asset-level climate data services. Verify against current IPCC AR6 (2021-2022) and your own physical risk assessments before use in reporting. Deterministic logic · no inference · zero PII · CC BY 4.0.
