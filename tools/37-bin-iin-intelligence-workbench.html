Tool 37 · Fraud & Risk

BIN / IIN Intelligence Workbench

Decode Bank Identification Numbers without an API key. Luhn-validate PANs, identify card network, infer card type, flag SCA/PSD2 applicability for EU issuers, and map to interchange tier. Batch-process a CSV column of PANs — full PANs are truncated to BIN immediately in-browser.

Scope & reliance — Runs entirely in your browser for modeling and educational purposes. Embedded rates, thresholds, and regulatory citations are static reference values that may age — verify against current primary sources and your own data before relying on any output for commercial, legal, or compliance decisions. Deterministic logic · no inference · zero PII · CC BY 4.0.
v1.0 Luhn + BIN Decode CSV Batch Mode SCA / PSD2 Flags

Paste one PAN or BIN per line (or a CSV with PANs in the first column). Full PANs are truncated to BIN on analysis.
