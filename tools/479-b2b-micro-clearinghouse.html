T479 · Agentic Payments / M2M

B2B Micro-Clearinghouse

Three-stage autonomous procurement and micro-metered payment routing pipeline. Resolves the protocol arbitrage question, enforces an atomic spend cap via a W3C-compatible mandate, and validates the transaction against the team budget envelope: enabling sub-dollar M2M settlement that card interchange floors make impossible.

3-Stage Pipeline T276 → T285 → T320 Sub-Dollar M2M AP2 · x402 · Visa TAP Zero PII · Client-Side
How it works: Stage 1 selects the optimal payment protocol for your scenario. Stage 2 builds a tightly scoped cart mandate with a spend cap (e.g. $0.04). Stage 3 validates the mandate against your organisation's policy budget envelope. All three must pass before the routing record is emitted.

Compare AP2, ACP/Shared Payment Token, x402, Visa TAP, and Mastercard Agent Pay across credential, signing, scope, rail, and audit dimensions for your scenario. Micro-transactions <$1 favour x402 or AP2 due to interchange floor avoidance.
