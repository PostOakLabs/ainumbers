PRIIPs KID Compliance Checker

Evaluate whether a Packaged Retail and Insurance-based Investment Product (PRIIP) Key Information Document (KID) meets the requirements of Regulation (EU) No 1286/2014 and the Level 2 RTS. Checks mandatory content sections, format requirements, performance scenarios, risk classification, and cost disclosures.

⚠ This tool provides a structured compliance checklist based on published EU and UK regulatory requirements. It is not legal advice. Outputs should be reviewed by qualified compliance professionals. Regulatory requirements differ between EU and UK (post-Brexit) jurisdictions: verify against current primary sources before reliance.

KID Content Checklist: Mandatory Sections

Mark each section as present or absent in the KID being reviewed. All 10 mandatory sections must be present for a fully compliant KID under Regulation (EU) No 1286/2014, Art. 8.

"What is this product?"

"What are the risks and what could I get in return?"
SRI and performance scenarios present in same section
Summary Risk Indicator (SRI)
1–7 scale displayed prominently with narrative explanation
Performance scenarios
Unfavourable, moderate, favourable, stress (where applicable)

Regulatory Citations
Regulation (EU) No 1286/2014 (PRIIPs Regulation)Legal basis for KID requirements for retail investors in the EU. Mandates format, content, review, and provision of KIDs for all PRIIPs distributed to retail investors.
Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/653 (Level 2 RTS)Technical standards on presentation, content, review, revision, and provision of KIDs. Sets out the precise structure and content requirements for each mandatory section including Annex I template.
Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2021/2268Amends Level 2 RTS: revised performance scenario methodology replacing simulated scenarios with new prescribed approaches. Mandatory for new KIDs and material revisions from 1 January 2023 in the EU.
ESMA Q&A on PRIIPs KID (ESMA35-43-392, updated 2023)Authoritative Q&A from the European Securities and Markets Authority on scope, format, content, and supervisory expectations. Covers common issues including multi-option products and cross-border distribution.
FCA CP22/25 / PS23/9: UK Consumer Composite Investments (CCI)Post-Brexit UK divergence: the CCI regime replaces PRIIPs KID for the UK retail market. Firms distributing in both UK and EU must maintain separate documents under each regime.
