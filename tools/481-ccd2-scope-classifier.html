Cat-16 · T481 · EU Consumer Credit · CCD2

CCD2 Scope Classifier

Classify a credit product as in-scope or exempt under CCD2 (Directive (EU) 2023/2225). Determines applicable obligation tier: full regime or lighter-touch: for BNPL, interest-free instalments, POS finance, personal loans, overdrafts, and running-account credit. Rules apply from 20 Nov 2026.

EU Regime (CCD2): This tool implements Directive (EU) 2023/2225. It is distinct from the UK FCA BNPL regime (T187–T194, T329/T330). Do not use for products governed solely by UK FCA rules after Brexit. For the FCA temporary-permissions BNPL framework see the UK BNPL cluster.
