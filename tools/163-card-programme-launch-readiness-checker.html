Card Programme Launch Readiness Checker

Structured launch checklist for Visa and Mastercard card programmes. Seven domains: BIN sponsorship, scheme registration, KYC/KYB onboarding, Reg E compliance, network rule acknowledgement, fraud monitoring, and dispute resolution. Outputs domain readiness scores, critical blockers, estimated go-live timeline, and Markdown project plan.
