Card-Data Architecture TCO Comparator

Compare tokenization vault, network token, hosted fields, and proxy passthrough architectures for card data using only numbers you supply: the per-call vendor fee, one-time integration cost, and the annual assessment cost you carry at the SAQ level you declare for each approach. Outputs horizon TCO per architecture, pairwise break-even volumes with remainders, and SAQ-level cost deltas, all in fixed-point minor units.

Scope and reliance: 🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. Architecture labels are vendor-neutral: the calculator performs arithmetic on your numbers and treats each declared SAQ level as a label you typed, leaving eligibility and qualification to your own assessor. Cited text was retrieved as of November 2026; verify against the current official text and your own QSA or acquirer before relying on any output, since this page is not legal, compliance, or assessment advice. Architecture inputs (minor units throughout)
Tokenization vault
A vault service exchanges each PAN for a surrogate token and holds the mapping; detokenization happens inside the vault.
Network tokens
The card network issues a token per provisioned card; the vendor documentation states that network tokens are not cardholder data outside the Token Data Environment under PCI DSS.
Hosted fields
Vendor-rendered input fields inside your checkout; raw card data goes directly to the vendor and your systems never hold it.
Proxy passthrough
Card data crosses a vendor proxy in transit; storage and processing roles stay where they are today.
