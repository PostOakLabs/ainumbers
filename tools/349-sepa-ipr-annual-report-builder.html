Regulatory Citations

[1]Regulation (EU) 2024/886 — EU Instant Payments Regulation. Mandates PSPs to offer SCT Inst at fees not exceeding standard SCT, complete within 10 seconds, and submit annual NCA reports from April 2026.
[2]EU IPR Article 9 — Annual reporting obligation. PSPs must submit standardised reports to NCAs covering instant payment uptake rates, fee levels, VOP service performance, and rejection analysis.
[3]EPC SEPA Credit Transfer Instant (SCT Inst) Scheme Rulebook v1.1 2023 — defines 10-second execution SLA, IBAN-based routing, and 24/7/365 availability requirements.
[4]EPC Verification of Payee (VOP) Scheme Rulebook — mandatory from October 2025 for Eurozone PSPs. PSPs must provide VOP service to all payment service users before sending instant payments.
[5]EBA Guidelines on IPR Reporting (forthcoming) — European Banking Authority expected to issue implementing technical standards on the standardised NCA reporting template under Article 9 IPR.
