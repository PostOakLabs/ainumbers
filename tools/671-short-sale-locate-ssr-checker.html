Short-Sale Locate and SSR Checker

Deterministic classification of inputs you declare. Record a declared locate (source, list date, on-list flag) against a declared short-sale order, and carry the declared short-sale price restriction (SSR) flag through to the verdict. Every order fact, locate fact, and flag here is yours: the page holds no borrow lists, no SSR tapes, no cutoff feeds, and no clock.

⚠ This is a checker of declared-input arithmetic. It is not compliance advice, not a recommendation to borrow, locate, cover, or trade, and not connected to any live borrow list, SSR tape, cutoff feed, or register: an on-list flag is documentation you assert, never a fact this page verified. The not_proven discipline applies. An absent or invalid input is refused fail-closed and named, never silently repaired. Buy-in and close-out treatment remains your judgement; see the suite's T372 Buy-In Scope Classifier for that determination.
