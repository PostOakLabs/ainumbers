CVA Calculator (SA-CCR)

SA-CCR Basel III CVA engine for non-margined netting sets. Input trade type, notional, maturity, mark-to-market, and collateral to calculate Replacement Cost, Potential Future Exposure, EAD, and CVA capital charge. Supports up to 5 trades per netting set across IR, FX, Credit, Equity, and Commodity asset classes.

About This Tool
SA-CCR Formula Chain
RC = max(ΣV - C, 0)
MF = √min(M, 1yr)
SD = N × MF × SF × δ
PFE = mult × ΣAddOn
EAD = α × (RC + PFE)
CVA = ½ × h × RW × EAD × M
α = 1.4 (supervisory). h = 1yr. Multiplier floor = 5%.
