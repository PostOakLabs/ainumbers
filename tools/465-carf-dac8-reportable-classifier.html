CARF / DAC8 Reportable Classifier

Classify users and transactions as reportable under the OECD Crypto-Asset Reporting Framework (CARF) and EU DAC8. Determines reporting-CASP status, reportable-user residency, and which transaction types trigger reporting (crypto-to-fiat, crypto-to-crypto, transfers, retail payments ≥ USD 50,000). CARF effective 1 January 2026 across 52 jurisdictions; EU DAC8 for FY 2026 with first reports due 31 January 2027.

CARF · DAC8 · Crypto-Tax Deterministic · Zero PII · Client-side CARF live 1 Jan 2026 · DAC8 FY2026
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
