SR 26-2 Monitoring Bundle Assembler

Upload one or more receipt files you already exported from other AINumbers tools. This page recomputes each receipt's execution_hash directly from its own policy_parameters and output_payload, using the same canonicalization every AINumbers kernel uses, and reports whether the recomputation matches the receipt's own claimed value. It never trusts the value a file claims about itself. Verified and failed receipts are then assembled, dated, into a downloadable monitoring-receipt bundle.

SR 26-2 Independent Re-Derivation Zero PII Client-Side
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope & reliance This page checks receipt integrity only. It does not perform, and is not, model validation, ongoing model monitoring, or outcomes analysis under SR 26-2 (Supervisory Guidance on Model Risk Management); those are activities of your own model risk management function. A bundle assembled here is one input such a function may choose to weigh; it carries no assurance grade and no opinion on any model's fitness. Every entry, whether its integrity check passes or fails, ships in the bundle. Nothing is silently dropped. Deterministic logic, no inference, zero PII, CC BY 4.0.
