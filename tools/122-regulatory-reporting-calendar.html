Regulatory Reporting Calendar Builder

Map entity type, jurisdiction(s), and product categories to a complete AML/CFT regulatory reporting obligation calendar. Covers US BSA SAR/CTR/FBAR, EU DAC6/CARF/CRS, UK NCA SAR/HMRC DAC6, AU AUSTRAC TTR/SMR/IFTI, SG MAS STR, FATF cross-border declarations. Outputs 12-month compliance calendar with deadlines, form references, and penalty summaries. Export Markdown calendar or structured JSON. Client-side. Zero PII.
