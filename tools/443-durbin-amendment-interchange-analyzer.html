Durbin Amendment Interchange Analyzer

Determine debit card interchange fee caps under the Durbin Amendment (Regulation II, 12 CFR Part 235). Calculates whether an issuer is covered or exempt, the applicable interchange cap, routing compliance requirements, and merchant savings projections.

Reg II 12 CFR 235 Zero PII Client-Side
Scope & reliance — All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data. Interchange caps, asset thresholds, and exemption criteria reflect static regulatory reference values as of the 2024 regulatory year. Verify against current Federal Reserve Board guidance before relying on any output for compliance decisions. Deterministic logic · no inference · zero PII · CC BY 4.0.
Step 1 · Issuer Profile
Total consolidated assets as reported for the prior calendar year. The $10B threshold (12 CFR 235.5(c)) determines Durbin coverage.
Used to calculate the 0.05% ad valorem component of the interchange cap (12 CFR 235.3(a)(2)).
Step 2 · Exemption Screening
Government benefit programs (EBT, SNAP, Medicaid disbursements) are exempt from Durbin caps under 12 CFR 235.5(b).
Reloadable prepaid cards meeting 12 CFR 235.5(a) criteria (not marketed as a substitute for a checking account, no overdraft, etc.) may be exempt.
Covered issuers must enable at least two unaffiliated networks per 12 CFR 235.7. Network exclusivity arrangements are prohibited.
Context for savings projection. MCCs do not directly affect interchange caps but influence routing economics.

    Regulatory Citations
    • 1. Electronic Fund Transfer Act (EFTA), 15 U.S.C. § 1693o-2 — Durbin Amendment, added by Dodd-Frank Act § 1075 (2010). Authorizes Federal Reserve Board to prescribe interchange fee standards for covered issuers.
    • 2. Regulation II — Debit Card Interchange Fees and Routing, 12 CFR Part 235 — Federal Reserve Board implementing rule. Establishes $0.21 + 0.05% base cap and routing requirements for covered issuers.
    • 3. Federal Reserve Board Final Rule, 12 CFR Part 235, 76 Fed. Reg. 43394 (July 20, 2011) — Original interchange cap rule establishing covered issuer standards and exemption framework.
    • 4. Federal Reserve Board Final Rule, 12 CFR Part 235 (Oct 2022 amendment) — Extended routing requirements to card-not-present (online/e-commerce) debit transactions; compliance required by July 1, 2023.
    • 5. Federal Reserve Board Regulation II FAQs (2022) — Guidance on exemptions, fraud adjustment certification, and routing compliance obligations under 12 CFR 235.4 and 235.5.
