Control Attestation Campaign Roll-Up

Rolls up the control-attestation campaign you declare: you declare controls_total, attested, exceptions, unresponded, and the escalation threshold; the page computes the completion percentage, the exception rate, the below-threshold flag, and an ESCALATION_FLAGGED or NO_ESCALATION verdict. Every count here is yours: the page holds no register, no GRC system, no control repository, and no clock.

⚠ This tool computes arithmetic over caller-declared campaign declarations under named verdict rules. attested, exceptions, and unresponded are your declarations, never observations, and it never reads a register, GRC system, or control repository. Rounding is 2dp half-up, declared in the kernel and in the trace. An absent or invalid input is refused fail-closed and named, never silently repaired.
Campaign Roll-Up Completion · Exception Rate Zero PII Client-Side · Declared Dates Only
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Declared Campaign Counts

Declare the campaign counts and the escalation threshold. attested, exceptions, and unresponded are your declarations: this page never verifies them against a register, GRC system, or control repository. Completion is attested / controls_total, rounded half-up to 2 decimal places (a declared convention); below_threshold is completion strictly below the declared threshold.

Exam Pack Evidence Leg

This page covers the campaign completion / exception arithmetic only. The exam pack's evidence leg consumes this roll-up by pointer: the Policy Mandate export below carries the declared inputs and computed payload as its payload. The link is static: this page never reads the exam pack's data and the exam pack never reads this page.
