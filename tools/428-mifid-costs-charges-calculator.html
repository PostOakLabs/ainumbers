About
MiFID II Article 24(4) Requires aggregated ex-ante cost disclosure to all retail and professional clients.
PRIIPs KID Cost Categories Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/653. One-off, ongoing, and incidental.
Reduction in Yield (RIY) Standard MiFID II metric. Gross assumption: 5% pa per ESMA guidance.
UK Retained MiFID FCA COBS 6.1ZA maintains equivalent disclosure obligations post-Brexit.
