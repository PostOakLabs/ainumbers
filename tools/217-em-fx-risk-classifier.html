About this tool

Convertibility tiers: Freely convertible: no material restrictions; Partially convertible: some capital controls; Managed: significant restrictions, official rate regime; Non-convertible: exchange controls prevent free conversion.

NDF premiums are indicative 3-month contract levels. Volatility is 30-day realised (annualised). Data reflects market conditions as of Q1 2026: always verify with live market data before transacting.

  • IMF Annual Report on Exchange Arrangements and Exchange Restrictions (AREAER) 2024
  • BIS Triennial Survey 2022: EM FX turnover and NDF market depth
  • FATF: High-risk and other monitored jurisdictions, February 2024
