T581 · Cat-2 · Segment Reporting / ASC 280 · Worksheet Tool

DISE Caption-to-Category Matrix Builder + Coverage Checker

Paste your income statement captions and a trial-balance-caption-to-DISE-category mapping to compute a coverage matrix for the disaggregation of income statement expenses (DISE) required under ASU 2023-07 / ASC 220-40. Flags every unmapped caption explicitly and computes an optional dollar coverage percentage when trial balance amounts are supplied.

Worksheet Tool · No Chaingraph Node No FASB-Fixed Category Enum Policy Mandate Export Client-Side · Zero PII · CC BY 4.0
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope and category enum note: ASU 2023-07 (ASC 280-10-55-15D) requires disclosure of significant segment expense categories but does not prescribe a fixed, FASB-mandated list of category names: selection is left to management's judgment based on what is regularly provided to the chief operating decision maker. This tool therefore does not enforce a closed category enum. The category suggestions offered below are common industry examples drawn from secondary published guidance (reviewed 2026-08-15), not an authoritative FASB list; define categories that match your own entity's significant expenses. This worksheet computes coverage from whatever mapping you paste in; it never asserts that your mapping itself satisfies DISE, only that coverage was computed from it. Deterministic logic, no inference, zero PII.
