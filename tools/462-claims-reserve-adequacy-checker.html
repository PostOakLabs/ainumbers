Claims Reserve Adequacy Checker

About This Tool
Disclaimer Deterministic chain ladder and Bornhuetter-Ferguson calculation only. No confidence intervals or percentile estimates: stochastic reserving (Mack, bootstrap) requires additional modelling. The reserve adequacy conclusion is a mathematical comparison and does not constitute an actuarial opinion or satisfy appointed-actuary requirements under NAIC model law.
Development (Chain Ladder) MethodProjects ultimate losses by applying selected age-to-age factors to the latest diagonal. The dominant deterministic reserving technique for mature, stable lines.CAS · Friedland (2010)
Bornhuetter-Ferguson MethodCredibility-weights reported losses against an a priori expected-loss estimate (earned premium × ELR), stabilising immature accident years.Bornhuetter & Ferguson (1972), PCAS LIX
Loss Development Factors (LDFs)Cumulative product of selected age-to-age factors plus a tail factor: converts the latest diagonal to ultimate.CAS Exam 7 syllabus
IBNR & Reserve AdequacyIBNR = ultimate − reported. Redundancy / deficiency = booked reserve − selected IBNR.Friedland (2010), ch. 7–8
Tail FactorDevelopment beyond the last observed diagonal. Critical for long-tailed lines (workers' comp, medical malpractice); default 1.000 suits short-tailed lines only.CAS reserving practice
Method Cheat-Sheet
AGE-TO-AGE FACTOR (VW)
ATAF(j to j+1) = sum C(i,j+1) / sum C(i,j)
over rows with data in both cols
CHAIN LADDER
Ult = Latest * LDF(age to ult)
IBNR = Ult - Latest
% UNREPORTED
%Unrep(a) = 1 - 1/LDF(a to ult)
BORNHUETTER-FERGUSON
Exp = EP * ELR
BF Ult = Latest + %Unrep * Exp
IBNR = %Unrep * Exp
