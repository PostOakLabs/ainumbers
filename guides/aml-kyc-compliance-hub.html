Quick-Start Guide

Build your AML compliance program in six steps

A practical workflow for compliance officers and engineers building or auditing an AML/BSA program for the first time.

01
Establish your CDD framework. Use T109 — CDD/EDD Checklist Generator to build jurisdiction-aware onboarding requirements before writing a single policy line. Select entity types and jurisdictions to get a structured checklist with regulatory citations.
02
Design your customer risk rating model. Configure T110 — Customer Risk Rating Engine across FATF's six risk dimensions and set your institution's risk appetite thresholds. Export your weighting matrix as a Policy Mandate JSON policy artifact for use downstream.
03
Build your UBO and sanctions screening logic. Use T111 — KYB Mapper to model beneficial ownership structures and T112 — PEP & Sanctions Simulator to configure name-matching sensitivity and model expected false-positive rates.
04
Configure transaction monitoring rules. Use T116 — TM Rule Builder to generate structured rule pseudocode for velocity, structuring, and typology detection. Cross-reference T120 — Typology Identifier to ensure FATF typology coverage across all three ML stages.
05
Draft your SAR reporting workflow. Use T119 — CTR/SAR Threshold Simulator to map filing triggers across jurisdictions, then practise narrative construction with T121 — SAR Narrative Generator using structured field inputs for FinCEN, NCA, and AUSTRAC formats.
06
Audit your program and go agentic. Run T123 — AML Program Gap Auditor against your completed program across all five FinCEN pillars, then export a Policy Mandate AML Mandate via T131 for machine-readable policy enforcement in agentic payment systems.
