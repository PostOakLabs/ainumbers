Agentic Checkout Composer

Merchant readiness for agentic commerce in three stages: select the right checkout protocol for your stack (UCP, ACP, x402, Visa TAP), audit your product feed for ACP/UCP conformance, and build an agent-traffic acceptance policy mandate your API can publish for agents to read. All in one page — client-side, zero PII. Composite Policy Mandate export.

How it works: Work top to bottom. In each stage: ① Send upstream maps the previous stage's mandate into the current tool (where a direct field mapping exists), ② Run executes the tool's entry function, ③ Capture pulls the stage's Policy Mandate into the Composer. You can also interact with any frame directly — the Composer only reads what the tool has computed.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

Composite Policy Mandate

When all three stages are captured, export the combined run: a single Policy Mandate whose payload carries the three stage mandates in order, plus a Markdown transcript of the chain. The composite is validated against the Policy Mandate v1.0 required-field set before download.
