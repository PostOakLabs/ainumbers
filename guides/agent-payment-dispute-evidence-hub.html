Agent Payment Dispute Evidence Hub

Browser-based verify-only tools plus chain for assembling the evidence a chargeback or dispute review needs when an AI agent initiated the payment: was the underlying mandate's signature chain intact, was the disputed action inside the token's declared scope, and does the mandate/receipt, payment-protocol, and session/delivery evidence hold together in one hash-chained bundle.

Last Reviewed: 2026-07-25
What we verify vs. what we never do

Every tool on this page reads structures you supply: an AP2 mandate chain, an agent token or delegation record, or dispute-evidence artifacts already collected elsewhere. They check signature-chain integrity, scope and expiry bounds, and evidence completeness, and they say when a field can't be verified rather than guessing. None of these tools initiate, route, settle, or transmit a payment, query a live payment network, or move value in any way. They do not adjudicate a dispute outcome either — the adjudication bundle they produce is evidence for a human or downstream system to review, not a verdict.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

ART-01
AP2 Mandate-Chain Validator
Validates the AP2 Intent→Cart→Payment mandate trio: signature-chain integrity, scope/limit consistency, TTL/expiry, over-spend detection, Human-Not-Present flows.
ART-385
Agent Token Scope Checker
Compares a disputed action against a token or mandate's declared scope — spend cap, currency, MCC, expiry, attenuation chain — and returns an in-scope or out-of-scope verdict. Evaluation only, never enforces.
