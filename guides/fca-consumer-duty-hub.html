Regulatory Coverage

Regulatory Framework Coverage

Tools in this hub are mapped to the following FCA regulatory instruments. Verify current applicability with qualified legal counsel.

FCA Consumer Duty (PS22/9)

Consumer Duty rules effective 31 Jul 2023 (new products) and 31 Jul 2024 (closed products). Four outcome areas: Products & Services (PRIN 2A.2), Price & Value (PRIN 2A.4), Consumer Understanding (PRIN 2A.3), Consumer Support (PRIN 2A.5). Board annual review requirement PS22/9 §12.

FCA DPC — FSMA 2023 Sch.9

Deferred Payment Credit regulation effective 15 Jul 2026. Removes BNPL/DPC exemption from regulated consumer credit. Triggers FCA CONC 4, 5, 6, 7 obligations. Consumer Duty applies to all DPC products from authorisation. FSMA 2023 Schedule 9 scope criteria.

FG21/1 — Vulnerable Customers

FCA Guidance FG21/1 (effective Feb 2021) on firms' treatment of vulnerable customers. Applies alongside Consumer Duty. Four vulnerability drivers: health, life events, resilience, capability. Requires tailored communications, escalation processes, and MI monitoring. Reinforced by PS22/9 §5–8.

PRIN 2A — Consumer Duty Rules

Consumer Duty sits in PRIN 2A (the Consumer Principle, PRIN 12). PRIN 2A.2 Products & Services, PRIN 2A.3 Consumer Understanding, PRIN 2A.4 Price & Value, PRIN 2A.5 Consumer Support. Applies to firms distributing to retail customers. Underpins all 7 tools in this hub.

CONC — Consumer Credit Sourcebook

FCA CONC applies to consumer credit and DPC products. CONC 4 (financial promotions), CONC 5 (creditworthiness / affordability), CONC 6 (adequate explanations), CONC 7 (arrears / forbearance). Overlaps Consumer Duty for credit products including BNPL/DPC.

SUP 15.3 / SYSC 4

SUP 15.3 requires Material Events notification to FCA. SYSC 4 Senior Management Arrangements impose board responsibility for Consumer Duty oversight. Board must receive annual Consumer Duty report (PS22/9 §12.4) and MI must be designed to enable oversight (SYSC 4.1.1R).

Quick Start

Get Started in 4 Steps

  1. 1

    Classify DPC Scope & Map Obligations

    If your products include deferred payment or BNPL, open T329 DPC Scope Classifier to determine FCA DPC scope under FSMA 2023 Schedule 9. Then use T331 Consumer Duty DPC Mapper to map all applicable Consumer Duty obligations.

  2. 2

    Run Price & Value and Vulnerability Assessments

    Use T396 Price & Value Assessment to test each product against PRIN 2A.4 — the annual fair value review required under PS22/9 §4.25. Then run T395 Vulnerability Assessment Builder to identify gaps in your vulnerable customer treatment.

  3. 3

    Build Your MI Framework

    Open T397 MI Framework Builder to generate a complete management information suite for board oversight. Configure your firm type, product lines, and existing MI to receive a gap analysis with implementation priorities.

  4. 4

    Generate the Consumer Duty Board Report

    Use T347 Board Report Builder to generate the annual Consumer Duty board report required under PS22/9 §12. Export Policy Mandate JSON from all tools for agent pipeline and audit trail integration.
