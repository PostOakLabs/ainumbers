Compliance Officers

Payments Engineers

SEPA OLO implementation, SCA exemption routing, surcharging rules integration. Use T270 for OLO readiness, T419 to classify SCA exemptions by friction level, and T272 for cross-jurisdiction surcharging logic.

Risk & Finance Teams

Legal & Regulatory Affairs

PSD2/PSRs statutory obligation mapping, FCA CP22/15 compliance. Use T404 to identify authorisation gaps and T272 for cross-jurisdiction surcharging rule mapping across EU, UK, US, and Australia.

Regulatory Coverage

Key Regulatory Frameworks

Tools in this hub are mapped to obligations under the following frameworks. Verify current applicability with qualified legal counsel.

PSD2 / UK PSRs 2017

Authorisation, SCA, and safeguarding obligations. T404 gap-checks PI/EMI licence applications against PSD2 and UK PSRs 2017 Article requirements. T419 implements PSD2 RTS SCA exemption logic.

EMD2 / UK EMRs

E-Money capital and own-funds requirements. T405 computes EMI own-funds per EMD2 Article 5 Methods A/B/C. T418 computes PI own-funds per PSD2 Article 9 with €125,000 floor.

FCA CP22/15

Enhanced safeguarding for UK PIs and EMIs. T269 evaluates safeguarding method selection, permitted assets, daily reconciliation, acknowledgment letter, and shortfall calculation against CP22/15.

SEPA OLO Rules (EPC)

Mandatory EU rules effective October 2025. T270 checks structured postal address completeness, purpose code usage, and currency conversion disclosure for non-EEA leg transactions under EPC OLO rules.

FCA PS21/3 / PRA SS1/21

UK Operational Resilience framework. T376 builds FCA PS21/3 Important Business Services definitions, sets impact tolerances, maps vulnerabilities, and scenario-tests tolerance breaches. Board deadline: March 2025.

UK PSR PS23/3

APP Fraud Reimbursement, effective October 2024. T406 allocates liability between sending and receiving PSPs, computes £ splits and gross negligence defence thresholds under UK PSR PS23/3.

PSD2 RTS on SCA

Strong Customer Authentication and common secure communication. T419 classifies all applicable SCA exemptions — low value, TRA, trusted beneficiary, recurring, B2B, MIT — with liability shift positions for EU PSD2 RTS and UK FCA SCA-RTS.

Cross-Jurisdiction Surcharging

EU PSD2, UK PSR, US state laws (10 states), and Australia RBA. T272 determines whether card and A2A surcharging is permitted, banned, or cost-capped across all four regulatory regimes in a single navigator.

Quick Start

Get Started in 3 Steps

  1. 1

    Establish Your Licence Baseline

    Start with T404 to gap-check your PI or EMI authorisation readiness. Then compute your required capital: T405 for EMIs (EMD2 Article 5) or T418 for PIs (PSD2 Article 9). Assess safeguarding compliance with T269.

  2. 2

    Address Transaction and Product Compliance

    Check SEPA OLO readiness with T270 if you handle cross-border EU payments. Map surcharging rules across jurisdictions with T272. Classify every transaction type against SCA exemption routes with T419.

  3. 3

    Build Operational Resilience

    Map Important Business Services and impact tolerances with T376. Review APP fraud liability exposure with T406 and ensure your reimbursement workflows are ready for UK PSR PS23/3.
