Quick-Start Guide

Five steps from initial assessment to a funded, monitored facility.

01
Score your credit risk. Open T239 — SME Credit Risk Scoring Engine, input revenue, DSCR, business age, payment behaviour, sector, and adverse events. Export the Policy Mandate JSON SME credit profile as your starting audit trail.
02
Quantify the working capital gap. Use T240 — Working Capital Gap Calculator to calculate CCC from AR, AP, and inventory days. The funding gap output tells you exactly how large a facility you need.
03
Check lender readiness and choose the right instrument. Run T241 — Business Loan Readiness Checker to identify gaps versus specific lender criteria. Then use T242 or T244 to model invoice finance or RBF economics. Check T245 for non-dilutive grant options first.
04
Stress-test your cash position. Before drawing down, run T246 — Cash Flow Stress Test to confirm the facility stays solvent under revenue drop and payment delay scenarios. For a more comprehensive 13-week model, use Tool 89 — Treasury Liquidity Stress Lab.
05
Monitor your covenants. Post-drawdown, use T247 — Covenant Monitoring Dashboard to track DSCR, leverage, minimum cash balance, and current ratio against agreed thresholds. Export Markdown memos for board and lender reporting.
