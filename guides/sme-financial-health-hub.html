Audience

Who Uses These Tools

SME Owner / CFO

Pre-application readiness and gap analysis. Run T239 to understand your credit profile, T240 to quantify the working capital gap, and T241 to check readiness against lender criteria. T245 for non-dilutive grant options.

SME Lender / Credit Officer

Credit assessment and facility sizing. Use T239 for a deterministic internal credit score with dimension-level breakdown. T240 sizes the working capital facility. T247 monitors covenants post-drawdown.

Fintech / Embedded Finance

Open finance and RBF integration. T243 models CFPB 1033 / PSD3 data signal quality uplift. T244 prices revenue-based finance products. T242 assesses invoice finance eligibility.

Regulatory Coverage

Multi-Jurisdiction Frameworks

Tools in this hub are mapped to obligations under the following frameworks. Verify current applicability with qualified legal counsel.

🇬🇧 UK Finance · BBB

UK Finance SME Lending Standards and British Business Bank guidance. DSCR benchmarks, lender minimum criteria, covenant packages, and Growth Guarantee Scheme parameters. Tools T239, T241, T247.

🇺🇸 CFPB · Open Banking

CFPB Final Rule 12 CFR Part 1033 (2024) — consumer and SME financial data portability. Modelled credit signal quality improvement for SME underwriting. Tool T243.

🇪🇺 EU Commission · PSD3

EU Payment Services Directive 3 (expected 2026/27 transposition). Open banking data access for SME credit decisioning — data signal modelling. Tool T243.

🌍 SBIR · Innovate UK · Horizon

US SBIR/STTR, UK Innovate UK, UK Growth Guarantee Scheme, EU Horizon Europe, EU COSME successor. Eligibility thresholds and application requirements. Tool T245.

Quick Start

Get Started in 5 Steps

  1. 1

    Score Your Credit Risk

    Open T239 — SME Credit Risk Scoring Engine, input revenue, DSCR, business age, payment behaviour, sector, and adverse events. Export the Policy Mandate JSON SME credit profile as your starting audit trail.

  2. 2

    Quantify the Working Capital Gap

    Use T240 — Working Capital Gap Calculator to calculate CCC from AR, AP, and inventory days. The funding gap output tells you exactly how large a facility you need.

  3. 3

    Check Lender Readiness & Choose an Instrument

    Run T241 — Business Loan Readiness Checker to identify gaps versus specific lender criteria. Then use T242 or T244 to model invoice finance or RBF economics. Check T245 for non-dilutive grant options first.

  4. 4

    Stress-Test Your Cash Position

    Before drawing down, run T246 — Cash Flow Stress Test to confirm the facility stays solvent under revenue drop and payment delay scenarios.

  5. 5

    Monitor Your Covenants

    Post-drawdown, use T247 — Covenant Monitoring Dashboard to track DSCR, leverage, minimum cash balance, and current ratio against agreed thresholds. Export Markdown memos for board and lender reporting.
