FR 2052a: Report Structure, Appendix Inventory & Confidentiality

FR 2052a, the Complex Institution Liquidity Monitoring Report (OMB control number 7100-0361), is the Federal Reserve's daily and monthly liquidity data collection for large banking organizations. This page covers what a computation over a reporting row cannot resolve on its own: the report's own appendix inventory, its table structure, its field list, and its confidentiality position, as published in the bundle last updated February 26, 2025. Every claim here links to the Federal Reserve's own FR 2052a reporting-forms index page rather than restating its content.

Report Structure

The table groups a reporting row belongs to

The instructions organize the report into named tables. A reporting row's Table value is one of these, and it is the anchor every appendix requirement is keyed against alongside a row's PID.

Confidentiality

Why no firm data appears anywhere in this suite

The instructions' own Confidentiality heading states that FR 2052a data receives confidential treatment under 12 CFR 261.16, with agency sharing governed by 12 CFR 260.20(g). This estate treats that as an absolute: no firm-supplied FR 2052a row, value, or file may enter this repository, a fixture, a page, or a receipt at any point. Every FR 2052a fixture in this suite is either drawn from the published instructions' own illustrative values or authored as synthetic data.
