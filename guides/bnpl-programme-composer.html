BNPL Programme Composer

The five-stage UK FCA BNPL programme compliance chain (T187 → T190 → T193 → T191 → T192) runs in one page. Assess FCA regulatory readiness, model customer affordability, calculate representative APR, generate the required disclosure templates, and evaluate arrears and collections policies. FCA BNPL regulation comes into force 15 July 2026. Each tool loads in its own frame; the Composer maps stage outputs forward, captures each Policy Mandate, and assembles a composite programme-level mandate with per-stage audit trail. Everything stays in your browser.

5 Stages · Orchestrated T187 · T190 · T193 · T191 · T192 Client-side · Zero PII · Zero network Composite BNPL Programme Policy Mandate export FCA BNPL Regulation — In force 15 Jul 2026
How it works: Work top to bottom. In each stage: ① Send upstream maps the previous stage's mandate into the tool (where a direct mapping exists), ② Run executes the tool's entry function, ③ Capture pulls the stage's Policy Mandate into the Composer. You can also work inside any frame manually — the Composer only reads what the tool has computed.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

Composite BNPL Programme Policy Mandate

When all five stages are captured, export the combined run: a single Policy Mandate whose payload carries the five stage mandates in order, plus a Markdown transcript of the chain. The composite is validated against the Policy Mandate v1.0 required-field set before download.
