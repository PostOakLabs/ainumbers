E-Invoicing & ViDA Digital Reporting Composer

From ViDA DRR readiness to ISO 20022 payment mapping: the four-stage EU e-invoicing compliance chain (T179 → T180 → T174 → T178) runs in one page. Each tool loads in its own frame; the Composer maps stage outputs forward, captures each Policy Mandate, and assembles a composite e-invoicing / DRR compliance mandate with per-stage audit trail. Everything stays in your browser.

4 Stages · Orchestrated T179 · T180 · T174 · T178 Client-side · Zero PII · Zero network Composite E-Invoicing Policy Mandate export EU ViDA adopted Mar 2025 · Phased mandates 2026–2030
How it works: Work top to bottom. In each stage: ① Send upstream maps the previous stage's mandate into the tool (where a direct mapping exists), ② Run executes the tool's entry function, ③ Capture pulls the stage's Policy Mandate into the Composer. You can also work inside any frame manually — the Composer only reads what the tool has computed.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

Composite Policy Mandate

When all four stages are captured, export the combined run: a single Policy Mandate whose payload carries the four stage mandates in order, plus a Markdown transcript of the chain. The composite is validated against the Policy Mandate v1.0 required-field set before download.
