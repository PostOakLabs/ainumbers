Stablecoin Reserve & Examination Hub

The independent reserve-assurance toolkit for stablecoins: check a monthly reserve disclosure against the GENIUS Act statute, recompute reserves from three independently sourced legs, verify a Merkle-sum proof-of-reserves against a declared root, and receipt the result as a dated scorecard. The tools are issuer-neutral: the same examination passes over a GENIUS Act payment stablecoin or a MiCA-issued token. Everything runs in your browser: no account, no data transmission.

Who this hub is for: stablecoin issuers preparing monthly reserve disclosures and attestations, auditors and examiners who need to recompute an issuer's numbers independently, exchanges and custodians publishing proof-of-reserves, and investors or treasury desks running their own reserve checks on a coin they hold. The GENIUS Act & US Stablecoin Regulation Hub covers US stablecoin regulation itself: licensing paths and disclosure obligations. This hub carries the examination tooling any issuer is run through; the two hubs cross-link.

Group A · GENIUS Act reserve disclosure · ART-582 · ART-275
ART-582 ⚓ Flagship
GENIUS §4MET / NOT_MET

GENIUS Act Reserve-Disclosure Conformance Monitor

Checks a monthly payment-stablecoin-issuer reserve disclosure against two statute-derived GENIUS Act §4 requirements: the 1:1 reserve coverage arithmetic, and attestation presence and timeliness against the statutory monthly cadence. Returns a verdict per requirement. Because the federal implementing rulebooks were still at proposal stage as of August 2026, the monitor reports the statute's answer and stays away from final-rule attribution.

ART-275
GENIUS §4Dual-control gate

GENIUS Act Monthly Reserve Disclosure Checker

Lints an extracted monthly reserve disclosure field by field: composition-category eligibility, tenor, custody locations, the dual-control certification gate requiring distinct CEO and CFO identities, registered-examiner presence, a month-over-month diff, and an on-chain supply cross-check against a pasted figure. Where the conformance monitor answers the two statutory headline requirements, this is the recurring field-level filing check.

Group B · Independent proof-of-reserves verification · ART-584 · ART-280
ART-584
Merkle-sumCoverage ratio

Proof-of-Reserves Verifier

Independently recomputes an exchange or custodian's published proof-of-reserves data: a single-leaf Merkle-sum inclusion path, a liability-side Merkle-sum branch aggregation, and the coverage ratio between the two recomputed sums, with an optional cross-check against a caller-declared published reserve figure. Returns a finding per check plus an overall CONSISTENT, INCONSISTENT, or INDETERMINATE determination against the generic Merkle-sum schema.

ART-280
PoR exportsChainlink PoR

Reserve Proof Verifier

Verifies a Merkle-sum proof-of-reserves customer-inclusion proof against a declared root, reading OKX, Binance, Gate, and Kraken export formats or a generic canonical shape, and checks a Chainlink PoR / NAVLink aggregator round for staleness and deviation. The output records what stays outside proof scope: liabilities completeness, off-balance-sheet encumbrances, and the point-in-time nature of the snapshot.

Group C · Multi-source reserve recompute · ART-603
ART-603
3 legsRatio + WAM

Stablecoin Reserve 3-Source Recompute

Recomputes reserve ratio, weighted-average maturity, and per-holding GENIUS eligible-asset match from three independently sourced legs, then reconciles the legs against each other with a skew gate on how far apart their as-of dates are. Leg A is the issuer's own published report with a per-asset-class breakdown; Leg B is the EDGAR N-MFP series-level summary for the government money market fund holding the reserves; Leg C is a declared on-chain supply figure. A missing leg drives every check that depends on it to INDETERMINATE.

    Check the monthly disclosure against the statute

    Present the run as one dated scorecard over the issuer's published attestation, in the shape of Issue #1 and the series methodology, with an execution_hash on every published figure.

Regulatory Coverage

Framework Coverage and Dates

The standards behind the tools above. Where an obligation date and the enforcement or rulemaking window differ, both are stated. Verify current applicability with qualified counsel for your jurisdiction.

GENIUS Act §4

Reserve and disclosure requirements for payment stablecoin issuers: 1:1 backing in permitted assets (cash, insured deposits, short-dated Treasury bills, overnight repo, and government money market funds), a monthly public reserve report, and CEO and CFO certification of that report. Signed 18 July 2025; obligations attach from the earlier of 18 January 2027 or 120 days after the primary federal regulator's final rules. As of October 2026 the federal implementing rulebooks remain at proposal stage, so the conformance monitor reports the statute's answer without final-rule attribution. Primary source: S.1582, 119th Congress.

MiCA Articles 30, 36, 54

Reserve requirements for e-money tokens: reserves at least equal to the outstanding tokens, segregated from the issuer's own assets, invested in high-quality liquid assets, with redemption at par at any time. The stablecoin titles (Titles III and IV) applied to issuers from 30 June 2024, ahead of the general CASP regime. The scorecard series receipts an issuer against these declared terms. Primary source: Regulation (EU) 2023/1114.

SEC Form N-MFP

Money market funds file monthly portfolio disclosures with the SEC; the 3-source recompute reads the series-level summary for the government fund holding an issuer's reserves, rather than any per-security schedule, so the recompute stays at the granularity a non-holder can independently check. Primary source: SEC Form N-MFP.
