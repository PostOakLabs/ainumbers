Customer Risk Rating Engine

The entire AML workflow is calibrated to customer risk — and this is where you establish it. Input your customer population profile (entity types, geographies, product mix, channel distribution) to generate a risk-tier distribution across Low, Medium, High, and Enhanced Due Diligence segments. The output risk tier map directly drives TM rule threshold calibration in Step 2: high-risk segments require tighter velocity and amount thresholds than standard profiles.

↳ Inputs
Customer entity types (individual / corporate / VASP / MSB), geographic distribution, product mix, channel distribution, PEP exposure, adverse media signals, ownership complexity.
→ Outputs to Step 2
Risk tier distribution (Low / Medium / High / EDD%), individual CRR scores, segment risk profiles — feed directly into TM rule threshold calibration.

Transaction Monitoring Rule Builder

Transaction monitoring rules that are not calibrated to your specific customer risk profile generate excessive false positives and create regulatory exposure. This tool takes the risk-tier distribution from Step 1 and lets you design, document, and backtest TM rules with velocity, amount, geography, and counterparty conditions — segment by segment. It outputs a structured rule specification that can be loaded directly into most core TM platforms and is also the primary input for SAR narrative generation in Step 3.

↳ Inputs from Step 1
Risk tier distribution from Step 1, segment profiles, product mix, high-risk geography flags, velocity baselines per segment, PEP / MSB / VASP segment thresholds.
→ Outputs to Step 3
Structured rule specification (rule name, conditions, thresholds, lookback period, alert logic), tuning parameters, estimated false positive rate — rule output feeds SAR narrative generation in Step 3.

SAR Narrative Generator

When a transaction monitoring rule fires, the output is an alert — but regulatory filing requires a structured Suspicious Activity Report (SAR) narrative. This tool generates SAR narratives following FinCEN SAR narrative guidance and the UK POCA 2002 STR format. Input the alert details, transaction history, and customer risk profile to produce a narrative covering the 5 Ws (Who, What, When, Where, Why), typology codes, and disposition recommendation. The narrative output is referenced by the AP2 Mandate Builder in Step 4 to evidence SAR programme completeness.

↳ Inputs from Step 2
Alert details from TM rule firing (rule triggered, conditions met), transaction history (synthetic), customer risk tier from Step 1, counterparty details, typology hypothesis.
→ Outputs to Step 4
Structured SAR narrative (5 Ws, typology code, disposition), FinCEN / STR format output — feeds into AP2 Mandate Builder as SAR programme evidence in Step 4.

AP2 AML Mandate Builder

The final step packages the outputs from Steps 1–3 into a validated AP2 JSON mandate — the machine-readable policy format for agentic pipeline consumption — alongside a human-readable Markdown summary. The mandate captures your AML risk appetite, TM rule set, SAR programme evidence, and five-pillar programme structure (policies, procedures, controls, training, independent testing) mapped to FATF Immediate Outcomes and FinCEN examination criteria. This output is board-presentable and audit-ready.

↳ Inputs from Steps 1–3
Risk tier summary from Step 1, TM rule specification from Step 2, SAR narrative evidence from Step 3, AML programme governance details, jurisdiction and regulatory framework selection.
→ Final Output
AP2 JSON mandate (schema-validated), Markdown AML programme summary, five-pillar programme structure — board-presentable, audit-ready, and consumable by agentic pipelines.
