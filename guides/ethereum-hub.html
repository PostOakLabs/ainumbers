Audience

Who the Ethereum hub serves

Token and vault ops

Teams running tokenized assets who need interface, royalty, vault-share and permit claims recomputed before they rely on them.

Wallet and agent builders

Builders shipping account-abstraction flows who check UserOperation economics, delegated authorization, and proxy layouts without a node.

Settlement and payments desks

Desks bridging payment messages onto tokenized rails who verify calldata encodings, multipliers, and spend evidence signature by signature.
