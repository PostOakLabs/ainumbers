The Ethereum Workflow Hub

Ethereum-standard work in one place: deterministic browser workflows that recompute interface ids, royalties, vault shares, permit bindings, UserOperation math, EIP-712 digests and state proofs without a node and without a key. This page is the home of the Ethereum-standard tool family inside the web3 hub family, and every link resolves in-tree. The payments-first networks in the wider family are covered by their own guides.
