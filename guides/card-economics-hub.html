Audience

Who Uses These Tools

Merchant Finance Teams

Cost baseline, interchange optimization, CFO memo generation. Use T38 to dissect your MDR line-by-line, T44 to verify MCC classification and risk flags, T52 for Level 2/3 savings analysis, and T85 for a board-ready CFO optimization memo.

Acquirers & Processors

Scheme fee modeling, FANF tier analysis, cross-border cost. Use T415 to break down your Visa and Mastercard scheme fee load — FANF tiers, assessments, NABU/APS — often an overlooked 10–18 bps of volume.

Card Issuers

Portfolio P&L, unit economics, scenario comparison. Use T416 to model the full economics of your card portfolio — NII, interchange, rewards liability, credit losses — through to per-account unit economics and ROA.

Card Programme Managers

Co-brand/prepaid breakeven analysis, rewards liability modeling. Use T417 to model programme economics across co-brand, private label, BIN-sponsor, and prepaid structures with breakeven spend per card and sensitivity analysis.

Regulatory Coverage

Key Regulatory Frameworks

Tools in this hub are mapped to obligations and rate schedules under the following frameworks. Verify current applicability with qualified legal counsel.

💳 Visa VDCAP (Apr 2026)

Visa Digital Commerce Authentication Programme rules effective April 2026. T85 includes a VDCAP authentication compliance audit stage with actionable remediation steps.

🇺🇸 Durbin / Reg II

Durbin Amendment (Reg II) debit interchange caps and routing requirements. T38, T52, and T44 surface Durbin eligibility and PIN debit routing opportunities for qualifying merchants.

🇪🇺 EU IFR

EU Interchange Fee Regulation — 0.2% consumer debit cap, 0.3% consumer credit cap. T52 and T416 include EU IFR rate logic for cross-border scenario modeling.

💳 Mastercard 2025–2026

Mastercard US Interchange Rate Schedules effective 2025–2026. Embedded in T38, T52, T85, and T415 for real-rate modeling across all Mastercard product tiers.

💳 Visa 2025–2026

Visa US Interchange Reimbursement Fee schedules effective 2025–2026. Embedded in T38, T52, T85, and T415 covering all Visa product categories including CPS, EIRF, and standard rates.

Quick Start

Get Started in 3 Steps

  1. 1

    Understand your merchant costs

    Start with T38 — ClearCost or T52 — Interchange Fee Optimizer to baseline your current card acceptance cost. Use T44 — MCC Code Explorer to check MCC classification and risk flags that affect your interchange category.

  2. 2

    Optimize and model scheme fees

    Run T85 — Merchant Card Economics Optimizer for a full merchant optimization analysis including VDCAP 2026 compliance. Use T415 — Scheme Fee Analyzer to model your acquirer's scheme fee load — often an overlooked 10–18 bps of volume.

  3. 3

    Model issuer and programme economics

    Use T416 — Issuer Card Portfolio P&L Model to build a portfolio P&L view through to per-account unit economics and ROA. Use T417 — Card Programme Unit Economics Builder to model a card programme from the ground up with breakeven analysis and sensitivity scenarios.
