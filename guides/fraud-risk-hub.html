Ops / Disputes

Engineering

Treasury / CFO

Regulatory Coverage

Key Regulatory Frameworks

Tools in this hub are mapped to obligations under the following frameworks. Verify current applicability with qualified legal counsel.

🇺🇸 FRB SR 2022

Federal Reserve Board guidance on synthetic identity fraud risk management. T321 implements the 7-attribute structural identity scoring framework from SR 2022-18.

🇺🇸 FinCEN

FinCEN FIN-2021-A003 synthetic identity guidance. BSA SAR filing requirements for T80. FinCEN guidance on beneficial ownership and identity verification.

🇬🇧 PSR PS23/3

UK Payment Systems Regulator mandatory reimbursement rules. T322 covers pre-payment APP scam detection. UK FPS Confirmation of Payee v3.0 friction requirements.

🌍 FATF

FATF 40 Recommendations. T05 maps transaction monitoring rules to FATF typologies. T43 sanctions screening aligned to FATF Recommendation 6.

🇺🇸 OFAC / Sanctions

OFAC SDN list logic in T43. UN Consolidated, EU Consolidated, and HMT watchlist patterns. Fuzzy-match confidence scoring across Levenshtein, Jaro-Winkler, and Soundex.

💳 Visa CE 3.0

Visa Compelling Evidence 3.0 chargeback rules in T41. Mastercard dispute resolution rules. Amex and Discover representment evidence requirements.

🇪🇺 PSD2 / PSD3

PSD2/3 SCA requirements. T18 maps A2A compliance checklists across PSD2/3, FCA open banking, and FinCEN/BSA product launch requirements.

⚡ AP2 Agentic

T323 outputs validated Policy Mandate JSON aligned to the Agent-to-Agent Payments Protocol standard. agent_instructions array deployable directly via MCP server.

Quick Start

Get Started in 5 Steps

  1. 1

    Score Synthetic Identity Risk

    Open T321 — Synthetic Identity Fraud Risk Scorer and input the 7 observable identity signals using synthetic data. The composite score and CIP enhancement steps drive your onboarding risk tier.

  2. 2

    Assess APP Scam Risk Before Payment Release

    Use T322 — APP Scam Pre-Payment Risk Assessor to classify scam typology and trigger appropriate friction interventions under PSR PS23/3 before authorising payments.

  3. 3

    Run Sanctions Screening and Chargeback Defence

    Use T43 for in-browser batch sanctions screening — OFAC SDN, UN, and EU consolidated lists — entirely client-side. Use T41 to build Visa CE 3.0 and Mastercard chargeback representment packages.

  4. 4

    Investigate and Report Post-Event Fraud

    Use T80 — Payment Fraud Investigation Decision Lab for end-to-end five-stage investigation workflows with FinCEN-ready SAR narrative export.

  5. 5

    Build and Deploy Your AP2 Fraud Policy Mandate

    Use T323 — AP2 Fraud & Risk Policy Mandate Builder to configure detection thresholds, APP scam controls, loss tolerance budgets, and SAR triggers. Export the agent_instructions array for MCP deployment.
