The Islamic Finance Hub

Islamic finance work in one place: six browser workflows covering murabahah pricing and early settlement, tawarruq sequencing, ijarah leases that end in ownership, sukuk composition, Shariah equity screening, and money-market fund placement limits. Each workflow recomputes the figures a caller declares against the pinned text of the authority it names, and none of them issues a Shariah ruling, a certification, or a legal opinion. It is written for the product, treasury and compliance teams who work with declared structures and want the arithmetic redone rather than summarized.

The six workflows

Every card points at a live page on this site. Each workflow states the authority profile it applied, cites the paragraph its rule came from, and refuses an authority it does not carry instead of falling back to another profile's numbers. The paragraph-level citations live on the workflow pages; this page names the authorities only.

Workflows

Shariah Equity Purification

Runs one company's declared values through four pinned screening methodologies side by side, reports each ratio against the threshold as printed, and computes the dividend purification amount per profile.

Islamic MMF Placement Limits

Checks a money-market fund's declared holdings against the Saudi CMA Investment Funds Regulations Article 56 limits on permitted instrument types, concentration, weighted-average maturity and liquidity.

Open the workflow →

Murabahah Ibra' Settlement

Computes a deferred-price instalment schedule for a murabahah contract, the rebate on early settlement, and the non-delivery case, in integer minor units.

Open the workflow →

Sukuk Tradability Composition

Classifies, per authority and per period, whether sukuk with a mixed asset portfolio are tradable at a negotiated price or fall under sale-of-debt rules, under the SC Malaysia SAC 266th resolutions and the SBP Pakistan adoption of AAOIFI standard 59.

Open the workflow →

Murabahah and Tawarruq Sequence Check

Checks the order of steps and the sequencing rules of a declared murabahah or tawarruq structure against the Bank Negara Malaysia policy documents, one pass or fail verdict per cited paragraph.

Open the workflow →

Ijarah IMBT Rental Schedule

Computes a month-by-month rental schedule for an ijarah lease ending in ownership, with benchmark floor and cap clamps, lessor cost lines, takaful and total-loss branches, and the ownership transfer event after the lease ends.

Primary Texts

Where the rules are written

Every rule a workflow here applies is read from the authority documents named on its page. This section is the map from workflow to authority; the workflow pages carry the paragraph references, the authority profiles, and the values-only input contracts.

The two murabahah workflows read the Bank Negara Malaysia policy documents on murabahah and tawarruq. The deferred-price schedule, the early-settlement rebate and the non-delivery case come from the ibra' provisions, and the sequence checklist walks a declared structure through the contract steps in the order those documents set, keeping the order question separate from the rules question.

Workflows: Murabahah Ibra' Settlement (art-852) · Murabahah and Tawarruq Sequence Check (art-855)

The ijarah workflow runs on its Bank Negara Malaysia profile: rental agreed at inception, benchmark rents clamped to a declared floor and cap, lessor-borne cost lines, takaful, total-loss treatment with and without lessee fault, and the ownership transfer rendered as a separate event after the lease ends.

Workflow: Ijarah IMBT Rental Schedule (art-856)

The sukuk workflow reads the SC Malaysia SAC 266th resolutions on asset composition alongside the State Bank of Pakistan's adoption of AAOIFI standard 59, and reports the two authorities separately, because the same portfolio can answer differently under each.

Workflow: Sukuk Tradability Composition (art-854)

The fund placement workflow reads Article 56 of the Saudi CMA Investment Funds Regulations, and the screening workflow reads four pinned index methodologies as printed, keeping each methodology's thresholds, denominators and purification arithmetic separate.

Workflows: Islamic MMF Placement Limits (art-851) · Shariah Equity Purification (art-850)

Audience

Who the Islamic finance hub serves

Product and treasury teams

Teams pricing declared murabahah and ijarah structures who need the instalment schedule, the early-settlement rebate and the placement-limit check recomputed on their own contract terms.

Compliance readers

Readers verifying screening outcomes ratio by ratio, with each methodology's threshold, denominator and purification amount shown as that methodology prints them.

Structuring reviewers

Reviewers walking a declared murabahah or tawarruq sequence step by step, one verdict per cited paragraph, with the first failing step named.
