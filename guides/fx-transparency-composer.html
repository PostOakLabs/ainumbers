FX Transparency Composer

From FX spread deconstruction to optimised corridor routing in one page. Stage 1 decomposes the all-in FX margin and cost (T209); Stage 2 models payment failure probability on the corridor (T210); Stage 3 ranks alternatives by total cost (T216); Stage 4 optimises the routing recommendation (T95). Each stage's outputs map into the next, with a composite FX Policy Mandate export at the end.

4 Stages · Orchestrated T209 · T210 · T216 · T95 Client-side · Zero PII · Zero network Composite FX Policy Mandate export
How it works: Work top to bottom. In each stage: ① Send upstream maps the previous stage's mandate into the tool (where a direct mapping exists), ② Run executes the tool's entry function, ③ Capture pulls the stage's Policy Mandate into the Composer. You can also work inside any frame manually — the Composer only reads what the tool has computed.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

Composite Policy Mandate

When all four stages are captured, export the combined run: a single FX Policy Mandate whose payload carries the four stage mandates in order, plus a Markdown transcript of the chain. The composite is validated against the Policy Mandate v1.0 required-field set before download.
