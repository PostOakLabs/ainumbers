DORA Readiness Composer

From ICT risk gap to NCA submission in one page: the four-stage DORA chain (T300 → T304 → T307 → T310) runs end-to-end. Each tool loads below in its own frame; the Composer maps each stage's outputs into the next, captures each stage's Policy Mandate, and assembles a composite DORA mandate with a per-stage audit trail. Everything stays in your browser.

4 Stages · Orchestrated T300 · T304 · T307 · T310 Client-side · Zero PII · Zero network Composite DORA Policy Mandate export
How it works: Work top to bottom. In each stage: ① Send upstream maps the previous stage's mandate into the tool (where a direct mapping exists), ② Run executes the tool's entry function, ③ Capture pulls the stage's Policy Mandate into the Composer — no per-stage export click needed (the bridge builds it silently; the tool's own Export Policy Mandate button remains available if you want individual stage audit files). You can also work inside any frame manually — the Composer only reads what the tool has computed.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

Composite Policy Mandate

When all four stages are captured, export the combined run: a single Policy Mandate whose payload carries the four stage mandates in order, plus a Markdown transcript of the chain. The composite is validated against the Policy Mandate v1.0 required-field set before download.
