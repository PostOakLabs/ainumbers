Quick-Start Guides
Step 1. Open T324. Select your rail — FedNow, RTP, or SEPA Instant. These are the three prefunding-required real-time rails. For BCBS 248 intraday credit facility sizing on Fedwire, CHAPS, or FPS (credit-based rails), use T258 in the Cat-21 hub.
Step 2. Enter your daily outgoing volume, opening balance, and timing profile (morning-heavy, afternoon-heavy, even, or custom 24-hour breakdown).
Step 3. Enter your opportunity cost rate (e.g. 5.25% for Fed Funds) and top-up alert threshold percentage.
Step 4. Click Build. The 24-row hourly schedule shows exactly when top-ups are needed. The rail comparison panel shows the idle capital cost for the same inputs across all three prefunding rails — helping treasury choose the lowest-cost operational rail.
Step 5. Export the Policy Mandate JSON. The mandate encodes the prefunding schedule in payload.prefunding_schedule — import it directly into T326 to pre-populate your ops policy mandate.
Step 1. Open T325. Select today's date (or any future date) and your payment corridor — US Domestic, EU Domestic, UK Domestic, cross-border, or a regional corridor.
Step 2. Click Look Up. The tool shows cutoff times for all rails relevant to your corridor, with UTC equivalents, DST flag, and bank holiday closures.
Step 3. If today's cutoff has already passed for a rail, the tool shows the next available window. For weekends and holidays, it calculates the next business day.
Step 4. The ±7-day calendar strip shows upcoming holiday impacts at a glance — useful for pre-planning batch settlement around long weekends.
Step 5. For corridor-level rail selection, FX volatility, and regulatory risk across 30+ emerging markets, use T21. T325 gives you cutoff precision; T21 gives you corridor strategy.
Step 1. Open T78 Smart Routing Advisor. Configure your 7 decision dimensions — speed requirement, cost ceiling, finality need, geographic coverage, batch vs real-time, settlement currency, and regulatory jurisdiction.
Step 2. The tool scores ACH, FedNow, RTP, SWIFT gpi, SEPA CT, SEPA Instant, Fedwire, CHAPS, UK FPS, and BACS against your requirements.
Step 3. Review the ranked output — the top-ranked rail is your recommended primary route. The score breakdown shows which dimensions each rail excels or falls short on.
Step 4. Export the AP2 routing policy for governance documentation and agent deployment.
Step 1. Open T40. Select jurisdiction — OCC 36-hour notification window, GDPR 72-hour, or DORA 4-hour. If you operate under multiple regimes, choose the most restrictive (shortest) window.
Step 2. Select severity level (P1–P4) and configure your role matrix — who is notified at each stage, with what authority.
Step 3. The tool generates a complete runbook with countdown timers, communication templates for internal and external stakeholders, and regulatory notification checklist.
Step 4. Run the tabletop exercise scenario generator — the tool produces 3 scenarios appropriate to your severity level for team training.
Step 5. Export Policy Mandate for your incident management governance record. The runbook URL can be referenced in T326's incident policy dimension.
Step 1. Run T324 first and export the Policy Mandate JSON. Open T326 and paste that export into the Dimension 1 import field — opening balance and rail are auto-populated from payload.prefunding_schedule.
Step 2. Configure Dimension 2 routing policy — primary rail for each corridor (US, EU, UK, cross-border) and a fallback rail. The validator flags any corridor where primary and fallback are the same.
Step 3. Configure Dimension 3 SLA targets — real-time payment processing time (ms slider), batch SLA (hours), breach escalation threshold, and KPI alert frequency.
Step 4. Configure Dimension 4 incident policy — select your default incident classification threshold (P1–P4), paste your runbook reference URL, and enter the escalation contact role (title only, not stored).
Step 5. Click Build. The agent_instructions array contains your complete ops monitoring rulebook — deployable to your operations agent via the AINumbers.co MCP server.

About Cat-04 · Payment Ops & Monitoring

Eleven tools covering the full payment operations lifecycle

Cat-04 covers the operational infrastructure layer of payment systems. The eleven tools span real-time rail prefunding scheduling for entities operating on FedNow, RTP, and SEPA Instant with idle capital cost calculation and rail comparison (BCBS 248 facility sizing for credit-based rails is in Cat-21 T258); a 16-rail global payment cutoff and settlement window atlas with DST and bank holiday awareness; payment journey mapping with failure point overlays; smart rail selection and scoring across 10 global rails; A2A real-time migration planning; real-time ops dashboard simulation; incident response runbook generation with OCC/GDPR/DORA countdown timers; payment decline code lookup; emerging market corridor reference; pay-by-bank checkout prototyping; and a Policy Mandate agentic ops policy mandate builder that imports T324 prefunding output directly. Cross-links to Cat-03 (Fraud & Risk) and Cat-21 (Real-Time Payments Operations) complete the operational stack.

Machine-readable manifest files are co-located with each tool HTML file. The MCP server scans these manifests to expose tools via tools/list and route calls via tools/call. 6 of 11 Cat-04 tools have ap2_export: true. T08, T13, T14, T21, T22 are reference/operational tools without Policy Mandate.
