Protocol Context

What the primary texts state

Context lines on this page come from the pinned texts below, paraphrased rather than quoted. The page makes no claim about what any wallet, node, or exchange accepts at a given time, and nothing on it is financial advice.

The shielded pools carry transaction details in zero-knowledge proofs rather than in the clear. Sprout and Sapling use the Groth16 proving system, whose circuit parameters are a structured reference string with hidden structure: a holder of that structure could create fake proofs and counterfeit funds. The parameters are generated by multiparty computation, where the hidden structure stays unrecoverable as long as at least one participant was not compromised; the ZIP's own rationale records that generating these parameters remains a point of risk in the protocol, and that an earlier proving system's flaw was resolved only by changing systems and rerunning the ceremony.

Source: ZIP 224, Motivation

Orchard, the newer shielded pool, changes both the curves and the proving system: the Pallas and Vesta cycle replaces BLS12-381 and its embedded curve Jubjub (Pallas carries the application, Vesta the circuit), and the Halo 2 proving system with PLONKish arithmetization replaces Groth16 and R1CS. The stated motivation is a proving system that supports recursion and needs no structured reference string.

Source: ZIP 224, Curves and Proving system

The chain's value pools (transparent, Sprout, Sapling, Orchard, and the lockbox) are tracked as explicit balances, and the specification makes a negative pool balance an invalid-block condition. That is the arithmetic the planned supply surface recomputes: published pool balances pasted by the reader, checked for internal consistency, with no chain query of any kind.

Source: Zcash Protocol Specification, Chain Value Pool Balances
