Regulatory Change Programme

EU 2025–2027 Regulatory Triage Journey

Run this nine-step triage sequence to assess your organisation's exposure and readiness across the full EU regulatory pipeline in priority order of in-force dates.

  1. 1

    Assess PSD3 SCA transition (immediate priority)

    Start with T414 to compare your PSD2 SCA implementation against PSD3 requirements — SCA changes affect all PSPs from go-live.

    T414 PSD3 SCA Checker
  2. 2

    Validate EU IPR ISO 20022 address compliance

    Run T342 to validate structured address fields in your ISO 20022 messages against EU Instant Payments Regulation requirements.

    T342 EU IPR Address Validator
  3. 3

    Build SEPA IPR annual report

    Use T349 to generate a structured SEPA IPR annual compliance report — required for all PSPs subject to EU IPR 2024/886.

    T349 SEPA IPR Report Builder
  4. 4

    Score eIDAS2 EUDI wallet readiness

    Use T348 to score your readiness as a relying party for EUDI Wallet credential acceptance — obligation applies from 2024/1183 implementation dates.

    T348 eIDAS2 Wallet Readiness
  5. 5

    Map EU Data Act obligations (Sep 2025)

    Run T413 to map EU Data Act 2023/2854 obligations across your financial services data sharing and cloud switching arrangements before September 2025 application date.

    T413 EU Data Act Scope Mapper
  6. 6

    Assess MiFID III/IFR impact

    If you operate as an investment firm, use T412 to calculate IFR K-factor capital requirements and assess MiFID III reform impact by firm class.

    T412 MiFID III/IFR Assessor
  7. 7

    Analyze AMLA 2027 readiness gaps

    Use T350 to gap your AML programme against AMLA (EU) 2024/1620 requirements effective 2027 — early remediation is essential given scope of harmonisation.

    T350 AMLA 2027 Gap Analyzer
  8. 8

    Build CRD6 prudential transition plan

    Banks should use T352 to build a structured CRD6/CRR3 transition plan for January 2026 application — Basel IV output floor and IRB changes require early modelling.

    T352 CRD6 Transition Plan Builder
  9. 9

    Run PSD3/PSR2 full transition impact

    Complete the cycle with T343 to assess the full PSD3/PSR2 transition impact across all PSP categories, licensing, SCA, and open banking access obligations.

    T343 PSD3/PSR2 Impact Assessor

Regulatory Coverage

EU 2025–2027 Regulatory Pipeline

Tools in this hub cover the following EU regulations and directives. Verify in-force dates and national transposition timelines with qualified legal counsel. Proposed instruments remain subject to amendment.

Payments & Banking

EU IPR 2024/886 (instant payments), PSD3 COM/2023/366, PSR2, CRD6 2024/1623 (Jan 2026), and SEPA IPR annual reporting obligations under ECB oversight.

Identity & Data

eIDAS2 (EU) 2024/1183 — EUDI Wallet relying party obligations. EU Data Act 2023/2854 — applicable September 2025. Connected product data, cloud switching, and B2B data sharing.

AML & Financial Crime

AMLA Regulation (EU) 2024/1620 — new Anti-Money Laundering Authority with direct supervision from 2027. 6AMLD harmonised CDD standards, UBO registers, and high-risk third country obligations.

Investment Firms

IFR/IFD 2019/2033 — K-factor capital requirements by firm class. MiFID III reform proposals covering investor protection, research unbundling, and algorithmic trading. Best execution under MiFIR 2.0.

Quick Start

Get Started in 4 Steps

  1. 1

    Triage PSD3 and EU IPR payments obligations

    Start with T414 PSD3 SCA Transition Checker and T342 EU IPR Address Validator to assess payments regulatory exposure — these are the most time-sensitive obligations for PSPs.

  2. 2

    Map data and identity obligations

    Run T413 EU Data Act Scope Mapper before September 2025 application. Score EUDI Wallet readiness with T348 eIDAS2 Readiness Scorer.

  3. 3

    Assess prudential and AML gaps

    Banks use T352 CRD6 Transition Plan Builder for January 2026 Basel IV readiness. All regulated firms run T350 AMLA 2027 Gap Analyzer for 2027 AMLA preparation.

  4. 4

    Complete investment firm and full PSD3 assessment

    Investment firms use T412 MiFID III/IFR Assessor for K-factor capital requirements. Complete the programme with T343 PSD3/PSR2 Impact Assessor for a holistic PSP transition view.
