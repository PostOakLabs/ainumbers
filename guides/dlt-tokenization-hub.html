Regulatory Landscape
EU · EBA / ESMA
MiCA — Markets in Crypto-Assets
The EU's comprehensive crypto-asset regulatory framework, in force from 2024, covering issuers of asset-referenced tokens and e-money tokens, CASPs, and stablecoin reserve requirements. Directly relevant to stablecoin reserve modeling and tokenized asset compliance tools in this hub.
In Force
United States · US Senate
GENIUS Act
The Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for US Stablecoins Act, establishing a federal framework for payment stablecoin issuance. Defines reserve composition, redemption rights, and issuer capital requirements relevant to stablecoin reserve stress-testing tooling.
In Progress
Global · BIS
BIS Unified Ledger
The Bank for International Settlements' concept for shared, programmable financial infrastructure connecting tokenized commercial bank money, wholesale CBDC, and tokenized assets on a single platform. Foundational reference for CBDC interoperability modeling in this hub.
Active
Saudi Arabia / UAE · SAMA / CBUAE
Gulf Virtual Asset Frameworks
SAMA and CBUAE have each established virtual asset regulatory frameworks aligned with FATF guidelines, covering licensing for digital asset service providers, stablecoin issuance, and live CBDC pilot programmes across both jurisdictions.
Active
Global · FATF
FATF Travel Rule (Rec. 16)
The Financial Action Task Force's requirement that originator and beneficiary information accompany virtual asset transfers above the threshold. Relevant to cross-chain settlement finality auditing and the Tokenized Asset Compliance Pre-Checker tools in this hub.
In Force
EU · European Commission
EU Instant Payments Regulation
Mandates that EU payment service providers offer euro instant payments at parity pricing from 2025. Intersects with DLT settlement finality tooling for tokenized payment systems where euro settlement rails underpin on-chain DvP workflows.
In Force

Quick-Start Guide

Deploy a permissioned DLT network in five steps

A practical workflow for DLT architects and CBDC programme managers onboarding to a new enterprise deployment.

01
Select your platform and privacy model. Open Tool 68 — Enterprise DLT Platform Comparator and configure your use case (payment settlement, tokenized asset, CBDC). The comparator scores Corda, Fabric, Besu, and Canton across throughput, privacy model, finality type, and regulatory posture. Then open Tool 69 — DLT Privacy Model Selector to confirm the privacy architecture (Fabric PDC vs. channels, Corda confidential transactions, Canton sub-transaction privacy, Besu Tessera).
02
Design your node topology. Open Tool 70 — Node Role & Trust Boundary Modeler and configure node count, consensus type, and geographic regions. The tool renders an SVG topology diagram, runs a quorum health check, and flags single points of failure before you provision infrastructure.
03
Validate smart contracts and chain configuration. Open Tool 54 — Smart Contract Parameter Validator to check your contract parameters against platform constraints. Open Tool 56 — Permissioned Chain Config Auditor to validate your configtx.yaml (Fabric), network-parameters (Corda), or genesis.json (Besu) against production best practices.
04
Model settlement finality and DvP reconciliation. Open Tool 65 — Cross-Rail Settlement Finality Auditor to compare finality guarantees across your target settlement rails. If your deployment includes tokenized asset settlement, open Tool 55 — DvP Reconciliation Engine to model delivery-versus-payment matching and detect reconciliation gaps before go-live.
05
Stress-test economics and generate governance output. Open Tool 75 — Stablecoin Reserve & Peg Health Auditor if your deployment involves stablecoin settlement or reserve management. Then open Tool 73 — Enterprise DLT Decision Lab to synthesize all prior decisions into a structured Architecture Decision Record and board-ready governance memo.
