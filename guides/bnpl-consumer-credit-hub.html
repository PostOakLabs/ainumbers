Regulatory Timeline

Key BNPL regulatory effective dates and compliance deadlines. Urgency reflects time remaining as at May 2026.

Live · June 2023
NY DFS BNPL Supervisory Guidance
New York Department of Financial Services issued BNPL supervisory guidance covering consumer disclosures, credit reporting, data privacy, and complaint mechanisms for BNPL providers operating in New York State.
NY DFS · Jun 2023
Live · May 2024
CFPB BNPL Interpretive Rule — Closed-End Credit
CFPB issued interpretive rule classifying 4-instalment BNPL as closed-end credit cards subject to TILA Reg Z §1026.18 disclosures, dispute rights, and periodic statement requirements.
CFPB · Reg Z · May 2024
Transposition deadline: 20 Nov 2025
EU Consumer Credit Directive 2 (CCD2)
Directive 2023/2225/EU explicitly brings BNPL into scope of consumer credit regulation. SECCI disclosure, creditworthiness assessment (Art. 18), forbearance (Art. 35), and proportionate default charges (Art. 33) all apply. Member states transpose at varying pace.
EU CCD2 · 2023/2225 · SECCI
⚡ 15 July 2026 — 2 months
FCA Deferred Payment Credit (DPC) Regime — Live
Financial Services and Markets Act 2023, Schedule 9 removes the BNPL exemption from CCA 1974 s.60F(2). BNPL providers and merchants need FCA authorisation, CONC-compliant affordability assessments, PCCI disclosures, FOS access, and Consumer Duty compliance.
FCA DPC · FSMA 2023 Sch.9 · CONC
Ongoing · 2026–2027
FCA Consumer Duty — BNPL Distribution Chain
From July 2026, merchants in the BNPL distribution chain have product governance obligations under Consumer Duty (PS22/9). Fair value assessment, consumer understanding, and complaint outcome monitoring are required from distribution chain participants.
FCA Consumer Duty · PS22/9
Expected 2026–2028
US State-Level BNPL Regulation (Emerging)
Multiple US states (including California, Illinois) are developing specific BNPL licensing and disclosure requirements beyond the CFPB federal floor. State money transmission licences may apply to BNPL issuers depending on product structure.
US State BNPL · CA · IL · Emerging
Key Concepts

Core terminology and regulatory frameworks for global BNPL and consumer credit compliance.

💳
Deferred Payment Credit (DPC)
The UK FCA's term for regulated BNPL products from 15 July 2026. DPC covers short-term, interest-free instalment credit previously exempt under CCA 1974 s.60F(2). From this date, lenders need FCA authorisation, CONC rules apply, and CCA consumer protections (right of withdrawal, FOS access) attach.
📋
SECCI / PCCI Disclosures
The Standard European Consumer Credit Information (SECCI) form is mandatory pre-contract disclosure under both UK CONC 4.2 (as PCCI) and EU CCD2 Art. 10 / Annex II. Must include: creditor identity, type of credit, total amount, duration, APR with representative example, total repayable, payment schedule, right of withdrawal, and complaints pathway.
⚖️
Affordability vs Creditworthiness
UK (FCA CONC 5.2A): creditworthiness assessment — assesses both credit risk to lender AND ability to repay without undue difficulty. EU (CCD2 Art. 18): creditworthiness assessment — consumer-protection focused; mandatory refusal if cannot repay. US (CFPB): ability-to-repay standard under UDAAP — reasonable basis to believe consumer can repay.
🔢
APR — Actuarial vs Nominal
CCD2 Art. 31 uses the actuarial (effective annual) rate: solves for i in the present-value equation Σcf/(1+i)^t = 0. Reg Z Appendix J uses a nominal annual rate: unit-period rate × periods per year. These differ for non-monthly products. Both must show 0% for zero-interest BNPL, but fees change this.
🌬️
Breathing Space (Debt Respite)
UK Debt Respite Scheme (Debt Respite Regulations 2020). Standard Breathing Space: 60 days; all enforcement, interest, and charges frozen on notification. Mental Health Crisis Breathing Space: lasts as long as treatment + 30 days. BNPL lenders must identify and immediately pause collections on notification by the Insolvency Service.
🏢
B2B Trade Credit Exemptions
B2B deferred payment is generally exempt from consumer credit rules: UK CCA 1974 s.16B (business lending exemption for ≥25 employees or turnover ≥£1m). EU CCD2 Art. 2(2) — business-purpose exclusion. US Reg Z § 1026.3(a) — business-purpose exemption. T194 models B2B underwriting within these exemptions.
📞
Reg F — Debt Collection Limits
CFPB Regulation F (12 CFR Part 1006, effective Nov 2021) implementing FDCPA. Key rules: 7-call cap per week per debt (§ 1006.14(b)); validation notice within 5 days (§ 1006.34); cease communication honoured (FDCPA § 805(c)); electronic opt-out (§ 1006.6). First-party BNPL collectors may not be "debt collectors" but UDAAP still applies.
🤖
AP2 — Agent-Callable Compliance Mandates
Machine-readable JSON mandate format for AI agent runtimes. T187 and T192 output compliance_control mandates with obligation IDs, agent instructions, and regulatory citations. T191 outputs disclosure_template mandates routing generated disclosures to legal review queues. Consumed directly by MCP-compatible agent orchestration systems.
