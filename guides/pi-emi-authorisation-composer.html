PI/EMI Authorisation Composer

The five-stage UK/EU Payment Institution and E-Money Institution authorisation chain (T404 → T405 → T418 → T269 → T406) runs in one page. Check FCA/EBA authorisation readiness, calculate EMI capital requirements, determine own funds adequacy, assess customer fund safeguarding, and model PSR APP reimbursement liability. Each tool loads in its own frame; the Composer maps stage outputs forward, captures each Policy Mandate, and assembles a composite authorisation mandate with per-stage audit trail. Everything stays in your browser.

5 Stages · Orchestrated T404 · T405 · T418 · T269 · T406 Client-side · Zero PII · Zero network Composite PI/EMI Authorisation Policy Mandate export PSD2 · EMD2 · PSRs 2017 · FCA Perimeter
How it works: Work top to bottom. In each stage: ① Send upstream maps the previous stage's mandate into the tool (where a direct mapping exists), ② Run executes the tool's entry function, ③ Capture pulls the stage's Policy Mandate into the Composer. You can also work inside any frame manually — the Composer only reads what the tool has computed.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

Composite PI/EMI Authorisation Policy Mandate

When all five stages are captured, export the combined run: a single Policy Mandate whose payload carries the five stage mandates in order, plus a Markdown transcript of the chain. The composite is validated against the Policy Mandate v1.0 required-field set before download.
