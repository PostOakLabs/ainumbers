Regulatory Coverage

Regulatory Frameworks Covered

Tools in this hub are mapped to the following US regulatory frameworks. Verify current applicability with qualified legal counsel before relying on outputs for licensing or compliance decisions.

🇺🇸 GENIUS Act 2025

Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for US Stablecoins Act. §2 payment stablecoin definition, §3 permitted issuers, §4 reserve requirements (T-bills/repo/deposits), §8 disclosure/attestation, §10 consumer protections, §11 AML, §15 foreign issuer provisions.

🏦 OCC Guidance

OCC Interpretive Letter 1174 (stablecoin activities). National trust charter framework (12 C.F.R. Part 5). Capital adequacy standards. Permissible crypto-asset activities for national banks and federal savings associations.

🏛 Federal Reserve

Fed Regulation II stablecoin guidance. Federally qualified nonbank oversight under GENIUS Act §6. Payment system risk policy. Systemic importance designation under Dodd-Frank §804. Cross-border stablecoin flows.

⚖ BSA / AML / OFAC

Bank Secrecy Act (31 U.S.C. §5318): SAR/CTR filing, CDD/EDD, AML programme. GENIUS Act §11 AML requirements. FinCEN MSB registration for nonbank issuers. OFAC SDN and CAPTA screening for stablecoin redemptions.

Quick Start

Get Started in 4 Steps

  1. 1

    Classify and Check Compliance

    Open T336 Issuer Classification Mapper to identify your GENIUS Act issuer category, then run T386 Compliance Checker to test your stablecoin design against every §2 definition element and identify which licensing paths are available to you.

  2. 2

    Select Your Licensing Path

    Use T387 Licensing Path Selector to get a recommended path (OCC trust / state / federal nonbank), timeline estimate, capital requirements, and full obligations checklist. Export the Policy Mandate JSON for your licensing project plan.

  3. 3

    Stress Test Your Reserve Structure

    Before finalising your reserve composition, run T388 Reserve Stress Test Modeller across four scenarios. Confirm your structure passes the §4 30-day run scenario and produces a coverage ratio ≥1.0× under severe stress.

  4. 4

    Build Disclosure Templates & AML Programme

    Use T389 Disclosure Builder to generate §8 reserve reports and public disclosures. Use T337 Reserve Attestation Checklist for monthly filing preparation, and T338 AML Scope Builder to document your BSA/AML programme obligations.
