CCD2 Consumer Credit Composer

Orchestrated four-stage CCD2 compliance workflow: scope classification (T481) → Art. 18 creditworthiness assessment (T482) → SECCI Annex II pre-contractual disclosure (T483) → readiness scoring (T484). Each stage runs in its own frame; the Composer maps outputs forward and assembles a composite CCD2 Policy Mandate. Applies from 20 Nov 2026 — EU regime (distinct from UK FCA BNPL).

CCD2 · 4 Stages T481 · T482 · T483 · T484 Client-side · Zero PII · Zero network Composite CCD2 Policy Mandate export
How it works: Work top to bottom. In each stage: ① Send upstream maps the previous stage's mandate fields into the tool, ② Run executes the tool's entry function inside the frame, ③ Capture pulls the stage's Policy Mandate into the Composer. You can also work inside any frame manually — the Composer only reads what the tool has computed. All four stages must be captured before the composite export unlocks.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

Composite CCD2 Policy Mandate

When all four stages are captured, export the complete CCD2 run: a single Policy Mandate whose payload carries the four stage mandates in order, plus a Markdown transcript. Validated against the Policy Mandate v1.0 required-field set before download. Use for audit trail, agentic pipeline integration, or regulatory evidence.
