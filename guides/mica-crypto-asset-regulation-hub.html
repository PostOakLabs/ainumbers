MiCA ARF/EMT White Paper Builder

ThresholdMiCA

MiCA Significant Token Threshold Calculator

Test whether an ARF or EMT meets the "significant" classification thresholds under MiCA Art. 43 (ARF) and Art. 56 (EMT). Inputs: token holders, daily transaction volume, reserve size, cross-border reach, and systemic importance indicators. Determines significant/non-significant status and enhanced supervisory obligations applicable under EBA/ESMA RTS. Covers MiCA Art. 43, 56 and EBA RTS 2024.

Regulatory Coverage

MiCA Regulatory Framework

Tools in this hub are mapped to the following regulatory frameworks. Verify current applicability with qualified legal counsel.

MiCA 2023/1114

EU Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation. Titles III (ARF), IV (EMT) applicable from Jun 2024. Title V (CASP) from Dec 2024. Full application across all EU member states with single EU passport.

EBA / ESMA RTS

EBA regulatory technical standards for EMT reserve assets, own funds requirements, recovery plans, redemption rights, and significant token obligations. ESMA RTS for CASP organisational requirements, conflicts of interest, and complaint handling.

MAR 596/2014

Market Abuse Regulation: principles applied to crypto-assets under MiCA Art. 87–92. Insider dealing, market manipulation, and market soundings framework extended to crypto-asset markets.

FATF Travel Rule

FATF Recommendation 16: Transfer of Funds Regulation (TFR) 2023/1113 applies Travel Rule to crypto-asset transfers from 30 Dec 2024. Originator and beneficiary information requirements for CASPs.

TFR 2023/1113

EU Transfer of Funds Regulation: applies to crypto-asset transfers alongside MiCA. CASPs must collect, verify, and transmit originator/beneficiary data for all crypto-asset transfers regardless of amount.
