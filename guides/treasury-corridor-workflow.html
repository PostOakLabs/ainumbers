Cross-Border FX Netting Simulator

Before hedging or optimising a corridor, you need to know your actual net exposure — which means netting bilateral FX flows across all counterparty legs. This simulator takes your gross bilateral payment flows (currency pairs, notional amounts, settlement dates) and computes the multilateral net position that would result from netting, quantifying the capital efficiency and spread savings versus a gross settlement approach. In multi-currency corridors, netting can reduce gross FX turnover by 40–70% before a single hedge is placed.

↳ Inputs
Bilateral FX payment flows (currency pairs, notional, settlement dates), counterparty list, netting agreement eligibility, settlement cycle (T+0, T+1, T+2).
→ Outputs to Step 2
Net residual exposure per currency pair after netting, netting efficiency ratio, gross-to-net reduction — the residual exposure is the input to hedge optimisation in Step 2.

Hedge Strategy All-in Cost

FX Hedge Optimizer & All-in Cost Simulator

Once the net residual exposure from Step 1 is established, this tool optimises the hedge strategy for that residual position. It models forwards, NDFs, options, and swap overlays across your corridor currency pairs, computing the all-in cost per unit for each hedging instrument (bid-ask, forward points, option premium, rollover cost). The goal is to identify the minimum-cost hedge that meets your treasury policy's risk tolerance — the all-in cost figure then feeds directly into corridor economics in Step 3.

↳ Inputs from Step 1
Net residual exposure per pair from Step 1, hedge tenor (30 / 60 / 90 day), instrument preference (forward / NDF / option / swap), risk tolerance (hedge ratio), corridor currency pairs.
→ Outputs to Step 3
Recommended hedge instrument mix, all-in cost per unit (bps), cost breakdown (spread + forward points + premium + rollover) — feeds into full corridor economics in Step 3.

Cross-Border Corridor Savings Calculator

The final step closes the economics loop. This tool takes the netting savings from Step 1 and the hedged all-in FX cost from Step 2, then models full corridor economics versus baseline (correspondent banking / SWIFT MT). It quantifies total savings from netting efficiency, hedge cost reduction versus spot, nostro optimisation, and settlement finality improvement — producing a single corridor economics report suitable for treasury committee presentation or RFP benchmarking.

↳ Inputs from Steps 1–2
Netting savings and efficiency ratio from Step 1, all-in hedge cost per unit from Step 2, corridor volume (monthly notional), current correspondent banking cost baseline, settlement days.
→ Final Output
Total corridor savings (bps and USD equivalent), savings attribution (netting / hedge / nostro / finality), cost versus correspondent baseline — treasury committee-ready economics report.
