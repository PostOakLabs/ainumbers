Quick Start

Get Started in 6 Steps

  1. 1

    Prepare for FedNow Go-Live

    Start with T255 — FedNow Participation Readiness Scorer to score your institution against all 47 Federal Reserve requirements. The gap checklist becomes your implementation backlog. Then run T258 to size the intraday credit facility your FedNow volume will require under BCBS 248.

  2. 2

    Build a Multi-Rail ISO 20022 Integration

    Use T254 — ISO 20022 Cross-Rail Compatibility Checker to identify exactly which fields behave differently between your source and target rails. The severity-ranked diff report shows HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW issues — prioritise HIGH before deployment.

  3. 3

    Configure RTP Fraud Controls

    Open T256 — RTP Fraud Velocity Rule Builder and select your rail and latency budget (start with 500ms). Add velocity rules one by one — the latency waterfall updates live. The conflict detection catches duplicate BLOCK rules and unreachable MONITOR conditions before you push to production.

  4. 4

    Process a UK PSR APP Fraud Claim

    Open T257 — PSR APP Fraud Reimbursement Workflow Builder and complete the 4-step claim assessment. The decision engine applies the £85,000 cap, calculates the 50/50 PSP split, assesses gross negligence across 6 evidenced indicators, and generates a dated 5-business-day timeline.

  5. 5

    Size Your Intraday Liquidity Facility

    Open T258 — Intraday Credit Facility Sizing Model and enter your peak net debit position, average daily volume, and current LCR position. Select your collateral type — the haircut auto-populates from the Basel III table. The output shows your minimum buffer, recommended facility (buffer + 20% headroom), LCR impact, and all seven BCBS 248 ILM indicators.

  6. 6

    Compile a Policy Mandate for Your Payment Agent

    Use T259 — AP2 Real-Time Payments Policy Builder to configure all five policy dimensions. The live JSON preview updates as you configure each section. The built-in validator catches threshold conflicts before you export. Download the Policy Mandate JSON and reference the agent_instructions array as your agent's ordered rulebook.
