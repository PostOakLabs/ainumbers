Customer Onboarding Composer

Risk-adaptive onboarding: Stage 1 scores the customer (T110); Stage 2 branches on the captured risk tier — HIGH loads the Enhanced Due Diligence checklist (T109), MEDIUM or LOW loads the TM Rule Builder (T116); Stage 3 anchors everything in an AP2 AML Policy Mandate (T131). The branch decision is recorded in the composite mandate for deterministic audit replay.

3 Stages · Branching T110 → T109 / T116 → T131 Client-side · Zero PII · Zero network Branch decision recorded in composite
How it works: Work top to bottom. Stage 1 must be captured before Stage 2 can load — the risk tier from Stage 1 determines which tool Stage 2 shows. Once the branch resolves, Stage 2 operates identically to a linear composer stage. Capture Stage 3 last to unlock the composite export.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

Composite Policy Mandate

When all three stages are captured, export the combined run: a single Policy Mandate whose payload carries the three stage mandates in order, the branch decision record, and a Markdown transcript of the chain.
