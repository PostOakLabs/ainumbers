Compliance Journey

PSP Lifecycle Compliance Path

Use these tools in sequence to build a complete PSP compliance posture: from authorisation through ongoing safeguarding and fraud reimbursement obligations.

  • 1

    Authorisation Readiness

    Before applying for FCA or EU NCA authorisation, use T404 to score your application readiness, identify capital gaps, and map all PSD2/PSR 2017 article requirements. For EMIs, calculate exact own-funds obligation with T405.

    T404 PI/EMI Auth Readiness T405 EMI Capital Calculator
  • 2

    Safeguarding & Client Funds

    Establish and document your safeguarding arrangements. Use T269 to assess compliance with PSRs 2017 Reg 23: confirm segregated account setup, ring-fencing effectiveness, and daily reconciliation processes.

    T269 PSP Safeguarding Assessment
  • 3

    Payment Scheme & Directory Compliance

    Validate scheme compliance: SEPA OLO requirements with T270, CoP/IBAN alias directory integration with T271, and surcharging obligations across card schemes with T272.

    T270 SEPA OLO Readiness T271 Alias/Proxy Directory T272 Surcharging Navigator
  • 4

    APP Fraud & Reimbursement Obligations

    From October 2024, all UK PSPs must reimburse APP fraud victims under PSR PS23/3. Use T406 to model liability splits, assess claim eligibility, and generate Policy Mandate exports for audit trails.

    T406 APP Liability Splitter

Regulatory Coverage

6-Framework Regulatory Coverage

Tools in this hub are mapped to obligations under these frameworks. Verify current applicability with qualified legal counsel before relying on any output.

PSD2 / PSD3

Payment Services Directive 2 (2015/2366/EU) and forthcoming PSD3. Covers PI/EMI authorisation, safeguarding (Art 10), SCA (Art 97–98), and surcharging (Art 62).

UK PSRs 2017

Payment Services Regulations 2017 SI 752. UK post-Brexit equivalent. Governs PI/EMI authorisation (Reg 5), safeguarding (Reg 23), and complaints (Reg 101).

EMD2 / UK EMRs 2011

E-Money Directive 2 (2009/110/EC) and UK E-Money Regulations 2011. Own-funds requirements (EMD2 Art 5), initial capital (Art 4), and safeguarding (Art 7).

PSR APP Rules (Oct 2024)

PSR Policy Statement PS23/3. Mandatory reimbursement for APP fraud victims. 50/50 default split, £415,000 cap, consumer vulnerability protections, 13-month claim window.

FCA Payment Services Approach

FCA Payment Services & E-Money Approach Document. Supervisory expectations for authorisation applications, safeguarding, and client fund protection.

SEPA Regulation

SEPA Regulation (EU) 260/2012 and EPC scheme rulebooks. One-Leg-Out requirements, IBAN-only migration, and CT/DD scheme compliance for cross-border EUR payments.

Quick Start

Get Started in 4 Steps

  • 1

    Score Your Authorisation Readiness

    Open T404 PI/EMI Authorisation Readiness Checker and select your entity type (PI/EMI/AISP/PISP), jurisdiction (UK/EU), intended services, and capital position. Get a 0–100 readiness score with gap-by-gap PSD2/PSR article breakdown.

  • 2

    Calculate EMI Own-Funds Requirement

    If authorising as an EMI, use T405 EMI Capital Calculator to compute your exact own-funds requirement under Methods A, B, and C per EMD2 Art 5. Compare all three methods and get a capital buffer recommendation.

  • 3

    Validate Safeguarding & Scheme Compliance

    Use T269 PSP Safeguarding Assessment to verify client fund protection arrangements. Then check scheme readiness with T270 SEPA OLO, T271 Alias Directory, and T272 Surcharging Navigator.

  • 4

    Model APP Reimbursement Liability

    For UK PSPs, use T406 APP Liability Splitter to model sending/receiving PSP liability splits under PSR PS23/3, apply vulnerability and gross negligence flags, and export Policy Mandate JSON for audit trail.
