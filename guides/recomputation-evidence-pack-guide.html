The Recomputation Evidence Pack: CPA-in-Loop Specialist Input

A recomputation evidence pack packages already-verified OCG receipts (recompute digests, signed case-file binders, and an offline-verifiable handover bundle) into a form a registered public accounting firm can consume as specialist input under AU-C 500/620. It carries no new proof and no attestation of its own. The examination, the opinion, and the license belong to the CPA firm; the pack is the compiled evidence a specialist hands that firm, the same way a valuation or actuarial specialist's work enters an audit today.

CPA-in-Loop, Always AU-C 500/620 Posture Assembly, Not New Proof Zero PII
🧾 We hold no opinion; the licensed firm does. Every page and every sample on this site describing this pack states plainly: it is consumed by a registered public accounting firm, the issuer's own CPA receiving it as specialist input, or Post Oak Labs acting as subcontractor to that CPA. This pack is never sold or offered as a stand-in for an examination, a SOC 2, or any other regulated attestation.
Scope note: a GENIUS Act reserve examination for a registered issuer must come from a registered public accounting firm. That gate is statutory, and no unlicensed vendor's evidence pack can substitute for it. What this pack does is narrower: it compiles the recompute evidence a CPA already needs to scope and perform that examination, in the same shape auditors already accept non-CPA specialist work under AU-C 500/620.

Liability & Coverage

Where E&O sits in this, stated plainly

  • E&O is not legally mandatory for this kind of specialist work in any US state. It is contract-driven: a CPA-firm engagement or subcontract typically requires a certificate of insurance, customarily $1M/$1M, with some flow-downs asking $2M aggregate.
  • Claims-made coverage does not reach back. A policy must be bound before any deliverable is something a client relies on, which is exactly why every pre-signature sample on this page carries the draft-not-for-reliance marking above.
  • No coverage is purchased or promised by this page. Binding is a decision made at the moment an actual engagement is about to sign, not before, and not implied by anything shown here.
  • This page states no response time, no service window, and no delivery cadence for any engagement. None exists to promise.
