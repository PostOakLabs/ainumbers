Quick Start

Get Started in 4 Steps

  1. 1

    Calculate Your A2A Rail Economics

    Open Tool 01 A2A Fee Calculator + Route Optimizer. Enter your monthly transaction volume and average ticket size. Select the rails your institution currently uses. The tool compares all-in per-transaction costs across FedNow, RTP, Same-Day ACH, Next-Day ACH, and SEPA Instant, and calculates your projected annual savings from rail switching. Export the executive ROI report for your CFO or Treasury review.

  2. 2

    Build and Validate an ISO 20022 Message

    Open Tool 02 ISO 20022 Message Builder. Select your message type (pacs.008, pain.001, or camt.056) and complete the guided field input. The builder validates against CBPR+ SR2025 structural rules in real time: mandatory fields are flagged immediately. Use Tool 77 to audit structured address compliance before submission to your payment processor.

  3. 3

    Score Your ISO 20022 Migration Readiness

    Open Tool 81 ISO 20022 Migration Navigator. Select your institution type, the rails in scope (CBPR+, Fedwire, SEPA), and your current message format baseline. The tool scores readiness across six migration dimensions, maps coexistence-period constraints, and generates a phased implementation roadmap with milestone dates. Export the board brief as a Policy Mandate document.

  4. 4

    Diagnose a Payment Failure with RCA

    Open Tool 20 A2A Payment Failure Analyser. Enter the return code(s), rejection reason text, and rail (FedNow, RTP, ACH, SEPA, or SWIFT). The tool decodes the code against its embedded 200+ return code database, identifies the most probable root cause, and generates a structured RCA report with specific remediation steps and an escalation path: ready to share with your counterparty or operations team.
