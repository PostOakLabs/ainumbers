About A2A costs A2A rails (FedNow, RTP, ACH) carry flat per-transaction fees rather than percentage-based interchange. FedNow: $0.045/transaction (Fed pricing). RTP: ~$0.045/transaction. Same-Day ACH: ~$0.25/batch plus per-item. Next-Day ACH: ~$0.18/batch. Costs shown are indicative — actual pricing depends on your bank or processor.
