Scope: read before relying on this
This is a round-trip check against our own export, not an XBRL conformance authority. It confirms the export is internally well-formed (canonical JSON, JCS key order) and that its cited execution_hash matches the artifact it claims to come from. It does not perform DTS discovery, XDT dimensional validation, or formula-linkbase execution, and it is not a submittable wire format for any regime (FFIEC submission is XBRL 2.1 XML; EBA is xBRL-CSV; SEC/ESEF is iXBRL; see SPEC.md §13.13.3). For full taxonomy-aware OIM/XBRL conformance, use a certified processor such as Arelle (Apache-2.0). The execution_hash check below verifies a binding, not a re-execution of the underlying kernel; see SPEC.md §17.2: an advisory published claim, not a cryptographic proof of execution.
