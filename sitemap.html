About this site

AINumbers.co is a free, open-source suite of 40 browser-based fintech tools. All tools run entirely client-side — no data is ever transmitted to a server. Ideal for payments engineers, compliance professionals, treasury analysts, and fintech product managers who need to work with payment standards without standing up infrastructure.

Standards covered: ISO 20022 (pain.001, pain.002, pain.008, pacs.008, pacs.004, pacs.002, camt.053, camt.054, camt.056), SWIFT MT (MT103, MT202, MT940, MT950), NACHA ACH, FedNow, RTP, SEPA SCT, SEPA SCT Inst, IBAN (ISO 13616), BIC (ISO 9362), LEI (ISO 17442), UETR (RFC 4122), MCC (ISO 18245), Visa interchange, Mastercard interchange, Durbin Amendment.
