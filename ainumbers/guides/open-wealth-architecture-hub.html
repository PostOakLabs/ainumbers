Quick-Start Guide
From net worth to retirement probability in five steps
1
Know your baseline. Open the Personal Finance Life Simulator. Enter your age, income, expenses, assets, and liabilities. The tool generates a financial health snapshot. Your inputs are automatically saved as a PSS state — you won't re-enter them.
2
Run the Monte Carlo simulation. Open T107 — Monte Carlo FIRE Trajectory Simulator. Your age and portfolio data from Step 1 will pre-populate via the Portable Scenario State. Set your target retirement age, planned spending, and asset allocation.
3
Read the probability cone, not the median. The P50 line is the median outcome. Focus on the P10 line — that is your downside scenario. If the P10 portfolio reaches $0 before your horizon ends, your plan needs adjustment.
4
Toggle the Sequence of Returns overlay. This shows what happens if markets crash in the first 5 years of your retirement. It is often more damaging than a crash in year 15. If the overlay drops your success rate below 70%, consider a 1–2 year delay or 5–10% spending reduction.
5
Generate your Scenario Code. Click "Generate Scenario Code" to save your exact scenario as an AIN-XXXX-XXXX-XXXX code. Text it to yourself, share it with an advisor, or load it on another device. No account required. No data leaves your browser.
