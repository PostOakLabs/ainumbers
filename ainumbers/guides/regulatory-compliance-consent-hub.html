Consumer Duty Leads

Use T315 to score all four outcome areas, generate evidencing checklists, build MI monitoring frameworks, and produce the annual board attestation template required by FCA.
Sanctions Compliance Officers
OFAC / OFSI programme health
Use T316 to score the institutional programme against OFAC's Five Essential Components, identify gaps, and prepare a Voluntary Self-Disclosure readiness checklist before any regulatory engagement.

Use T314 to build the IBS register, set time-based impact tolerances, and generate a 6-scenario testing agenda. Testing phase is active — FCA expects end-to-end scenario tests per IBS from March 2025.
Transaction Reporting Teams

Use T313 to assess field population readiness across 65 mandatory fields, review ESMA validation rules, and audit LEI population rates before the next ARM submission cycle or supervisory review.
Payments & Clearing Operations
ISO 20022 LEI requirements
Use T317 to batch-validate up to 100 counterparty LEIs, detect LAPSED/RETIRED status before CBPR+ submission, and generate field population maps for pacs.008, pain.001, MiFID II, and EMIR.
CROs & Model Risk Committees
BCBS 239 / SR 11-7 governance
Use T312 to score all 14 BCBS 239 principles and SR 11-7 model risk governance dimensions, produce a gap table with remediation priorities, and generate the board Risk Committee pack.
Regulatory Change Managers

Use T318 to filter the 22-change regulatory database by institution type and jurisdiction, score by effort and risk, generate a 12-month roadmap, and cross-reference every change to the specific AINumbers tool that addresses it.
Quick-Start Guides
Step 1. Open T311. Select your jurisdiction (EU GDPR / UK GDPR / CCPA) and the right type of request — access, erasure, portability, etc.
Step 2. Select the data category (payment data, transaction history, standard personal data) and the requestor type. If the requester is a third-party representative, select that option — the workflow includes verification requirements.
Step 3. Enter the date the request was received — the compliance clock auto-calculates your primary deadline and extension date.
Step 4. Click Run Analysis. You get a step-by-step workflow checklist, identity verification requirements, exemptions relevant to your organisation type (controller vs processor), and draft acknowledgement and response letters.
Step 5. Export Policy Mandate for your DPO audit trail. Export Markdown for your legal team's DSR register.
Step 1. Open T315. Select firm type (manufacturer, distributor, or both) and your primary product category. If you serve a significant proportion of vulnerable customers, select that segment — the tool applies heightened FG21/1 requirements.
Step 2. Rate each of the 16 implementation dimensions across the four outcome areas (PRIN 2A.3–2A.6) on a 0–4 scale. Be honest — a low score triggers specific remediation guidance, not a penalty.
Step 3. Click Run. The RAG scorecard shows which outcome areas are RED/AMBER/GREEN. Focus immediately on any RED outcomes — these block board sign-off.
Step 4. Use the evidencing checklist to confirm you have the documentary evidence FCA would expect at a supervisory review. High priority items are marked.
Step 5. The board report template is pre-populated with your scores. Complete the bracketed fields. Review with your compliance function before presenting. Export Policy Mandate for programme tracking.
Step 1. Open T316. Select your institution type and the applicable regimes (OFAC, OFSI, EU, or all). If you process USD flows, OFAC applies regardless of your home jurisdiction.
Step 2. Rate each of the 25 OFAC Five Essential Components dimensions (5 per component) on a 0–4 scale. The tool covers: Management Commitment, Risk Assessment, Internal Controls, Testing & Auditing, and Training.
Step 3. Review the gap analysis table — gaps are ranked by component and score. The tool provides a specific remediation action for each of the 25 possible gaps.
Step 4. Check the Screening Coverage Audit — the tool shows which sanctions lists are required vs. recommended for your selected regime combination.
Step 5. If you have discovered a potential violation, work through the 9-item VSD (Voluntary Self-Disclosure) readiness checklist. Export Policy Mandate before engaging legal counsel.
Step 1. Open T318. Select institution type, jurisdictions in scope (UK, EU, US, or combinations), and optionally filter by product scope.
Step 2. Select your current compliance programme maturity — the tool contextualises the output narrative to your starting point.
Step 3. Click Generate. The tool filters all 22 changes in the embedded database to those applicable to your profile, sorted by status (in force > upcoming > horizon) and risk.
Step 4. Review the effort/impact 2×2 matrix — Urgent items (high risk + high effort) need immediate resourcing decisions at board level. Quick Wins (high risk + low effort) should be addressed first.
Step 5. Use the tool cross-reference map — every regulatory change in your filtered list links directly to the specific AINumbers.co tool that addresses it. Export Markdown for your board regulatory change report.
