Quick-start guides

Five paths through the RBE toolset — start where your immediate obligation or opportunity lies.

01
Authoring a compliant fee schedule. Start with RBE·08 — Fee Schedule Authoring Engine. Load sample rules or build from scratch across your rail and corridor mix. Set cliff detection to 30% and enable L2/L3 indicators for commercial card rows. Run analysis to detect gaps and price cliffs requiring PSD2 Art. 45 disclosure. Then use RBE·13 to verify the interchange cost floor beneath each fee row.
02
Building a credit underwriting policy. Open RBE·09 — Credit Policy Decision Table Builder. Load the sample policy (FICO × DTI, 6×6 matrix) or configure your own dimensions. Enable Champion-Challenger mode before your governance committee meeting — the divergence table and net approval rate delta export as an SR 11-7-formatted Markdown document.
03
Calibrating AML velocity rules before production. Open RBE·10 — Velocity Rule Simulator. Load the four sample AML rules or define your own. Set the random seed (default: 42) for a reproducible stream. Set the time distribution to “Business Hours” if your transactions are daytime-heavy — temporal velocity rules fire at operationally representative rates. Share the EBA GL/2021/05 pre-deployment audit trail with your model governance team.
04
Classifying a DORA ICT incident under time pressure. Open RBE·11 — DORA Incident Classifier. Click “Load Major Scenario” to verify the tool works. Then enter your actual incident values. The classification time you enter starts the live 4-hour countdown. If Major: the ITS 2025/302 Annex I notification draft auto-populates — complete the three freetext fields before filing with your NCA. Remember: the intermediate report is due 72h from initial notification submission, not from classification.
05
Optimising card interchange costs. Open RBE·13 — Interchange Qualification & LCR Builder. Select your country — the context box explains applicable regulations (Durbin, EU IFR, RBA). Set your card mix and processing capability toggles. Click “Auto-Generate” to build LCR rules from your portfolio. Use the transaction simulator to find downgrade risks on your highest-volume transaction type. The portfolio savings estimate gives you the CFO-ready ROI case for enabling 3DS2, L2/L3 data submission, and LCR.
