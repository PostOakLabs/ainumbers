Quick-start guides

Six paths through the Cat-21 toolset — start where your immediate need is.

01
Preparing for FedNow go-live. Start with T255 — FedNow Participation Readiness Scorer to score your institution against all 47 Federal Reserve requirements. The gap checklist becomes your implementation backlog. Then open Tool 06 — FedNow Participant Lookup to verify whether your key counterparties are already live. Finally, run T258 to size the intraday credit facility your FedNow volume will require under BCBS 248.
02
Building a multi-rail ISO 20022 integration. Use T254 — ISO 20022 Cross-Rail Compatibility Checker to identify exactly which fields behave differently between your source and target rails. The severity-ranked diff report shows HIGH/MEDIUM/LOW issues — prioritise HIGH before deployment. Key gotchas: UETR is mandatory on FedNow but absent from PIX/PayNow; creditor account schemes differ (IBAN vs routing+account vs proxy); reason code registries are non-interoperable across all rails.
03
Configuring RTP fraud controls. Open T256 — RTP Fraud Velocity Rule Builder and select your rail and latency budget (start with 500ms). Add velocity rules one by one — the latency waterfall updates live. Watch for the budget gauge turning amber (>75%) or red (>100%). The conflict detection catches duplicate BLOCK rules and unreachable MONITOR conditions before you push to production. Export the Policy Mandate JSON fraud policy for your payments system configuration.
04
Processing a UK PSR APP fraud claim. Open T257 — PSR APP Fraud Reimbursement Workflow Builder and complete the 4-step claim assessment. The decision engine applies the £85,000 cap, calculates the 50/50 PSP split, assesses gross negligence across 6 evidenced indicators, and generates a dated 5-business-day timeline. Export the Policy Mandate JSON as your compliance audit trail. For portfolio-level liability modelling, see Tool 26 — APP Fraud Liability Decision Matrix.
05
Sizing your intraday liquidity facility. Open T258 — Intraday Credit Facility Sizing Model and enter your peak net debit position, average daily volume, and current LCR position. Select your collateral type — the haircut auto-populates from the Basel III table. The output shows your minimum buffer, recommended facility (buffer + 20% headroom), LCR impact of pre-positioning, and all seven BCBS 248 ILM indicators. For the minute-by-minute cash flow picture, cross-link to Tool 39 — A2A Liquidity Simulator.
06
Compiling a Policy Mandate policy mandate for your payment agent. Use T259 — AP2 Real-Time Payments Policy Builder to configure all five policy dimensions. The live JSON preview updates as you configure each section. The built-in validator catches threshold conflicts before you export. Download the Policy Mandate JSON and reference the agent_instructions array as your agent's ordered rulebook. The MCP server at AINumbers.co exposes all six Cat-21 tools via tools/list for automated discovery.
