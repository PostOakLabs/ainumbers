Quick-Start Guide

Four steps to go from counterparty onboarding to a fully-priced, IFRS 9-compliant credit assessment with Policy Mandate JSON export.

01
Establish the internal credit rating. Open T197 — Credit Risk Rating Calculator, input the counterparty's key financial ratios, select industry sector and counterparty type. Export the Policy Mandate JSON credit profile as your audit trail.
02
Run PD/LGD/EAD and ECL staging. Feed your rating output into T198 — PD/LGD/EAD Modeller to calculate IFRS 9 or CECL parameters, determine the ECL staging, and export the risk parameters as Policy Mandate JSON.
03
Compute RWA and CVA capital. Use T201 — Basel III/IV RWA Calculator for the standardised approach capital requirement, and T200 — CVA Calculator for any derivatives exposures under SA-CCR.
04
Price the deal and stress-test it. Input the T198 expected loss and T201 capital allocation into T203 — RAROC Loan Pricing Model. Then run T202 — Credit Stress Testing Workbench to validate the pricing holds under macro scenarios. Export the Policy Mandate JSON loan pricing record.
