Mandate Timeline

Key e-invoicing mandate effective dates and compliance deadlines. Urgency rating reflects time remaining as at May 2026.

Live since Jan 2019
Italy SdI — FatturaPA mandatory B2B
All Italian B2B and B2C invoices must flow through the Sistema di Interscambio (SDI) in FatturaPA XML format. Italy was the first EU country to mandate e-invoicing nationwide.
IT · SdI · FatturaPA
Live since Jan 2025
Germany — receive structured e-invoices mandatory
All German VAT-registered businesses must be capable of receiving XRechnung v3.0 or Peppol BIS 3.0 structured e-invoices. Issuance mandatory from Jan 2027 (large) / Jan 2028 (SME).
DE · XRechnung v3.0 · JStG 2024
1 January 2026 — 8 months
Belgium — Peppol BIS 3.0 mandatory (all businesses)
All Belgian VAT-registered companies must issue and receive structured B2B e-invoices in Peppol BIS 3.0 format via the Peppol network. Hermes platform routes to PPF for cross-border.
BE · Peppol BIS 3.0 · Loi 2023
1 Feb 2026 · 1 Apr 2026 — 9–11 months
Poland KSeF — mandatory clearance e-invoicing
KSeF clearance mandatory for large taxpayers (VAT turnover > PLN 200m in 2024) from 1 February 2026; all other VAT-registered businesses from 1 April 2026. FA(3) XML schema required.
PL · KSeF · FA(3) · ustawa 2023
Sep 2026 · Mar 2027 · Sep 2027
France — PPF/PDP clearance e-invoicing (phased)
Large enterprises must receive and issue e-invoices from September 2026; mid-size from March 2027; small from September 2027. All must route through France PPF or an accredited PDP.
FR · PPF/PDP · Factur-X · Ord.2021-1190
Jan 2027 / Jan 2028
Germany — issue structured e-invoices mandatory
Large German enterprises must issue XRechnung or Peppol BIS 3.0 from January 2027; SMEs from January 2028. ZUGFeRD hybrid format (PDF/A-3 + XML) also accepted.
DE · XRechnung · ZUGFeRD · JStG 2024
1 January 2030
EU ViDA DRR Phase 1 — intra-EU cross-border e-invoicing
ViDA Digital Reporting Requirements Phase 1: mandatory structured e-invoicing for all intra-EU cross-border B2B supplies. EN 16931-compliant format. Near-real-time reporting (2-day window).
EU ViDA · DRR Phase 1 · EN 16931
1 January 2032
EU ViDA CTC Phase 2 — domestic reporting extension
ViDA Phase 2 extends Digital Reporting Requirements to domestic B2B transactions within EU member states. Member states may implement CTC earlier under national mandates (France, Italy, Poland).
EU ViDA · CTC Phase 2 · 2032
Key Concepts

Core terminology and frameworks for EU e-invoicing and VAT compliance.

🔄
4-Corner / 5-Corner Model
C1: Seller, C2: Sender AP, C3: Receiver AP, C4: Buyer. CTC countries (France, Poland) add C5 — a national tax authority platform that must clear or receive the invoice before or during delivery.
📋
CTC — Continuous Transaction Controls
A tax authority model where invoices are reported to or cleared by the government in real or near-real time. Clearance model (France PPF, Poland KSeF): tax authority approves before sending. Post-audit model (Greece myDATA): report after exchange.
🌐
ViDA — VAT in the Digital Age
EU legislative package amending VAT Directive 2006/112/EC. Three pillars: DRR (digital reporting), Single VAT Registration (OSS extension), and Platform Economy (deemed supplier for ride-sharing and short-term accommodation). Political agreement reached November 2024.
🏛️
OSS & IOSS
OSS (One-Stop Shop): EU VAT registration for B2C distance sales — single return covers all EU member states. Threshold: EUR 10,000 global. IOSS (Import One-Stop Shop): for goods imported <EUR 150 — charge VAT at point of sale to avoid import VAT complexity.
📄
EN 16931 — EU Invoice Standard
European standard for electronic invoicing semantic data model (CEN/TC 434). Defines mandatory fields: seller/buyer VAT IDs, IBAN (BT-84), payment reference (BT-89), tax breakdown, and delivery information. Basis for all ViDA DRR-compliant e-invoices. Implemented as Peppol BIS 3.0, Factur-X, XRechnung, and PINT.
🔗
ISO 11649 — Structured Creditor Reference
International standard for structured remittance references. RF format: "RF" + 2-digit MOD 97 check digits + alphanumeric reference (max 21 chars). Referenced as SCOR (Structured Creditor Reference) in ISO 20022 pain.001. Enables straight-through reconciliation without manual matching.
💳
DST — Digital Services Tax
Unilateral national taxes on digital services revenues pending OECD Pillar 1 multilateral solution. Cover online advertising, online intermediation, and data transmission. Thresholds vary: global (EUR 750m most jurisdictions) and country-specific. OECD Pillar 1 Amount A would replace DSTs but is not yet in force.
🔒
E-Invoice Archiving & PAF
EU requires authenticity of origin, integrity of content, and legibility for the full retention period (5–11 years by jurisdiction). France PAF (Piste d'Audit Fiable): documented audit trail from order → invoice → payment → delivery is mandatory even for paper invoices. Italy requires conservazione sostitutiva (qualified digital preservation with QES + timestamp).
🤖
AP2 — Agentic Payments Protocol
Machine-readable JSON mandate format used by AINumbers.co tools. Encodes compliance assessments, payment policies, and regulatory citations into structured agent instructions. T178 outputs payment_policy; T179, T180, T183 output compliance_control mandates. Consumed by AI agents for automated compliance workflows.
