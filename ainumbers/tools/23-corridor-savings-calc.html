About This Tool ▾

What it simulates: The Cross-Border Corridor Savings Calculator models the all-in cost of sending money across 25 major remittance corridors using four payment rails: traditional correspondent banking, fintech providers (e.g. Wise/Remitly), stablecoin rails (USDC/USDT), and tokenized A2A (AINumbers.co model at ~0.20% flat fee). Costs include simulated flat fees, FX spread, and opportunity cost (float on multi-day settlement).

Target audience: Bank treasury teams, fintech product managers, and payment consultants preparing client-facing materials or internal business cases for A2A rail migration.

Data privacy note: This tool calculates mock corridor savings using client-side logic only. No real transaction data, account information, or PII is processed or transmitted. All figures are embedded as static mock data and labeled as illustrative estimates.

Formula used: All-in cost = flat fee + (FX spread % × amount) + opportunity cost (settlement days × daily cost of capital × amount). Annual projection multiplies per-transaction savings by estimated annual frequency.

Privacy Statement ▾

This tool calculates mock corridor savings using client-side logic. No real transaction data, account information, or PII is processed or transmitted. All processing happens in your browser. No data is sent to any server. No analytics, no cookies, no telemetry.
