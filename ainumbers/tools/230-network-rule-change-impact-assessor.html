About This Tool

This tool models the estimated impact of 2025–2026 card network and NACHA rule changes on a transaction portfolio. Rule changes are drawn from the current Visa, Mastercard, and NACHA published update cycles. Impact estimates are indicative — actual impact depends on portfolio composition, issuer mix, and specific programme configurations.

⚠ Impact estimates are indicative only and based on published rule change summaries. Always verify effective dates and specific requirements directly with the relevant network or NACHA. Last Reviewed: 2026-05-09.
