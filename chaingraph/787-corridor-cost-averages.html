Corridor Cost Averages

Computes one remittance corridor's cost THREE ways from the provider quotes you paste in, and reports each average against the SDG 10.c 3 percent target and the G20 no-corridor-above-5 percent per-corridor target for 2030. The simple average is the UN SDG indicator 10.c.1 metadata section 4.c simple average. The lowest-N average defaults to n=3: the SmaRT average, the simple average of the three cheapest transparent services, defined by the same metadata section. The weighted average is a market-share-weighted analyst convention motivated by the World Bank RPW survey frame; it reports null with a reason code when shares are missing or do not sum to 100 within tolerance, and is never silently renormalised. Which average you quote can decide whether a corridor "meets" SDG 10.c - so this page shows all three side by side, plus the spread between them.
