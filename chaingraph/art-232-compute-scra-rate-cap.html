SCRA 6% Interest-Rate Cap

Computes the SCRA 6% interest-rate cap per 50 USC §3937 and determines whether excess interest is forgiven (never deferred). Applies to pre-service obligations of active-duty servicemembers upon written notice. Covers retroactive interest credit from the date military service begins. Table: SCRA-50USC3937-2022.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
ⓘ Not legal or compliance advice. SCRA coverage requires verification that the obligation pre-dates active-duty orders and that written notice (with a copy of the orders) was provided to the lender. This tool computes the arithmetic of the rate reduction; it does not substitute for legal review of covered-servicemember status.
