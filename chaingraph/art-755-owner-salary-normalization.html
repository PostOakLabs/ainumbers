Owner Salary Normalization

Separates what a replacement manager would actually cost from what the owner takes out of the business, so seller discretionary earnings and normalized EBITDA come out on a comparable basis. Employer payroll taxes are the employer share only (IRC 3111), computed from the 2026 OASDI wage base unless you enter your own figure.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted, and this page makes no network calls. Use synthetic or anonymised figures only; enter no real taxpayer data.
Scope & reliance
This node produces an illustrative planning estimate and is not tax advice; a CPA or tax attorney should review any real decision. Declared limits: employer payroll taxes cover the employer share only (IRC 3111), federal rules only, one year only, one replacement manager; the market wage, benefits load, SDE multiple and the 60%-of-replacement threshold are caller-supplied or labelled heuristics, and the pattern flag is a neutral observation about the numbers rather than a finding about any owner. Results are a deterministic recompute checked against 31 captured fixtures, with the kernel shipping compute_proof_ready: deferred.
