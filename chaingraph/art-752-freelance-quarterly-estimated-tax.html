Freelance Quarterly Estimated Tax Calculator

Quarterly IRS estimated payments for self-employed income: net SE income, SE tax (Social Security + Medicare), federal income tax on the 2026 brackets, an elective state bucket, the 2026 due dates, and the safe-harbor amount. Enter your figures, run.

Scope & reliance
The node computes net SE income (gross minus business expenses), SE tax at 12.4% Social Security (capped at the 2026 wage base of $184,500) plus 2.9% Medicare on 92.35% of net earnings, federal income tax on the 2026 brackets after the employer-equivalent half of SE tax and the 2026 standard deduction ($16,100 single / $32,200 MFJ), an elective state bucket (none/low/mid/high = 0/3/5/9.3%, an illustrative assumption rather than a jurisdiction lookup), equal quarterly installments of the annual total, and the safe harbor (the lesser of 100% of prior-year federal tax or 90% of current-year federal tax). Ported from ApexLogics Apex #116 (AL-123) with the official 2026 IRS constants. One constant was corrected to the official source at porting: the Q2 2026 due date ships as June 15, 2026 per IRS Form 1040-ES, where the Apex page listed June 16 (payload DIFFERS.md D1). Three Apex omissions ride flagged rather than silently patched: the 0.9% Additional Medicare Tax, the 110% high-earner safe-harbor prong, and the $1,000 de minimis exemption (DIFFERS.md D2-D4, report items R2-R3); above those thresholds this calculator understates tax, exactly as the source page does.
