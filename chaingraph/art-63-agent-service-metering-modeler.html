ART-63 · Wave 14 · Agent Economy Runtime · W-D

Agent-Service Metering & Marketplace Economics Modeler

Model the unit economics of an agent-consumed service paid via x402: per-call pricing, settlement cost (batched vs per-request), marketplace take-rate, net margin, break-even volume, and sensitivity analysis across batch sizes. The economics counterpart to the Arc/Tempo modelers, for the agent-service micropayment market. Client-side, zero-egress; emits a hash-anchored OpenChainGraph v0.4 artifact.

EDUCATIONAL ESTIMATOR — not pricing, legal, or financial advice · All figures illustrative · Verify x402 V2 cost mechanics against current Linux Foundation x402 Foundation primary sources
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
