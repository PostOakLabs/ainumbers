Story PIL Flavor Mapper

Maps creator answers to a Story Protocol Programmable IP License (PIL) flavor and emits the full PILTerms struct. Three standard flavors: Non-Commercial Social Remixing (licenseTermsId=1, the protocol constant), Commercial Use, and Commercial Remix. Kernel twin of T522, callable via MCP.

Story Protocol PIL PILTerms Struct licenseTermsId=1 Zero PII Selection Only
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ This tool maps creator answers to a standard Story Protocol PIL flavor. It does not draft custom license terms and is not legal advice. Story PIL documentation and license text govern; consult a licensed attorney before relying on any output. See: docs.story.foundation/concepts/programmable-ip-license/pil-flavors
