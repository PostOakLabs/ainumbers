BNPL Plan Comparator

A side-by-side of buy now, pay later plan costs: schedules, fees and finance charges for the plans you enter, every APR computed on the estate's Reg Z Appendix J maths, and provider plan shapes carried as cited labelled examples, never offers.

Presenter mode shows one step per screen. Arrow keys move, A toggles autoplay, Esc exits.
1
Step 1

Price several plans on one purchase

Enter a principal and up to eight plans; each plan is priced on the same stream discipline, then laid side by side.

A plan is either a provider preset or explicit parameters: number of instalments, cadence (weekly, biweekly or monthly), stated APR, and fees. The comparison ranks plans by total of payments, exact to the cent, and names the lowest-total plan; a tie stays a tie.

An agent asks at this stepMCP
compare_bnpl_plans {"principal":400,"plans":[{"preset":"paypal_pay_in_4"},{"preset":"affirm_monthly_example"}]}
2
Step 2

Every APR comes from the Reg Z Appendix J solver

This node authors no APR maths of its own: it carries the estate's Reg Z Appendix J solver inline, copied verbatim, and hands it each plan's payment stream; a parity test holds the copy equal to the live art-215 module.

The stream is expressed in unit periods: instalment one at purchase sits at period 0, the rest at 1, 2, 3. Amounts pass to the solver as exact cent integers so a zero-cost stream prices at exactly 0. When no sign-change bracket exists the solver reports no rate, and this node returns a null APR with a named flag rather than a guess.

An agent asks at this stepMCP
compare_bnpl_plans {"principal":500,"plans":[{"num_instalments":6,"cadence":"monthly","apr_pct":19.9,"instalment_amount":88.25}]}
3
Step 3

Read pay-in-N as periods from purchase

The shape every provider calls pay in 4: instalment one at purchase, the rest one unit period apart.

Cadence sets the unit period: weekly is 52 periods a year, biweekly 26, monthly 12. An interest-free custom plan splits the principal equally with the cent remainder on the last instalment, so the pieces always sum to the principal exactly. An interest-bearing custom plan must carry its quoted instalment amount: this node compares plans as quoted, it does not originate an amortizing schedule.

4
Step 4

A small fee moves the APR more than the rate does

Fees are part of the finance charge, so the Appendix J rate prices them in; on short plans the effect is large.

The same maths reads the providers' own labelled examples honestly. Klarna's published apply-in-advance example ($248 purchase, $62 down and three biweekly $62, $4.99 service fee, a stated 35.68% APR) prices at 35.6757% on this method; Afterpay's finance-fee example ($110 loan, $27.50 down and three biweekly $28.26, stated 36%) prices at 35.7644%. Late fees are different: they are contingent on missed payments, never part of a schedule here, and each preset cites the provider's stated late-fee clause as information only.

An agent asks at this stepMCP
compare_bnpl_plans {"principal":100,"plans":[{"num_instalments":4,"cadence":"biweekly","apr_pct":0},{"num_instalments":4,"cadence":"biweekly","apr_pct":0,"per_instalment_fee":1.25}]}
5
Step 5

Presets are labelled examples with clause citations

Every preset is a provider's own published example or plan shape, quoted with clause id, snapshot path and fetch date. None is an offer.

The quotes are verbatim from dated snapshots of the providers' US consumer pages (fetched 2026-10-28): research/clause-snapshots/bnpl-paypal-2026-10, bnpl-klarna-2026-10, bnpl-afterpay-2026-10 and bnpl-affirm-2026-10. Terms vary by provider, merchant and credit decision, and each preset carries the provider's own qualifier ("Options depend on your purchase amount, may vary by merchant, and may not be available in all states."), the lender of record where stated, and the late-fee clause. A preset with no published dollar example fills only the plan shape on your principal; a labelled example carries the provider's own amounts.

An agent asks at this stepMCP
compare_bnpl_plans {"principal":248,"plans":[{"preset":"klarna_service_fee_example"}]}
6
Step 6

What one comparison returns

Per plan: the schedule, the totals, the finance charge, and the computed APR. Across plans: a ranking by total cost.

Refusals are named, never silent: an unknown preset, an unknown cadence, preset and explicit fields together, a bad principal, instalment count, fee or APR, a non-USD currency, more than eight plans, or an interest-bearing plan without a quoted instalment each return a domain_error by name. The stated APR you entered is echoed next to the computed one, and a flag names any gap wider than one percentage point.
