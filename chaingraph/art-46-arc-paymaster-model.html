Arc Paymaster Economics

Model Circle Paymaster USDC-as-gas economics on Arc vs native gas on Ethereum. Circle Paymaster implements ERC-4337 account abstraction — allowing agents and users to pay gas in USDC or be sponsored. Outputs per-UserOperation costs, sponsorship break-even, and a CFO memo. Grounded in ERC-4337 spec and Circle Arc Paymaster documentation. Client-side. Zero PII.
