Can't Be Evil License Selector

Rights-matrix lookup for a16z's six Can't Be Evil NFT licenses. Answer four questions and get the matching license with both on-chain enum names (launch alias and current Oct 2022 name), Solidity LicenseVersion ordinal, both Arweave manifest URIs, full rights matrix, and normative caveats verified against canonical license PDFs. Kernel twin of T521, callable via MCP.

a16z Can't Be Evil NFT Licensing Arweave Zero PII Selection Only
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ This tool selects among the six standard a16z Can't Be Evil licenses based on your answers. It does not draft custom terms and is not legal advice. The canonical Arweave license texts govern; verify before relying on any output for commercial or legal decisions. Consult a licensed attorney for your jurisdiction.
Presets:
Select CBE_CC0 (PUBLIC ordinal 0) to place the work in the public domain with no conditions. Choosing yes skips the remaining questions.
Yes grants use and display plus commercial exploitation rights. No restricts the grant to personal use and display only (CBE_PR family).
Exclusive (CBE_ECR / EXCLUSIVE ordinal 1) means the creator gives up retained commercial exploitation via a pre-grant covenant. Creator still owns copyright. Only applies when commercial = yes.
Adds the §1.6 broad objectionable-use restriction (11 categories). Enforced via ordinary §3.2 material-breach termination — NOT automatic termination. Ignored when exclusive = yes.
