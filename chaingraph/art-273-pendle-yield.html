Pendle PT/YT Yield Decomposition

Decomposes Pendle Finance Principal Token (PT) and Yield Token (YT) positions. Computes PT implied fixed yield, YT leverage and break-even APY, invariant check, and notional sizing.

gpu:false analytics_mandate groth16-deferred
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Not financial or investment advice. PT and YT are experimental DeFi instruments; prices change continuously with AMM trading. Underlying yields are variable; YT carries total loss risk if underlying yield drops to zero. For informational purposes only.
Market Prices
Principal Token price as fraction of face (1.0). E.g. 0.94 = buys at 6% discount.
Yield Token price. Default auto = 1 - PT price. PT + YT must sum to ~1.0.
Calendar days until PT/YT expire. 180 = 6-month pool.
Current underlying yield APY (e.g. sUSDe or aUSDC yield). Used for YT profitability analysis.
Notional size for position sizing analysis. Zero to skip.
