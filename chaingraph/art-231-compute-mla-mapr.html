MLA MAPR Calculator (closed-end)

Computes the Military Annual Percentage Rate for closed-end consumer credit only, and tests it against the 36 percent limit that 10 USC §987(b) imposes and 32 CFR §232.4(b) sets out. Because §232.4(c)(2)(i) directs that a closed-end MAPR is calculated by Regulation Z's own annual-percentage-rate rules over the wider charge set in §232.4(c)(1), this page solves the Appendix J actuarial equation from a cash-flow schedule rather than dividing charges by an original principal. The term is day-granular, so a 45-day loan is expressible instead of being quantised to whole months. Table: MLA-DOD-32CFR232-2015-10-01.
