DTI Ratio Calculator

Front-end (housing) and back-end (total) debt-to-income ratios per Fannie Mae Selling Guide B3-6-02 and Freddie Mac Single-Family Seller/Servicer Guide 5401.2. Classifies the back-end ratio into a standard / extended-compensating-factors / AUS-only / exceeds-max tier and checks it against the max DTI for the selected underwriting type. Feeds ART-222 Agency Eligibility Matrix.
