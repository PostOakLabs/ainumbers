ART-467 · DORA Reporting Clock (2025/301 Art. 5)

DORA Major-Incident Reporting Clock

Computes the DORA 2025/301 Art. 5 report-stage deadline schedule from caller-declared timestamps: the initial notification is due within 4 hours of classification AND no later than 24 hours after awareness (the earlier limb binds); the intermediate report within 72 hours of SUBMISSION of the initial; the final report no later than one month after the intermediate or the latest updated intermediate. Weekend/bank-holiday extension to noon next working day, with the entity-class exceptions on initial/intermediate and kept on final. Emits a hash-anchored OpenChainGraph v0.4 artifact. Classification is not performed here: ART-09 classifies per 2024/1772 Art. 8(1) and consumes this clock result.

Deadline schedule, not a filing system
Not legal advice
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Use synthetic or anonymised inputs only, do not enter real personal or confidential data.
Scope limits, read before use: this tool computes the 2025/301 Art. 5 clock schedule only; it does not classify. Authority is pinned at clause level (2025/301 Art. 5 time limits and extension exceptions; Arts. 1-4 content; 2025/302 Art. 7 TPP aggregated reporting; Regulation 1182/71 month arithmetic; DORA Arts. 19-20). All timestamps are caller-declared with explicit offsets; due-ness states are computed against the declared logical date, never a wall clock. Under the REVERSED D split, ART-09 classifies (2024/1772 Art. 8(1) gateway) and consumes this schedule through the declared chain edge (art-467 feeds art-09). This tool computes deadlines; it does not itself transmit, file, or submit any regulatory notification.
Drives the Art. 5 weekend/bank-holiday extension exceptions on initial/intermediate.
24h initial-report limb starts here.
4h initial-report limb starts here; the earlier limb binds.
Intermediate 72h limb starts at SUBMISSION, never at the initial deadline.
Final one-month limb starts here when no later update exists.
Re-bases the final deadline when later than the intermediate submission.
The instant the not_yet_due/due/overdue states are computed against, never a wall clock.
Declared, never inferred. Holiday dates below.
