NIS2 Entity Scope Classifier (Essential / Important / Out-of-Scope)

Classify an entity as Essential, Important, or Out-of-Scope under NIS2 Directive 2022/2555 Annex I and II, applying sector codes, employee/turnover size thresholds, and automatic-essential carve-outs.

NIS2 Directive 2022/2555Active Oct 2024Annex I / IIZero PIIW3C VC §13.11
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope
Root node of the nis2-compliance chain (art-141→142→143). Classifies entities under NIS2 Directive 2022/2555 using Annex I (essential sectors) and Annex II (important sectors) combined with size thresholds. Certain entity types (DNS providers, qualified trust service providers, public electronic communications networks) are automatically classified as Essential regardless of size.
