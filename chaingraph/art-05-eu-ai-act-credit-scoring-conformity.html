⏰ 2 December 2027 — EU AI Act Annex III Part 5(b) high-risk AI obligations for credit scoring and creditworthiness assessment fully apply. This includes Articles 9 (risk management), 10 (data governance), 11 (technical documentation), 13 (transparency), 14 (human oversight), and 15 (accuracy and robustness). Entities subject to this regulation must complete conformity assessment before this date.
⚠ Educational pre-assessment only. Bias metrics are computed on user-entered synthetic or anonymised performance data — results are indicative, not a substitute for a full third-party conformity assessment or notified body certification. Regulatory citations are real (EU AI Act, Regulation (EU) 2024/1689). The 0.8 disparate impact threshold is a widely used indicator (EEOC four-fifths rule, referenced in EU anti-discrimination guidance); the EU AI Act does not specify a numeric threshold — verify with legal counsel. Enter your model's approval rates and error rates (TPR / FPR) per demographic group for each protected characteristic. Use synthetic or anonymised aggregate statistics — do not enter individual records. Disparate Impact Ratio (DIR) = lowest group approval rate ÷ highest group approval rate. DIR < 0.8 flags adverse impact. Equalized Odds Gap = max spread in TPR or FPR across groups — gap > 5pp flags imbalance.
