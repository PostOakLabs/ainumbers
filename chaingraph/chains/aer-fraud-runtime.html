Runtime Agent-Payment Fraud Surveillance

Runtime fraud surveillance over an autonomous agent's payment stream — applied after payments are executing, not before. Detects spend-policy violations in the live payment stream (art-02), graphs agent-network collusion or phantom-agent patterns (mms-03), and monitors payment velocity for statistical anomalies (ml-03). Designed for agent-ops teams watching autonomous payment streams with no human approval.

How this chain runs. Each stage is a v0.4 OpenChainGraph tool: call over MCP or in-browser, capture execution_hash, pass as next stage's parent_hashes. Terminal artifact carries full provenance and is exportable via export_artifact.
All three stages are reused tools applied to a new runtime context. Stage 1 (art-02, Wave 6 pre-trade) simulates spend policy to assess what an agent is allowed before a payment; here it detects violations in the live payment stream of an agent already operating — policy-enforcement in motion, not pre-trade simulation. Stage 2 (mms-03) was built for authorized-push (APP) fraud graph analysis; here it is applied to agent-network topology — phantom agents, collusion rings, and payment-splitting schemes in an autonomous agent commerce network, which is structurally identical to APP fraud at the network layer. Stage 3 (ml-03) applies timeseries anomaly detection to payment velocity in the agent's live stream — statistical process control over an autonomous payment pipeline. Runtime / post-trade reframe: Wave 6 tools performing surveillance over an executing autonomous payment stream rather than their original pre-trade analysis context. Not legal, compliance, or fraud-detection advice.
