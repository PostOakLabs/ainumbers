How this chain runs. Each stage is an OpenChainGraph v0.4 tool: call it over MCP or in-browser, capture its execution_hash, and pass it as the next stage's parent_hashes. The terminal artifact carries the full provenance chain and is exportable (pdf / xlsx) via export_artifact.
Stage 2 (510 digital-asset-regulatory-classifier) was built for tokenized-asset classification; the jurisdiction decision-tree applies natively to MLETR adoption status across trade corridors. Stage 3 (cry-04 Merkle verifier) applies its SHA-256 batch integrity check natively to the MLETR Art. 10 authoritative-copy requirement. Citations: UNCITRAL MLETR Arts. 8–12; UK ETDA 2023; Singapore ETA; France Decree 2025-811. Verify jurisdiction status against current UNCITRAL MLETR status table — educational tool, not legal advice.
