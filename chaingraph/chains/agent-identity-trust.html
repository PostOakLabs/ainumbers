Agent Identity & Trust-Chain

The horizontal agent↔agent trust complement to the vertical payments chain. In three hash-linked steps: validate an A2A v1.0 agent card and its delegated-authority chain, attest the agent's identity against KYA-OS (DIF Trusted AI Agents WG), then simulate the spend policy. The chain answers three questions in order — who is this agent, what is it authorized to do, and does its spend policy hold. This is a links-only narrative orchestration; the terminal node emits a unified OpenChainGraph §4 artifact.

1
Validate the A2A Agent Card & Trust Chain ART-32
A2A Agent-Card Trust-Chain Validator · validate_a2a_trust_chain · compliance_mandate · root
▼
Validate an A2A v1.0 agent-card.json (served at /.well-known/agent-card.json) and its delegated-authority chain. Deterministic checks: card schema (name/url/version/protocolVersion/capabilities/skills), the signature / verification-method block, capability extension URIs, and the delegation chain — depth ≤ 4, no scope escalation across hops, validity windows ≤ 90 days, no expired links. Establishes who this agent is and that its authority was lawfully delegated.
chain_depth: 0 (root) parent_hashes: [] mandate_type: compliance_mandate Client-Side · Zero PII
Handoff → ART-04
Export ART-32's §4 artifact. Its execution_hash becomes the single entry in ART-04's parent_hashes. The validated agent card and delegation chain are the credential ART-04 attests against KYA-OS / DIF.
Open ART-32 →
2
Attest Agent Identity & Authorization ART-04
Agent Identity Attestation Checker · check_agent_attestation · compliance_mandate · depth:1
▼
Validate the agent identity credential and delegated-authority chain against KYA-OS (DIF Trusted AI Agents WG, donated March 2026): credential-chain integrity, scope limits, validity windows, delegation depth, and EU AI Act (Aug 2026) compliance posture. This node answers what the agent is authorized to do — turning the structurally-valid A2A card from Step 1 into an attested identity-and-scope determination.
chain_depth: 1 parent: ART-32 mandate_type: compliance_mandate Client-Side · Zero PII
Handoff → ART-02
Export ART-04's §4 artifact. Its execution_hash becomes ART-02's parent_hashes entry (passed as parent_artifact). The attested scope limits and caps define the spend policy that the terminal node stress-tests.
Open ART-04 →
3
Simulate the Spend Policy ART-02 · TERMINAL
Agent Spend-Policy Simulator · simulate_spend_policy · payment_policy · chain_depth:2
▼
Simulate N synthetic AP2 v0.2 agent transactions against the attested spend policy and detect per-transaction over-limits, daily/monthly cumulative violations, scope creep (unauthorized merchant/category), policy-bypass drip patterns that evade per-tx caps, and unauthorized Human-Not-Present autonomous flows. This answers the third question — does the agent's spend policy actually hold under load — closing the identity → authorization → behavior arc.
chain_depth: 2 (terminal) parent: ART-04 (via parent_artifact) mandate_type: payment_policy Client-Side · Zero PII
Terminal — emits unified §4 artifact
ART-02 emits the terminal OpenChainGraph §4 artifact with its own execution_hash, chain.parent_hashes set to ART-04's hash, and chain_depth: 2 — a single object proving the agent is who it claims, is authorized for its scope, and operates a spend policy that withstands simulated abuse.

Terminal Node Emits One Unified §4 Artifact
This is a links-only narrative chain — there is no in-frame composer. Run each node in its own tool, export its §4 artifact, and carry each execution_hash forward. The terminal node, ART-02, emits a single OpenChainGraph §4 artifact whose parent_hashes reference the upstream identity-trust nodes — one cryptographic object answering who the agent is, what it may do, and whether its spend policy holds.
