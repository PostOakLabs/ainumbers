Arc Fit Diagnostic
12-question A–F diagnostic assessing Arc adoption fit across four dimensions: D1 CPN (Circle Payments Network global rail), D2 StableFX (24/7 on-chain FX, Herstatt risk elimination, PFMI P12), D3 DvP atomic settlement (on-chain delivery-vs-payment, ERC-4337 Paymaster), D4 Agentic commerce (USDC-as-gas, x402/MPP agent payment flows). Inputs: organization type, payment volume, FX exposure, existing rails, jurisdiction. Output: scored fit report, chain routing recommendation, and an execution_hash-anchored §4 artifact.
