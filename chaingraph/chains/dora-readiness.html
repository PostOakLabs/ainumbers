DORA Readiness Chain

DORA (EU Regulation 2022/2554) enforcement has been active since 17 January 2025. This chain covers ~22,000 in-scope financial entities and ICT TPPs. Where you start depends on your situation — a live incident, a board gap review, or an upcoming NCA supervisory engagement each require a different sequence. Two OpenChainGraph-promoted nodes (ART-29 diagnostic + ART-09 incident classifier) anchor the chain with §4 hash-anchored artifacts.

DORA Major-Incident Reporting Threshold Classifier
Classify the ICT incident against DORA Article 17/19 criteria — availability, authenticity, integrity, confidentiality impacts — and determine whether it crosses the major incident threshold requiring NCA notification.
