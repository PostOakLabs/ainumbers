Tempo Zones (June 2026)

Tempo Zones are private permissioned parallel chains. Operator sees all transactions — enabling full AML surveillance. Users see only their own transactions — preserving commercial confidentiality. Outsiders see only ZK validity proofs — no transaction data is exposed. TIP-403 allowlist, blocklist, and freeze rules apply across zones. This chain validates that the operator's AML surveillance capability and regulatory disclosure obligations are met without exposing competitor activity.

⬡ Bilateral Verification · Operator ↔ Auditor/Regulator

Zone operator emits the disclosure attestation (ART-39); auditor or regulator re-runs verify_execution_hash to confirm that AML coverage is maintained and competitors see nothing. The CRY-01 ZK proof enables the regulator to verify compliance without requiring access to the Zone's full transaction set — preserving the confidentiality of non-sanctioned participants.

Tempo On-Chain AML (Zone Transaction Set)
Run ART-38 on the full Zone transaction set — the operator-visible view of all transfers within the Zone. This establishes the AML surveillance baseline: OFAC/SDN screening across all Zone transfers (not just the operator's own), FATF Travel Rule completeness for transfers at or above the $3,000 threshold, and SAR determination for flagged transactions. The operator's Zone-wide visibility is the legal basis for this step — regulators expect operators to screen the transactions they can see. Outputs a §4 execution_hash covering the operator's Zone-level AML screening run.

ZK Compliance Proof Generator
Generate a ZK (zero-knowledge) compliance proof that the Zone's AML obligations are met — without revealing the underlying transaction data to the verifier. The proof attests: AML screening coverage ≥ [operator-declared threshold]% of Zone transfer volume, no unresolved SAR flags outstanding, and TIP-403 freeze controls are active for all flagged counterparties. The verifier (regulator or auditor) can confirm compliance using only the ZK proof and the ART-39 execution_hash, without accessing the Zone transaction set. The terminal §4 execution_hash covers the full W-F chain: ART-38 → ART-39 → CRY-01.
