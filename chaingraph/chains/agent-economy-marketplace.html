Agent-Service Marketplace Onboarding & Metering

Onboards an agent service into a micropayment marketplace with a metered, policy-gated listing. Verifies seller and buyer agent identity and authorization for listing (art-04), builds an acceptance policy governing what agent traffic the service will accept (art-21), then models the metering and pricing structure for the listed service (ART-63). The operator/marketplace view of the agent-economy runtime layer.

How this chain runs. Each stage is a v0.4 OpenChainGraph tool: call over MCP or in-browser, capture execution_hash, pass as next stage's parent_hashes. Terminal artifact carries full provenance and is exportable (pdf / xlsx / csv) via export_artifact.
Stage 1 (art-04, Wave 6 pre-trade) was built to attest an agent's identity for commerce authorization before a transaction; here it is applied to marketplace onboarding — verifying the seller/buyer agent's identity and authorization to list a service, which is a runtime/operational decision for a marketplace operator rather than a pre-trade buyer check. Stage 2 (art-21, Wave 6) builds an acceptance policy governing agent traffic into a system; here that policy governs which agents may consume the listed service — scoped to the marketplace listing operator context, not buyer-side pre-trade filtering. Stage 3 (ART-63, Wave 14) completes the onboarding by modeling the metering and economics of the listed service, including take-rate, batch settlement savings, and break-even — the commercial terms the marketplace operator sets. Educational estimator — not pricing or marketplace-rule advice.
