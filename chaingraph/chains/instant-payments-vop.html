Instant Payments & VoP Chain

The EU Instant Payments Regulation (IPR, EU 2024/886) is live. Verification of Payee (VoP) has been mandatory for euro-area PSPs offering SCT Inst since 9 October 2025. This chain covers the full IPR compliance workflow in five steps: rail participation gap check → VoP batch match-rate analysis (ART-11, OpenChainGraph §4 artifact) → intraday credit facility sizing → SEPA IPR annual report → RTP policy mandate. Each step's output conditions the next.

EU IPR 2024/886 · VoP Live Oct 2025 ART-11 · OpenChainGraph §4 Artifact 5 Steps · Linear Client-side · Zero PII Wave B
⚠ Regulatory deadlines

VoP mandatory for euro-area PSPs offering SCT and SCT Inst: 9 October 2025 (already in force). EEA non-euro PSPs: 9 July 2027. SEPA Instant mandatory for all EU PSPs offering SCT: 9 January 2025 (receiving) / 9 October 2025 (sending). Non-euro EEA: 9 July 2027. SEPA IPR annual report: annual obligation starting from first full calendar year of obligation.

T258 · Original Tool
Intraday Credit Facility Sizer
24/7 instant settlement requires a pre-funded or central-bank-backed intraday credit facility — liquidity must be available at all times, not just during business hours. Sizes the required facility based on peak-hour payment volumes, VoP match rates from step 2 (mismatch rejection rates affect net settlement flow), participant tier, and stress assumptions. Produces a recommended facility floor with Basel III LCR interaction analysis.
