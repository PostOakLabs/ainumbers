How this chain runs. Each stage is a v0.4 OpenChainGraph tool: call over MCP or in-browser, capture execution_hash, pass as next stage's parent_hashes. Terminal artifact carries full provenance and is exportable (pdf / xlsx / csv) via export_artifact.
Stage 1 (ART-63, Wave 14) is the metering and marketplace economics modeler — no prior chain covers agent-service unit economics. Stage 2 (art-03, Wave 6 pre-trade) modeled x402 V1 settlement cost for a single payment in pre-trade analysis; here it grounds ART-63's batch-settlement cost assumptions by applying the underlying settlement-finality model — the same cost mechanics, contextualized by the metering layer's aggregate volume. Stage 3 (ml-03) performs timeseries anomaly detection; here it monitors the usage and billing stream of the metered service for velocity spikes or charge anomalies — a runtime billing-control function, not a pre-trade ML scoring task. All economics figures are educational estimators — verify x402 V2 cost mechanics against current Linux Foundation x402 Foundation primary sources. Not pricing or financial advice.
