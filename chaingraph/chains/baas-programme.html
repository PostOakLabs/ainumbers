BaaS Programme Design Chain

Three embedded finance paths in one chain. Scenario A — Greenfield Launch: select and score BaaS providers (T152) → build a fee schedule validated against Reg E and CFPB UDAP (RBE-08) → score sponsor-bank readiness (T162) → export an AP2 BaaS Policy Mandate (T164). Scenario B — Provider Migration: provider comparison (T152) → credit policy risk-tier re-segmentation (RBE-09) → settlement orchestration and FBO reconciliation modelling (T157) → AP2 mandate (T164). Scenario C — Card Programme Addition: provider assessment (T152) → card programme launch readiness across PCI DSS, 3DS, and scheme certification (T163) → interchange qualification and least-cost routing (RBE-13) → AP2 mandate (T164).

T152 · BaaS Provider Comparator
BaaS Provider Comparator
Score and rank BaaS platform providers across 8 decision dimensions: regulatory coverage, FBO structure quality, ledger architecture, fee schedule flexibility, API maturity, sponsor-bank relationships, programme management support, and AP2 compatibility. Generates a weighted vendor selection decision matrix with per-provider risk flags and an AP2 compatibility score. For Greenfield launches, this is the foundation — choose the wrong provider and you inherit their regulatory debt. Output: ranked vendor scorecard with AP2 compatibility assessment and deal-structuring red flags.

RBE-08 · Fee Schedule Engine
Fee Schedule Engine
Build and validate the programme fee schedule for your chosen provider — monthly maintenance fees, transaction fees, ATM fees, and pass-through interchange — against CFPB UDAP/UDAAP unfair-practice standards and Reg E disclosure requirements. Models interchange revenue at transaction volume tiers, calculates pass-through pricing economics, and flags any fee structures that could constitute unfair, deceptive, or abusive acts or practices. For Greenfield: use the T152 provider's fee schedule as input. Output: validated fee schedule, UDAP risk assessment, annual percentage yield (APY)/fee disclosure requirements, and interchange revenue model.

A3
T162 · Sponsor Bank Readiness Scorer
Sponsor Bank Readiness Scorer
Score the sponsor-bank relationship embedded in your chosen provider across credit risk, settlement risk, and compliance risk vectors. Identifies contract clauses that create operational risk (unilateral termination, pass-through liability, indemnity scope), models the financial exposure of a sponsor-bank switch mid-programme, and benchmarks the sponsor's BSA/AML oversight against post-Synapse OCC guidance. For Greenfield: confirms that the chosen provider's sponsor bank can actually support the intended programme economics from your T152 selection. Output: sponsor-bank risk score, contract red-flag register, and switching cost model.

B1
T152 · BaaS Provider Comparator
BaaS Provider Comparator
Compare your current BaaS provider against alternatives across 8 decision dimensions, with a migration-specific lens: data portability, customer account migration path, ledger continuity, settlement rail compatibility, and regulatory change-in-control obligations. For migrations, the primary output is not just who to migrate to, but whether migration is structurally feasible without breaching existing programme agreements or triggering regulatory notification requirements. Output: migration feasibility matrix, switching triggers and blockers, and candidate provider shortlist with AP2 compatibility scores.

B2
RBE-09 · Credit Policy Decision Table
Credit Policy Decision Table
Build a credit policy decision table for the migration — re-segmenting the existing customer base into risk tiers under the new provider's underwriting infrastructure. Covers loss rate assumptions by risk tier, UDAAP stress-test scenarios for adverse action communications, and the credit policy delta between current and target provider. Identifies which credit product configurations will require regulatory reconsideration (e.g. NSF fee structure, overdraft, credit line limits) during the migration window. Output: risk-tier segmentation table, loss rate assumptions, UDAAP stress scenarios, and credit policy change log.
RBE-09 Consumes: T152 migration feasibility Risk-tier segmentation · UDAAP Loss rate assumptions Feeds: T157 settlement orchestration
Open RBE-09 ↗
B3
T157 · Settlement Orchestration Simulator
Settlement Orchestration Simulator
Model the settlement timing gap, intraday liquidity exposure, and FBO reconciliation complexity during the migration window — the period where customer funds may be in-flight across two providers simultaneously. Simulates nostro balance requirements at peak migration velocity, FBO sub-ledger reconciliation across old and new sponsors, and the intraday credit line requirements to bridge settlement timing differences during the switchover. Identifies high-risk migration dates (payroll settlement days, end-of-month peak) and recommends sequencing to minimise concurrent liquidity exposure. Output: migration liquidity model, FBO reconciliation framework, and high-risk date register.

⬡ Scenario C · Card Programme Addition · T152 → T163 → RBE-13 → T164

T152 · BaaS Provider Comparator
BaaS Provider Comparator
Assess whether your current BaaS provider can support a card programme addition — evaluating BIN sponsorship capabilities, scheme (Visa/Mastercard) certification relationships, 3DS 2.x infrastructure, card management system (CMS) architecture, and Durbin/Reg II compliance for debit card programmes. For existing BaaS customers adding card issuance, this validates whether a new provider is needed or whether the existing sponsor can extend coverage. Output: card-capability assessment per provider, BIN sponsorship feasibility, scheme certification timeline, and AP2 compatibility score for card operations.

C2
T163 · Card Programme Launch Readiness Checker
Card Programme Launch Readiness Checker
Score card programme launch readiness across the five operational dimensions required before going live: PCI DSS scope assessment (tokenisation, vault, SAQ level), 3DS 2.x integration and authentication exemption strategy, BIN sponsorship agreement status and scheme rules compliance, scheme certification timeline (Visa/Mastercard programme certification, principal membership vs. affiliate model), and Durbin/Reg II interchange routing compliance. Identifies which dimensions have launch blockers versus launch risks. For an addition to an existing programme: also assesses the operational impact on the current programme. Output: readiness score per dimension, launch blockers, risk register, and certification timeline.

C3
RBE-13 · Interchange Qualification Engine
Interchange Qualification Engine
Map your projected transaction mix to the most advantageous interchange rate categories across Visa, Mastercard, and Amex — qualifying transactions to the lowest-cost interchange rate category consistent with Reg II routing requirements and scheme rules. Builds least-cost routing (LCR) rule sets, models the interchange revenue impact of different card product configurations (credit vs. debit, signature vs. PIN, regulated vs. unregulated), and identifies the debit routing network selection strategy under Durbin. Consumes T163's transaction mix assumptions and T152's provider routing capabilities. Output: interchange qualification matrix, LCR rule set, interchange revenue model, and Durbin routing compliance assessment.
