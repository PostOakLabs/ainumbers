Corporate Treasury Statement Reconciliation

Note (dated 2026-08-04): a chain composes independent computations performed in an analytical order. No step's output is carried into the next step by the execution model; each step computes from its own declared inputs. This is true of every chain on this site, including chains whose per-step copy below reads as a data handoff.
Reading mode (RUNBOOK): Run these steps in order. The forecast-accuracy step scores forecasts against the actual amounts the reconciliation step just produced, so reconciliation has to run first.

Linear two-step chain for TMS straight-through reconciliation. Step 1 classifies ISO 20022 camt.053 BkTxCd entries (Domain/Family/SubFamily), validates the OPBD+Σ=CLBD balance equation, and scores structured-remittance match rate against the CGI-MP camt.053 Usage Guide v5.0. Step 2 scores AFP Cash Forecasting Survey 2024 MAPE and directional bias of the treasury cash forecasts against the reconciled actual transaction amounts, closing the TMS forecast-vs-actual cycle. No personal data is processed.

OpenChainGraph · 2 Steps analytics mandate · Linear Hash-Anchored §4 chain_depth:2 Client-Side · Zero PII
Not accounting, tax, or legal advice. Outputs are computed from your inputs and version-pinned reference tables only. Reconciliation outputs require review by qualified finance staff before use in financial statements. Cash forecast accuracy scores are for internal TMS benchmarking only.

1 ROOT node
camt.053 Statement Reconciliation art-258-parse-camt053-reconciliation
Classifies BkTxCd Domain/Family/SubFamily per ISO 20022 ExternalBankTransactionCode1Code registry 2023-03. Validates balance equation OPBD + Σ movements = CLBD. Scores structured-remittance match rate. Emits reconciliation_status (CLEAN/PARTIAL_MATCH/LOW_MATCH_RATE/FAILED_BALANCE), match_rate_pct, and per-bucket transaction counts. Output passed as actuals context to Step 2.
