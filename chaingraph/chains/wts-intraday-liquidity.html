How this chain runs. Standard v0.4 hash-chained execution. 24/7 tokenized settlement eliminates RTGS settlement windows, changing intraday liquidity profiles, HQLA requirements, and LCR/NSFR ratios. Exportable (pdf / xlsx) via export_artifact.
Stage 1 (504 settlement-risk-capital-optimizer): was built for Canton settlement-risk capital modelling in a single-network context; reframed here for a 24/7 TOKENIZED-CASH operating model — the driver is the collapse of netting windows under continuous settlement, which increases gross settlement exposure and changes capital requirements. Stage 2 (sim-01 lcr-nsfr-liquidity-stress-test): native LCR/NSFR liquidity stress — applies directly to the continuous-settlement scenario (no RTGS intraday liquidity recycling, higher average intraday exposures). Stage 3 (ml-03): native timeseries anomaly detection — monitors intraday liquidity-usage patterns for settlement-flow anomalies in a 24/7 rail. Cash/settlement-layer reframe: this chain models the money-side friction of switching from batch RTGS to continuous tokenized settlement — a distinct problem from the asset-leg collateral mobility chain (wts-collateral-mobility). Educational estimator — not a capital, regulatory, or liquidity advice.
