Bank Capital & Liquidity Chain

The Basel III framework (BCBS 189, 238, 295, 270, 309) requires banks to maintain minimum risk-weighted capital ratios (CET1, Tier 1, Total Capital), a Liquidity Coverage Ratio ≥100%, a Net Stable Funding Ratio ≥100%, and a Leverage Ratio ≥3% (G-SIBs: ≥3.5%). Pillar 3 public disclosure (KM1/OV1/LIQ1/LIQ2) anchors the reporting regime. Basel 3.1 (BCBS 424/561) finalises the output floor and standardised-approach constraints. This chain runs the full capital and liquidity adequacy cycle: RWA scenario modelling → LCR/NSFR stress simulation → leverage ratio → Basel 3.1 reporting delta → Pillar 3 disclosure tables. Three OpenChainGraph §4 promoted nodes generate verifiable capital-adequacy artifacts.

⚠ Key frameworks

Basel III LCR: BCBS 238 — minimum LCR ≥100% (phase-in complete globally). Basel III NSFR: BCBS 295 — minimum NSFR ≥100% (most jurisdictions implemented). Basel 3.1 (EU CRR3): Output floor fully phased to 72.5% by Jan 2030 under EU CRR3 — first disclosure obligations from Jan 2025 reporting periods. Pillar 3: BCBS 309 templates KM1/OV1/LIQ1/LIQ2 — quarterly reporting for G-SIBs. ECB/PRA SREP: Internal capital/liquidity adequacy assessments (ICAAP/ILAAP) align to the chain outputs.

Leverage Ratio Calculator
Calculate the Basel III Leverage Ratio (Tier 1 capital ÷ total exposure measure) against the 3% minimum (3.5% for G-SIBs). Total exposure measure covers: on-balance sheet items (at accounting value), SFT exposures, derivative exposures (replacement cost + PFE), and off-balance sheet items (credit conversion factors). Flag any ratio breach and quantify the Tier 1 capital shortfall or exposure reduction needed to restore compliance. The leverage ratio feeds the ART-07 Basel 3.1 delta step and the final Pillar 3 KM1 template.
