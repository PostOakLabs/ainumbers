How this chain runs. Standard v0.4 hash-chained execution. AML settlement note: wholesale tokenized settlement is allowlisted to verified LEI counterparties; open/KYC-tiered access is a compliance red flag (wts-audit-pack AML flag). Exportable (pdf) via export_artifact. 110-customer-risk-rating resolves via tools/110-customer-risk-rating.html (S3 path; same precedent as dtc-counterparty-aml).
Stage 1 (509 canton-party-allowlist-validator): was built for Canton party allowlist management; reframed here for CROSS-NETWORK SETTLEMENT PARTICIPANT eligibility — a deposit-token holder on the cash network AND a party on the asset network both need to pass LEI verification and sanctions screening. Stage 2 (110-customer-risk-rating): native use — risk-rates the participant using standard CRR methodology. Stage 3 (art-10 amla-transaction-typology-risk-scorer): native AML typology — applies directly to wholesale settlement flows (structuring, round-tripping, nocturnal settlement anomalies). Cash/settlement-layer reframe: participant onboarding for tokenized settlement is stricter than for Canton asset operations because the cash leg creates an immediate finality risk if a participant is not fully cleared. Educational tool — not sanctions, AML, or legal advice.
