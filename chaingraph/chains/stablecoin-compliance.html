Stablecoin Compliance Chain

The US GENIUS Act (Guiding and Establishing National Innovation for US Stablecoins) and EU MiCA (Markets in Crypto-Assets Regulation) create parallel but distinct reserve, attestation, and white paper obligations for stablecoin issuers. This chain covers both regimes end-to-end: CBDC architecture framing → GENIUS Act reserve attestation pre-check (ART-06, §4) → MiCA Monte Carlo reserve stress simulation (RCA-02, §4) → GENIUS Act payment stablecoin compliance checker → MiCA white paper builder. Two ChainGraph §4 promoted nodes generate hash-anchored reserve compliance artifacts.

⚠ Key deadlines

EU MiCA: Full application as of June 2024; EMT/ART issuers subject to reserve requirements (Art. 36), white paper obligations (Art. 17–19), and MiCA Title III/IV supervisory regime. US GENIUS Act: Enacted May 2025. Payment stablecoin issuers (Federal/State qualified) must maintain 1:1 reserve backing in cash, Treasuries ≤93 days, or Fed reserves; monthly attestation required; FDIC eligibility rules for custodians apply. White paper: MiCA requires CCA-approved white paper before any crypto-asset offering.
