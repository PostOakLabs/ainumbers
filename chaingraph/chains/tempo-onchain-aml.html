ART-38 · OpenChainGraph Promoted · screen_tip20_transfer_batch · chain_depth: 1
Tempo On-Chain AML & Travel Rule Screener
Screen a batch of TIP-20 transfers against OFAC/SDN lists and FATF Travel Rule requirements. Input: a transfer batch with originator and beneficiary address/memo fields. OFAC screening: flag addresses or 32-byte memo fields that resolve to SDN-listed entities. Travel Rule: for transfers at or above the $3,000 FATF threshold, confirm that originator name, account number, and address are present in the 32-byte memo or off-chain message field. Outputs: per-transfer screening results, a consolidated SAR determination, and a Travel Rule completeness attestation. Emits a §4 execution_hash referencing ART-37's TIP-403 verification.
