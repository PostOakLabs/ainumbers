⚠ ACH Fraud — Urgent deadline: 22 June 2026

Nacha Phase 2 (effective 22 June 2026) removes the $5M origination volume threshold from the credit-entry fraud monitoring rule. From that date: every RDFI, ODFI, Originator, Third-Party Sender (TPS), and Third-Party Service Provider (TPSP) must have written risk-based fraud monitoring procedures for ACH credit entries — regardless of volume. Failure to implement written procedures constitutes a Nacha Rules violation. Use Branch B now.

Isolation Forest Transaction Anomaly Detector

Outputs an anomaly score (0–1) per transaction, a threshold flag at your configured sensitivity, and a feature-importance attribution explaining which dimensions drove the anomaly flag. Exports a §4 hash-anchored anomaly detection artifact — the ML model output is verifiable and reproducible from the input parameters. APP Fraud Graph Simulator

APP Scam Risk Assessor
Score the APP scam reimbursement risk for a specific payment — the probability that the payment will result in a successful PSR/mandatory reimbursement claim against your firm. Factors: vulnerability score (ML-01 anomaly + T117 structuring), social engineering stage reached (MMS-03 graph), payment characteristics (amount, beneficiary history, payment purpose), and your firm's gross-negligence exemption evidence (SCA applied, fraud warnings shown, confirmation of payee). Output: reimbursement risk score, the key risk drivers, and the recommended pre-authorisation intervention (soft block, additional confirmation, human review). The terminal node of Branch A.
