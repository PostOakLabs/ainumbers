E-Invoicing ViDA Chain

The EU VAT in the Digital Age (ViDA) directive requires structured e-invoicing and Digital Reporting Requirements (DRR) across member states. France is the first mover — mandatory for large and medium enterprises from September 2026, SMEs from September 2027. This chain covers the full e-invoicing compliance workflow: ViDA DRR readiness → B2B compliance scoring → EN 16931 / Factur-X batch validation (ART-08, OpenChainGraph §4 artifact) → PEPPOL XML structure audit → ISO 20022 payment bridge.

⚠ Key deadlines

France (B2B e-invoicing mandate): Large & medium enterprises — September 2026. SMEs — September 2027. EU ViDA DRR: Member-state transposition underway; cross-border transactions in scope from 2030. PEPPOL BIS 3.0: Active procurement requirement across EU, Norway, Singapore, Australia. EN 16931: Core European standard for electronic invoicing — mandatory baseline for all ViDA-compliant e-invoices.

T174 · Original Tool
PEPPOL XML Auditor
Audit your PEPPOL BIS 3.0 XML invoice structure — namespace declarations, UBL/CII schema compliance, business-rule validation (PEPPOL-EN16931-R0xx rules), and access-point interchange requirements. ART-08 batch validation catches field-level EN 16931 failures; T174 catches PEPPOL-specific structural and business-rule failures that ART-08 doesn't cover.
