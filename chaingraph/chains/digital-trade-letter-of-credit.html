How this chain runs. Each stage is an OpenChainGraph v0.4 tool: call it over MCP or in-browser, capture its execution_hash, and pass it as the next stage's parent_hashes. The terminal artifact carries the full provenance chain and is exportable (xlsx / csv / pdf) via export_artifact.
Stage 2 (art-08 EN16931 e-Invoice Batch Validator) was built for EU e-invoicing; its invoice field/format validation engine applies natively to the commercial invoice within a digital LC presentation. Citations: ICC eUCP v2.1, eURC v1.1, URDTT v1.0. Educational rules-check tool, not a legal determination.
