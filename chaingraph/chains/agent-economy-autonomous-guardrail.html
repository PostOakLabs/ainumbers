Human-Not-Present Autonomous-Payment Guardrail

End-to-end gate for an agent paying with no human present. Establishes the spend-policy envelope under which HNP autonomy is permitted (art-02), validates the mandate's scope and freshness (art-01), then applies the HNP guardrail verdict to the executed payment (ART-62). This is the runtime runtime execution layer — distinct from the Wave 6 pre-trade policy simulation: here an agent has already paid and the chain confirms the autonomy was authorized. Implements AP2 v0.2 HNP semantics (FIDO Alliance, Apr 2026).

How this chain runs. Each stage is a v0.4 OpenChainGraph tool: call over MCP or in-browser, capture execution_hash, pass as next stage's parent_hashes. Terminal artifact carries full provenance and is exportable (pdf / xlsx) via export_artifact.
Stage 1 (art-02, Wave 6 pre-trade) simulates an agent's spend policy before a payment to determine what autonomy budget is allowed; here that simulation frames the runtime autonomy budget — establishing the envelope the HNP guardrail will enforce against an already-executed payment. Stage 2 (art-01, Wave 6 pre-trade) validates the AP2 mandate chain before a buy; here it confirms mandate freshness and scope at the moment the autonomous payment was executed — a post-trade mandate audit. Stage 3 (ART-62, Wave 14) is the terminal runtime judge: it verifies the actual PaymentReceipt and applies the HNP guardrail verdict. Together these three stages answer: did the spend policy permit this? was the mandate still valid? and was the receipt lawful? — the full autonomous-payment trust chain.
