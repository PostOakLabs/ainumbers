Agentic Checkout Chain

A three-node ChainGraph for teams building AI-agent-accessible checkout flows. Select the right payment protocol, audit your product-feed schema, and generate an agent-traffic acceptance policy — each step producing a §4 hash-anchored artifact that the next node can consume.

Chain Overview
Who this is for: Merchants, platform engineers, and fintech teams adding AI-agent checkout support (ACP, UCP, x402, Visa TAP).
What it produces: Three §4 hash-anchored ChainGraph policy artifacts — a protocol routing decision, a product-feed conformance report, and an agent-traffic acceptance mandate — that together form an auditable agent-commerce policy bundle.
How to use it: Run nodes in sequence. Export each §4 artifact. Pass ART-19's primary_protocol output into ART-20 as the protocol_target parameter. Use ART-21's exported mandate as your API's /agent-policy endpoint payload.
