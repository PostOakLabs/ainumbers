EUDI Wallet Acceptance Chain

Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 (eIDAS 2.0) requires all EU member states to offer an EUDI Wallet to every citizen by 31 December 2026 (Article 5a) and mandates that regulated financial institutions performing Strong Customer Authentication must accept EUDI Wallet credentials by approximately December 2027 (Article 5f). This chain covers the full acceptance workflow: attribute attestation mapping (PID + QEAAs) → KYC flow redesign for wallet-based onboarding → RP registration obligations → ART-13 credential-acceptance readiness check (§4) → overall EUDI readiness scoring and gap analysis.

eIDAS 2.0 · Regulation (EU) 2024/1183 ART-13 · OpenChainGraph §4 Artifact Art. 5a: Wallet available Dec 2026 Art. 5f: SCA acceptance ~Dec 2027 Client-side · Zero PII Wave B
⚠ Key deadlines

Art. 5a — EUDI Wallet availability: All EU member states must offer an EUDI Wallet to citizens and residents by 31 December 2026. Art. 5b — Acceptance by public authorities: Member state public bodies must accept EUDI Wallet credentials by early 2027. Art. 5f — Regulated FI SCA acceptance: Banks, payment institutions, and other regulated entities performing SCA must accept EUDI Wallet credentials by approximately December 2027. Relying Party registration: RP registration processes are being established by member states — start the registration runway now to avoid last-minute compliance risk.
