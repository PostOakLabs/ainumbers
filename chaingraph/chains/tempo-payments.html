Tempo Payments Business Case
Input your current payment rail mix — card interchange rates, wire fees, ACH batch costs, SWIFT correspondent banking fees — and annual volumes by corridor and currency. ART-35 models the cost savings from migrating to Tempo's payments-first L1: near-zero settlement latency, programmable compliance via TIP-403, and direct stablecoin settlement without correspondent intermediaries. Outputs: annual savings in $ and bps by rail; blended cost comparison; recommended migration sequence; payback period; and a §4 execution_hash-anchored CFO brief suitable for board presentation. Cost savings and rail recommendation feed downstream issuance (W-B) or agent mandate (W-C) chains.
