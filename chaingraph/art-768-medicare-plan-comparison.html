Medicare Plan Comparison

Prices two enrollment paths for a full year at a utilization level you set: Path A, Original Medicare plus a Medigap supplement plus stand-alone Part D, against Path B, Medicare Advantage. Both paths are priced for a typical year and for a bad-year stress test, with the Part D out-of-pocket cap and the Advantage maximum out-of-pocket limit applied. Client-side port of ApexLogics Apex #151 (AL-165).

Scope & reliance
This compares projected annual cost only. It does not compare networks, formularies, prior authorization rules, provider access or plan availability, and those often matter more than the dollar difference. Plan details vary by county. Confirm with the plan and with your State Health Insurance Assistance Program before enrolling. Source: 2026 CMS Parts A & B and Part D standard benefit parameters.

Shared assumptions

Both paths pay the same Part B premium; tiers 1–5 add the income-related adjustment and the Part D surcharge.
Days 1–60 are covered by the Part A deductible; 61–90 cost $434/day; 91–150 cost $868/day each and use the 60 lifetime reserve days; beyond 150 you pay all costs.
Medicare-approved amount: lab work, imaging, therapy, surgery centre.
Plan-negotiated retail cost for the year.

Path A – Original Medicare + Medigap + stand-alone Part D

Quote from your county. Varies widely by plan letter, age and state.
Pre-filled with the 2026 Part D base beneficiary premium, $35.09 (CMS: "from $35.09 in 2026 to $36 in 2027"). The KFF 2026 PDP average is $36. Actual premiums vary by plan and county.
Cannot exceed the 2026 maximum of $615 (G4).

Path B – Medicare Advantage

Some plans rebate part of the Part B premium.
In-network maximum out-of-pocket for 2026 may not exceed $9,250 and does not include Part D spending (G7). A value of 0 means "not entered": Path B medical cost sharing is shown uncapped.
