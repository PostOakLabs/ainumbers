Creative Commons License Chooser

Answer three questions and get the matching Creative Commons 4.0 license with its SPDX identifier, canonical deed URL, required elements, and attribution requirement. A deterministic decision tree: the same answers always resolve to the same published license. Kernel twin of T520, callable via MCP.

Creative Commons 4.0 SPDX Zero PII Zero Network Selection Only
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ This tool selects among standard Creative Commons licenses based on your answers. It does not draft custom terms and is not legal advice. The linked CC deed and legal code govern; consult a licensed attorney for your jurisdiction before relying on any output.
Presets:
Put the work in the public domain with no conditions. Yes returns CC0 and skips the remaining questions.
May others use the work for commercial purposes? No adds the NonCommercial (NC) condition.
May others remix or build upon the work? ShareAlike (SA) requires derivatives under the same license; None adds NoDerivatives (ND).
