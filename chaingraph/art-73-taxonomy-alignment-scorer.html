ART-73 · Wave 16 · Carbon Compliance · EU Taxonomy Alignment Scorer

EU Taxonomy Alignment Scorer

Scores an economic activity against the EU Taxonomy's three-gate test: Substantial Contribution (SC) to an environmental objective, Do No Significant Harm (DNSH) to the other five objectives, and Minimum Safeguards — all three required for alignment. Omnibus I revisions in force 28 Jan 2026. Part of the taxonomy-align, taxonomy-kpi, and eugb-conformance chains.

Omnibus I Taxonomy revisions in force 28 Jan 2026 6 environmental objectives SC + DNSH + Safeguards = Alignment
✅ IN FORCE: Omnibus I (Reg. (EU) 2025/XX) Taxonomy revisions entered into force 28 Jan 2026. Verify current technical screening criteria for the specific NACE code against up-to-date Delegated Acts before filing KPIs or green bond declarations.
