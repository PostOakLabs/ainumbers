Arc CPN Corridor Economics

Model the economics of migrating cross-border USD payment flows to Circle Payments Network (CPN) vs incumbent rails — SWIFT wire, ACH, SEPA, and card. Produces per-transaction savings, annualised savings in basis points, and a CFO settlement memo. Grounded in publicly disclosed industry fee structures and Circle Arc documentation.
