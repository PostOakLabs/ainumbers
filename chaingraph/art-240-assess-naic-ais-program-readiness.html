NAIC AIS Program Readiness Assessor

Scores insurance AI systems against the 6-dimension NAIC AI Model Bulletin (Dec 2020, updated 2023) and NAIC AI in Insurance (AIS) Evaluation Tool (2024). Each dimension scored 0–3 (0=not implemented, 1=early, 2=developing, 3=implemented). Total 0–18: GREEN ≥78% (14–18), YELLOW ≥44% (8–13), RED <44% (0–7). US standard; not mandated federally but forms the baseline for state-level AI governance examinations.
