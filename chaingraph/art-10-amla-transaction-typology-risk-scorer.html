AMLA Transaction-Typology Risk Scorer

Paste or generate a synthetic transaction graph and score it against six AML typology detectors: structuring (sub-threshold splitting), layering (rapid re-transfer chains), funnel accounts (many-to-one aggregation), round-tripping (funds returning to origin), high-velocity (burst activity), and FATF Travel Rule gaps (missing originator/beneficiary info on cross-border transfers). Each transaction receives a composite risk score 0–100. Aligned with EU AMLR (full application July 2027) and FATF Recommendation 16.
