GENIUS Act Reserve-Disclosure Conformance Monitor

Checks a monthly reserve disclosure against two statute-derived GENIUS Act S.394 §4 requirements: 1:1 reserve coverage arithmetic and attestation presence/timeliness against the statutory monthly cadence. Verdict per requirement: MET / NOT_MET / INDETERMINATE. Cross-links: pre-issuance Reserve Attestation Pre-Check and the fuller Monthly Reserve Disclosure Checker (asset composition and dual-control certification, statute-restatement pending final rule).

GENIUS Act S.394 §4 Narrowed scope No final implementing rule (as of 2026-08-07)
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope: two checks only, not a compliance certification
This monitor does not check permitted-asset composition. No final GENIUS Act implementing regulation exists as of 2026-08-07 to check the reserve-asset list against (every OCC/FDIC/Treasury/FinCEN/NCUA instrument remains NPRM/ANPRM, and the statutory 2026-07-18 rulemaking deadline was missed). It checks only 1:1 coverage arithmetic and attestation presence/timeliness, both derivable directly from statute. A MET verdict is never "compliance certified" language, and all thresholds should be re-verified against final rule text once one publishes.
