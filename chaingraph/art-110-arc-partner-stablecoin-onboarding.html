Arc Partner-Stablecoin Onboarding Conformance

Score a non-USD stablecoin issuer's readiness to join Circle Partner Stablecoins on Arc. Grades A–F across technical/operational, reserve-management, and risk-management dimensions. Optional Ed25519 §16 proof binds the conformance attestation. Distinct from USDC issuer / GENIUS Act path.
