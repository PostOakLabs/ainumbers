ERC-1967 Proxy Slot Classifier

Recomputes the four canonical EIP-1967 storage slots fresh on every run (keccak256 of a fixed label, minus 1), then classifies a pasted (declared_slot, storage_value) pair: which role the slot matches, if any, and whether the value looks like the zero-padded address EIP-1967 expects there. Zero network calls: never reads live chain state.

Slots recomputed fresh, not hardcoded EIP-55 checksum on any embedded address Address/slot fact only: no upgrade-safety judgment
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope: address/slot fact, never a safety judgment
declared_slot and storage_value are both values YOU read from your own eth_getStorageAt call or a block explorer; this tool never fetches them and cannot confirm they are current. A recognized slot with an address-shaped value says nothing about whether that implementation, admin, or beacon address is safe, audited, or behaves correctly.
