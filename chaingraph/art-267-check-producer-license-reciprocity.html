Producer License Reciprocity Checker

Checks NAIC producer license reciprocity for non-resident filing across target states. Identifies non-standard states requiring independent filing or state exams: CA, FL, NJ, NY, HI, MN, WI. Per NAIC Producer Licensing Model Act (MDL-218) and NIPR Reciprocity Matrix 2024. Accepts state codes and standard NAIC LOA codes only. ZERO PII: never real NPN, SSN, or PDB data.

Revenue Operations NAIC MDL-218 Producer Licensing Reciprocity Proofs Deferred
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. State codes and LOA enum codes only. Never real NPN, SSN, or PDB data.
Licensing Parameters
Producer's home state of residence. E.g. OR, TX, WA.
Standard NAIC Lines of Authority codes. E.g. L,H,A for Life, Health, Accident. Valid codes: L H A LTC VI CV P C HO CA LA MV WC SP PH FM OR SB MR PL LH PC VL.
States where non-resident license is sought. E.g. TX,IL,CO,NY.
