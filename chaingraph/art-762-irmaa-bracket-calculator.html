IRMAA Bracket Calculator

Given a tax filing status, the look-back-year adjusted gross income and tax-exempt interest, and the Medicare enrollment facts, this node computes modified adjusted gross income (MAGI), the IRMAA band, the resulting 2026 Part B total monthly premium and Part D income-related monthly adjustment amount, the dollars of headroom before the next cliff, and the effect of additional income under consideration.

Scope & reliance
Premium year is an input; the node reads no clock. Band edges follow the CMS band semantics: every threshold except the last is an exclusive edge (MAGI exactly ON it is still the lower band) and the last threshold is an inclusive edge (CMS: “Greater than or equal to $500,000”). Money is integer USD cents. Full Part B coverage only; SSA makes the final determination, this is a planning estimate. Kernel constants carry the 2026 CMS tables; see CITES.md and DIFFERS.md for the fetch-pinned provenance.
