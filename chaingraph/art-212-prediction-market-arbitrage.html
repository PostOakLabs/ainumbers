Prediction Market Arbitrage

Calculate cross-venue arbitrage: gross spread, fee-adjusted net edge, capital required, and the minimum spread needed to survive fees. A 5% gross spread on Polymarket/Kalshi yields roughly 3% net after Kalshi's parabolic taker fee.

Polymarket Kalshi SX Bet Robinhood Not Financial Advice
Not financial advice. Arbitrage opportunities are transient and may disappear before execution. Verify current prices and fees with each venue. This tool does not access live prices or execute orders.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Best ask for the YES side. E.g. $0.45 per $1 contract.
Best ask for the NO side. E.g. $0.50 per $1 contract.
