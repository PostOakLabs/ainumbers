Prediction Market Arbitrage

Calculate cross-venue arbitrage: gross spread, fee-adjusted net edge, capital required, and the minimum spread needed to survive fees. A 5% gross spread on Polymarket/Kalshi yields roughly 3% net after Kalshi's parabolic taker fee.
