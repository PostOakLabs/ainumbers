QM APR-APOR Spread Classifier

QM APR-APOR spread classification per Reg Z §1026.43(e)(2)(vi) and §1026.43(b)(4). Determines general QM eligibility, safe-harbor vs rebuttable-presumption status, and whether the loan is a Higher-Priced Covered Transaction. Requires QM points-and-fees pass (ART-218) as prerequisite.
