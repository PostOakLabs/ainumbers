GPAI Code of Practice Conformance

Checks GPAI provider obligations under EU AI Act Art. 53 (technical docs, training-data summary, copyright policy, model card) and Art. 55 systemic-risk obligations (risk eval, adversarial testing, incident reporting, cybersecurity). Also checks voluntary Code of Practice sign-up. Middle node of the ai-governance-framework-crosswalk chain. Zero network.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope
Middle node of ai-governance-framework-crosswalk chain (art-174→175→176). GPAI (General-Purpose AI) obligations under the EU AI Act became enforceable Aug 2 2025; Commission enforcement started Aug 2 2026. Art. 53 applies to all GPAI providers; Art. 55 adds systemic-risk obligations for models trained on >10^25 FLOPs or designated as systemic-risk. The voluntary Code of Practice provides a compliance route. Feeds readiness diagnostic (art-176).
