OpenChainGraph Suite · ART-523 · Identity & KYC Evidence

Identity-Proofing Assurance Level Evaluator

Rates whether a declared identity-evidence set reaches a declared target level of a caller-supplied, versioned assurance-level framework — the same policy-input pattern as the Collateral Haircut Engine's supervisory table. Never a hardcoded framework: swap the level definition below for NIST 800-63-3, eIDAS LoA, or any other structurally expressible framework without touching this page's logic. A criterion the definition cannot express (no required evidence type, no minimum strength) is flagged as an insufficient definition, kept separate from a criterion the evidence fails to meet.

Caller-supplied framework (policy input)Zero identity dataDeclared evidence, not verified identity
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. Evidence items are TYPES and STRENGTHS only — never document numbers, images, biometric templates, or any person-level value.
