Ethereum Validator Queue and Sweep Wait

Works out when one validator’s exit, consolidation, activation and withdrawal sweep land, from the Electra consensus specification’s balance-based churn rules and the queue state you declare. Every figure is a single integer division, with no loop over the queue.

Electra consensus spec Balance-based churn Declared inputs only
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ The wait follows from the queue state you declare and the published rules. It is not a forecast, reads no chain, and states nothing about any live network. The activation wait is marked as a lower bound when small deposits ahead let the 16-deposit limit set the pace, and the sweep wait is a lower bound whenever withdrawals sit ahead of the target. The activation figure assumes no Eth1 bridge backlog and no deposits ahead that belong to exiting validators, and holds the churn at its current value.
Inputs
Gwei amounts above 2^53 go in as decimal strings. withdrawal_credential_prefix is 0, 1 or 2. The exit, consolidation, activation and sweep sections are each optional; leave one out and its figures come back as null. seconds_per_slot is optional too; without it no wall-clock figure is returned. The sample is a 2048 ETH compounding validator with all four sections filled in.
