Arc StableFX RFQ Model

Quantify Herstatt principal risk exposure under current FX settlement and model Arc StableFX RFQ spread savings vs CLS settlement windows. Arc StableFX is Circle's 24/7 on-chain FX engine — providing continuous atomic PvP settlement to eliminate Herstatt risk per PFMI Principle 12. Client-side. Deterministic. Zero PII.
