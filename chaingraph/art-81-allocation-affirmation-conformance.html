ART-81 · Settlement Discipline · Allocation and Confirmation Evidence

Allocation / Affirmation Conformance Checker

Classifies allocation, confirmation, receipt-confirmation and settlement-instruction events against Commission Delegated Regulation C(2026) 4640 final of 2026-07-06, which applies from 2026-12-07, and against the UK T+1 Code of Conduct as a second declared regime. Every deadline comes from inputs you declare: this page reads no clock and resolves no timezone database. Part of the settlement-discipline-alloc-affirm chain.

Applies 2026-12-07 · 23:00 CET on trade date Structured-format mandate, amended Art. 2(1) Receipt confirmation within two hours, amended Art. 2(2) UK-TCC SETT 01 · 23:59 UK time
Rule version c(2026)-4640-final, adopted 2026-07-06. Applicability split: amended Art. 2(1) to (3) and the replaced Art. 3 retail rule from 2026-12-07; Art. 1(3)(a) instruction-field changes from 2027-07-01; new Art. 5(5) settlement-instruction timing from 2027-10-11. Events dated before 2026-12-07 are classified under the rule in force before the amendment and labelled as such; the amended rule is never applied early. Clause digests are pinned in chaingraph/standard/clause-snapshot-registry.json.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Run parameters (caller-declared)
The regulation states "23:00 CET" and never defines CET against CEST, so the reading is yours to declare and is printed with every result. Business days are Monday to Friday; no public-holiday calendar is applied.
Events
Timestamps must be ISO-8601 with an explicit offset, for example 2026-12-07T22:30:00+01:00. A timestamp without an offset is reported as malformed rather than guessed. Leave the event type blank to skip a row.
