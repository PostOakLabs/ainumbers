Caregiver Income Impact

The financial cost of unpaid caregiving: direct income loss while out of the workforce, the retirement savings gap from missed 401(k) contributions and their lost compounding, the cost of a post-care career setback, and a flagged Social Security estimate — plus a total. Scope & reliance
Not a benefit projection and not tax advice. as_of is an input and the node reads no clock: the SSA contribution and benefit base and the SSA average annual benefit are selected from a pinned vintage table by the as_of year, and an unpinned year is a refusal rather than a carry-forward. The Social Security term is a flagged linearisation (ART761_SS_ESTIMATE_MODELED) — it is not the SSA AIME formula; use art-282-social-security-claiming-optimizer or pf-139-social-security-bridge for a real benefit estimate. The career-setback compounding rate (2%), the caregiving-year divisor (35), the 15% cap and the 20-year draw are modelling constants carried from the source and each marked UNGROUNDED. marginal_tax_rate_pct is accepted and echoed but enters no formula — the source reads it and never uses it. Money is integer USD cents; the rounding happens once at the payload edge.
