FR Y-14 Capital Worksheet Roll-Forward & Cross-Check

Rolls a caller-declared FR Y-14A/Q capital worksheet (CET1, additional Tier 1, Tier 2) forward from a beginning balance through period additions, deductions, and a caller-declared published-scenario adjustment to an ending balance, then cross-checks the ending total capital against a caller-declared reference figure from another schedule (e.g. ART-436 Schedule HC-R) within a caller-declared tolerance.

FR Y-14A/Q Capital Roll-Forward Published-Scenario Adjustment Not A Filer — Evidence Only
🔒 All computation runs locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Reporting entity
Beginning balances (USD)
Additions (USD)
Deductions (USD)
Published-scenario adjustment (caller-declared)
Enter the delta figure from a published scenario (e.g. Federal Reserve DFAST/CCAR severely-adverse), NOT a firm capital-planning model output. This tool applies the figure you supply; it does not derive it.
Cross-check (caller-declared reference)
Reference figure from another schedule (e.g. Schedule HC-R total capital). Ending total capital is compared against it within the tolerance.
