License Terms Assembler

Fills a pre-approved license term template from structured inputs and renders plain-text and HTML output. Choose from three fixed templates: CC Standard Use, IP3 Rights Record, and NFT Embedded License. No bespoke drafting. Selection only.

Compliance Mandate Fixed Templates Only Plain Text + HTML Output Not Legal Advice
Not legal advice. This tool fills fixed, pre-approved templates only. No bespoke drafting. The published license texts govern all rights. Consult a licensed attorney for your jurisdiction.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
1. Choose Template
CC-STANDARD-USE
Creative Commons standard use grant with attribution. Markdown template, plain-text render.
IP3-RIGHTS-RECORD
Structured IP rights record with licensor, licensee, territory, royalty, renewal, and rights vector summary.
NFT-EMBEDDED-LICENSE
On-chain NFT embedded license: collection, token ID, tier, commercial/display/sharing rights.
