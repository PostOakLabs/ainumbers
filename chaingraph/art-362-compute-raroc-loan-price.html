RAROC Loan Pricing Calculator

Risk-Adjusted Return on Capital loan pricing per Basel II BCBS 128 (2006) / Basel III BCBS 189 (2010), a simplified public approximation of the IRB economic-capital formula (single-factor Vasicek model at 99.9% confidence) or the SA risk-weight bucket table. Returns RAROC versus hurdle rate, the net income waterfall, and the break-even spread. Provable version of tools/437-raroc-loan-pricing.html and tools/203-raroc-loan-pricing-model.html.

Basel II BCBS 128 Basel III BCBS 189 AIRB / FIRB / SA
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ Simplified public approximation of the IRB economic-capital formula. Not a substitute for an internally approved IRB model.
