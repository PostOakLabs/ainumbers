Private Student Loan Disclosure & Rescission Checker

Checks a private-education-loan disclosure-element checklist across the application, approval, and final stages (12 CFR 1026.46-48), the HEOA self-certification presence requirement, and computes the 3-business-day right-to-cancel window from a declared final-disclosure date.

12 CFR 1026.46-48 HEOA Self-Cert Collections Compliance Family
🔒 All checking runs locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real consumer data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ SCOPE NOTE: Federal Income-Driven Repayment (IDR) calculations are explicitly OUT OF SCOPE: IDR formulas churn on a policy cycle distinct from the stable 1026.46-48 element/timing requirements checked here.
