Workload Identity Claims

Lints claims you paste against a policy you declare. It does not verify signatures, does not contact an identity provider and does not prove the token is genuine. Paste decoded claims only: anything shaped like a compact signed token is refused.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

What it checks

    
Subject is a well-formed workload identifier in an allowed trust domain; audience present and matching; expiry present and within your declared lifetime; issued-at and not-before sane against the clock you supply; actor chain depth and each actor an identifier; authorized-actor consistency; for an exchange, scope a subset of the subject scope and audience or resource not wider. A claim the policy needs but the input lacks gives INDETERMINATE, never a guess.
