Agent Spend-Policy Simulator

Author a spend policy — merchant allowlists, per-transaction and rolling limits, HNP autonomous-flow rules — then simulate up to 5,000 synthetic agent transactions against it in seconds. Detects scope creep (unauthorized merchants/categories), drip-pattern policy bypass (sequences of sub-limit transactions that cumulatively exceed daily or monthly caps), and unauthorized HNP flows. Paste an ART-01 artifact to anchor into the chain. Exports a full transaction log as CSV.

AP2 Export Chains: ART-01 → ART-02 GPU Parallel Bypass Detection Zero PII · Synthetic Only
🔒 All simulation runs locally. All transactions are fully synthetic. No data leaves your browser. Paste ART-01 artifact JSON only — no real payment data.
