In-House Bank Interest Allocation

Allocates overnight interest for notional pooling (pro-rata on pool net balance) or zero-balance account (ZBA) sweep structures. Applies ACT/360, ACT/365, or 30/360 day-count. Computes per-member gross interest, withholding tax deduction, and net interest payable. OECD transfer-pricing arm’s-length rate per OECD TP Guidelines 2022 Chapter X (intra-group financial transactions). Produces an auditable IHB interest statement.

Corporate Treasury In-House Bank OECD Transfer Pricing Notional Pooling / ZBA Proofs Deferred
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. This tool accepts member IDs, balances, rates, and withholding rates only — no account holder or personal data.
Pool Parameters
Arm's-length overnight / overnight-indexed rate for the pool.
Number of calendar days in the accrual period.
Pool Members (JSON Array)
Array of {member_id, balance, withholding_rate_pct (0-100)}. Positive balance = depositor; negative = borrower. Withholding rate: 0 for domestic, e.g. 15 for 15% WHT.
