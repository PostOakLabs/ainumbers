Servicing Compliance Clock

Computes servicer-compliance due dates and clock states from the event dates and loan values you type in: error-resolution and information-request clocks, loss-mitigation acknowledgment and evaluation, the 37-day pre-sale boundary, the 120-day pre-foreclosure bar, no-duplicate processing, force-placed-insurance notice, reminder, cancel and refund clocks, payment-crediting and payoff-statement deadlines, and ARM notice windows with their exception branches. Every obligation carries its paragraph citation and a named business-day calendar assumption. Dates that are invalid or ambiguous, rescinded rule branches, and over-bound event sets are refused and never computed.
