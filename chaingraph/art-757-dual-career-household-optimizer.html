Dual-Career Household Optimizer

Three coupled legs for a two-career household: what each partner's childcare break-even salary is, what a sabbatical truly costs over ten years, and whether a relocation pays once cost of living is counted. Set the inputs, run.

Scope & reliance
Tab 1 prices childcare into each partner's after-tax contribution: break-even gross = annual childcare total / (1 − combined effective tax rate/100). Tab 2 prices a sabbatical as direct lost pay, lost employer match on it, and a 10-year compounding opportunity cost of the reduced contribution track net of the direct loss already counted. Tab 3 restates the destination household in origin dollars (origin COL / dest COL) and applies the page's 3-months-per-$10k re-employment break-even heuristic. A tax rate at or above 100%, a zero-current household or a zero destination COL returns null legs with a DIV_ZERO_GUARD flag instead of the page's Infinity/NaN. The city cost-of-living index is the Apex page's own select table (UNGROUNDED, user-overridable); the BLS CES 2024 banner vintage is informational only; no numeric vintage constant enters the arithmetic.
