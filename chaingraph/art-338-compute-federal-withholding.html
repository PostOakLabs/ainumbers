OpenChainGraph Suite · ART-338 · US Payroll Compliance

Federal Withholding Calculator

Federal income tax withholding via the IRS Publication 15-T (2025) percentage method, Worksheet 1A, for a 2020-or-later Form W-4. Supports single/MFS, married filing jointly, and head of household, plus Step 3 dependents credit and Step 4(a)/(b)/(c) adjustments. Feeds ART-339 Gross-to-Net Payroll Calculator.

IRS Pub 15-T (2025) Worksheet 1A Percentage Method Federal Only
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ Federal withholding only. Not tax advice. State and local withholding are out of scope for this tool. The Form W-4 Step 2 multiple-jobs checkbox withholding table is not implemented in v1 — results assume the box is not checked.
