One workflow step by step

An AI shopping agent wants to spend USD 120 for you. Before it may, three small web pages each answer one question. Follow along to see where the work happens and what you keep at the end.

Goal of this animation

Show where a calculation runs when you use AINumbers. One AI shopping agent is checked by three small pages, and each page computes inside your browser and leaves a receipt you keep.

🔒 No personal dataYour inputs stay in your browser tab and are never sent or stored. Every value shown is synthetic.
☁ Our server is Cloudflare“AINumbers server” means Cloudflare today. It delivers the three page files once and takes no part in the checks.
Why every result here is green
● green · the check passed and the step may go ahead● amber · held, flagged for review or waiting on something● red · refused, or a mismatch was caught
ALL GREEN ON PURPOSEThis walkthrough follows one purchase that meets every rule, so all three checks pass. It is the simplest case, used to show where the work happens.
WHAT A NO WOULD LOOK LIKEHad the purchase been USD 620 against the USD 500 limit, art-02 would have answered over budget and the agent would have stopped. The no would still come back as a result with its own fingerprint.
WHERE TO SEE ONELevel 2 shows a double order being held. Level 3 shows a payment over its cap and an edited record being caught.

Try a fingerprint yourself

Each page stamps its result with a fingerprint, also called a hash. The same inputs always give the same fingerprint, and any change gives a completely different one. Edit a value below and watch.

This is a real SHA-256, computed in your browser.

Words used here

workflow
A few pages run in order, each feeding the next. This one has three.
page
A single web page that holds one calculation. It runs inside your browser tab.
fingerprint
A short code computed from the inputs and the result. Anyone can recompute it to check nothing changed.
receipt
The result plus its fingerprint. Receipts move between pages. Your inputs stay put.
inverted database
Usually your data goes up to a company's database. Here the pages come down to you, and you keep the receipts.

This is the "agent-identity-trust" workflow from the AINumbers ChainGraph: art-32, art-04 and art-02. The values are made up, and the fingerprints are real SHA-256 over the values shown.
