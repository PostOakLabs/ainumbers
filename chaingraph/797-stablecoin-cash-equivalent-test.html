Stablecoin Cash Equivalent Test (FASB Proposed 2026-08)

Applies the FASB proposed Update of August 18, 2026, "Statement of Cash Flows (Topic 230): Cash Equivalents - Disclosure Enhancement and Evaluation of Certain Digital Assets" (exposure draft, File Reference No. 2026-ED400), to the declared redemption and reserve terms of one stablecoin holding, criterion by criterion, and returns per-criterion verdicts, a collective classification, the disclosure checklist the text proposes, and a Policy Mandate export.

Declared terms only. Values you enter are processed client-side and are never uploaded. Do not enter personal data or identifiers; a token label longer than 64 characters or a non-boolean declared term is refused, never guessed.
Status: PROPOSED guidance. This node applies an exposure draft (issued 2026-08-18, comments due 2026-11-19). It is not authoritative until a final ASU is issued, and this page is not an accounting opinion: presenting a qualifying asset as a cash equivalent is an accounting policy election. No token is named here as qualifying, the node evaluates declared terms only.

Collective evaluation: The criteria are inherently interrelated and are evaluated collectively: the classification is MEETS_PROPOSED_EXAMPLES only when all six criteria are PASS; any FAIL gives DOES_NOT_MEET; any remaining NOT_DETERMINABLE (an absent declared term) gives NOT_DETERMINABLE. Absent terms are never guessed, and a declared term of the wrong type is refused.
