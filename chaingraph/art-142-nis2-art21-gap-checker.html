NIS2 Article 21 Gap Checker (Ten Cybersecurity Risk-Management Measures)

Assess compliance with NIS2 Article 21's ten mandatory cybersecurity risk-management measures (a–j). Score each measure by implementation status, documentation, and last test date. Receive an overall compliance score (0–100), grade (A–F), and prioritised remediation list.

Maturity scoring: 3=implemented+tested, 2=implemented only, 1=documented only, 0=absent. Overall score = sum of maturity scores / 30 × 100. Grade A≥90, B≥75, C≥60, D≥40, F<40.
