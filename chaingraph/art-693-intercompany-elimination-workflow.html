Intercompany Elimination and Netting Workflow

Runs the matching arithmetic of an intercompany elimination and netting workflow over caller-declared entity pairs: for each declared pair the receivable declared by entity a is compared against the payable declared by entity b (both rounded to 2 decimal places, half-up); equal amounts form a matched pair, unequal amounts are listed as a mismatch with their difference. The kernel reports matched and mismatched pair counts, the elimination total (sum of the smaller side of every pair), the unmatched residual (sum of mismatch differences), a full trace, and an overall GAPS_FOUND or ALL_MATCHED verdict. HARD FENCE: pure matching arithmetic over declared synthetic balances: not legal advice, not an audit opinion, not a settlement instruction; nothing is posted, netted, or paid anywhere, and no counterparty is contacted.
