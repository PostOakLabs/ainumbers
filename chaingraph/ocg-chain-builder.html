Why verifiability matters at audit time

In a legacy audit, a regulator receives a PDF that says "our VaR is $2.85M." To challenge it, they must request the underlying model, the input data, and the calculation methodology — three separate documents, often in three different departments. Under OCG, the execution_hash is a single identifier that simultaneously proves the inputs, the computation, and the output haven't been altered. The auditor runs one SHA-256 check, in their own browser, against the artifact. No document requests, no email chains, no version confusion.
