LCR / NSFR Liquidity Stress Simulator

Monte Carlo simulation of the Liquidity Coverage Ratio and Net Stable Funding Ratio under Basel III stress. Runs 1,000 stochastic paths × 250 daily time steps with configurable outflow-shock and HQLA-haircut volatility. Outputs P5–P95 percentile fan charts, breach probability, time-to-breach histogram, and a 6-factor sensitivity tornado identifying which inputs move your liquidity ratios most. GPU chunk-yield keeps the browser responsive throughout.

AP2 Export Chains: PTG-01 Monte Carlo · 1k paths Basel III LCR/NSFR GPU Chunk-Yield · Zero PII
🔒 Fully client-side. All simulation runs locally in your browser. No data leaves this page.

LCR (Liquidity Coverage Ratio) = HQLA ÷ Net Cash Outflows (30-day stress). Regulatory minimum: 100%.
NSFR (Net Stable Funding Ratio) = Available Stable Funding ÷ Required Stable Funding (1-year horizon). Regulatory minimum: 100%.
Each Monte Carlo path applies daily log-normal shocks to outflows, HQLA, ASF, and RSF. Scenario multiplier scales shock magnitude per Basel III Annex 2 and BCBS 238.
