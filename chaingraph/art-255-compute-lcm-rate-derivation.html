LCM Rate Derivation Calculator

Computes indicated rates from user-supplied loss costs using Loss Cost Multiplier (LCM) decomposition arithmetic per ASOP 25. Enter your own pure loss costs and loading components. This tool performs the LCM math only; it does not embed, store, or redistribute ISO/Verisk advisory loss costs or any proprietary rate-bureau filing.

ASOP 25 LCM Arithmetic SERFF Filing Support User-Supplied Loss Costs Only Proofs Deferred
⚠ PROPRIETARY DATA NOTICE: Enter only your own internally-derived loss costs. Do not enter ISO/Verisk advisory pure premium or loss cost rate pages — those are proprietary to Verisk Analytics and require a license to reproduce. This tool performs LCM decomposition arithmetic only on data you supply.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. This tool accepts aggregate rate components only — no policyholder or personal data.
Formula Reference
LCM = 1 / (1 - LAE% - fixed_exp% - variable_exp% - profit%)
indicated_rate = credibility_weighted_loss_cost × LCM
rate_change% = (indicated_rate / current_rate - 1) × 100
