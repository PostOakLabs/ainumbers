Bond Ladder Builder

Builds a target-weight bond ladder: a total notional split across maturity rungs, each rung priced as an even-period bullet bond. Reports per-rung price, Macaulay and modified duration, DV01 and convexity, plus the weight-weighted portfolio aggregates, and names every reinvestment assumption in plain sentences: hold coupons and maturing principal as cash at 0%, or roll them into the longest rung's yield. Rung weights must sum to 1 within 1e-9 or the tool refuses, naming the sum.
