AI Displacement Risk Screener

Automation-exposure score (0–100) from SOC task-composition data, with a risk tier and adjacent lower-exposure pivots. Select a job (or leave it custom), set the task sliders, run.

Scope & reliance
The score is a fixed-weight composite: 0.40 × automation probability + 0.40 × routine-task fraction (routine cognitive + routine manual over 100) + 0.20 × the selected sector exposure factor, clamped to 0..100 and rounded. A SOC code selects an occupation from the embedded 62-row table; a blank selection estimates the probability from the sliders (slope 0.85, floor 0.05, cap 0.99). The embedded probabilities are Frey-Osborne 2013-vintage estimates, the task splits are Apex-authored approximations, and the median-salary cells labelled BLS OES 2025 stayed ungrounded at porting time (BLS HTTP 403); salary figures are informational only and stay out of the score, with every vintage and grounding flag carried in the kernel metadata.
