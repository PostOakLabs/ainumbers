PoR Liabilities Composer

Composes an art-280 Reserve Proof Verifier inclusion result (soft-dep: a caller-restated inclusion_verified and computed_root.sum) with a caller-supplied aggregate liabilities total. Produces a reserve-to-liability ratio and a composite determination: consistent, inclusion failed, liabilities undercovered, or no liabilities figure supplied. Does not audit the liabilities figure's source.

Composes with ART-280 (soft-dep) Deterministic Ratio Arithmetic
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Composed PoR Inclusion Result (art-280, soft-dep)

Leave unset to see the stubbed-composition edge case: inclusion is treated as NOT verified when this is absent.
