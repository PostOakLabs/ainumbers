OpenChainGraph Suite · ART-769

Medicare Out-of-Pocket Exposure

Walks a modelled year of Medicare utilisation through the cost-sharing rules benefit period by benefit period, then reports what the coverage structure leaves you: the medical out-of-pocket, the drug cost sharing after the plan-year cap, premiums including IRMAA, and a routine / rough / worst-case spread.

RetirementInteger CentsNo ClockZero PIIClient-side only
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data; use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope & reliance
Parameters are an as-of question, so the plan year plan_year is an input and the node reads no clock: the same inputs always produce the same result. Every monetary quantity is an integer number of US cents; coinsurance is exact round-half-up integer division by 10000, so there is no floating-point rounding boundary anywhere in the arithmetic. The node charges what Medicare charges - the Part A deductible, the day 61-90 and lifetime-reserve coinsurance, SNF coinsurance on days 21-100, the Part B deductible and coinsurance, and the Part D deductible-then-coinsurance walk capped at the plan-year out-of-pocket threshold. It does not model Part B excess charges above the Medicare-approved amount, non-covered services, the Part D base premium, or the full cost of days after 150 in a benefit period or after 100 SNF days, and it does not count real visits or price any actual plan. Medigap Plan G and Plan N carry no annual ceiling here: the Part B deductible is only the residual they leave, not a cap. The worst-case multiplier (default 4) is a modelling assumption of this tool, not a CMS figure; you can change it below. Income tiers run 0-5 as in the CMS 2026 Part B fact sheet; any other tier is refused. Verify-only estimate - not an official CMS, SSA or plan communication, and not financial, legal or tax advice.
