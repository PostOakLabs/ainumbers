Scope & reliance
Liquidation-preference mechanics follow NVCA model term sheet conventions (fetched 2026-10-06): non-participating preferred takes the greater of its preference and its as-converted pro-rata share, converting only when the pro-rata share strictly exceeds the preference; full participating preferred takes the preference and then shares pro-rata; capped participating takes the preference plus pro-rata participation up to a cap expressed as a multiple of invested. Taxable gain is computed as max(0, net proceeds − cost basis) per IRC §1001 (fetched 2026-10-06). The preferred-wins warning is Apex #141’s own heuristic: no official threshold exists for it, so the kernel carries it as a flagged flag rather than a rule. The model is single-round and fully-diluted with a single preferred class; seniority stacks, option-pool top-ups, transaction fees, carry and taxes beyond the §1001 gain computation sit outside its scope, and the result is a planning estimate rather than a return.
