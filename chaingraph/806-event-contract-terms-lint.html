Event Contract Terms Lint

Screens a prediction-market event contract's declared settlement terms (which source decides, what happens if it is late or revised, whether the venue keeps discretion, how long a dispute stays open) against a policy you declare, and emits one PASS or FINDING line per policy item plus an overall verdict. Venue-neutral: inputs are enums, flags, counts and one opaque label only. Free text, URLs and anything identifier-like are refused and never echoed. This lint never says a contract is lawful, fair or compliant.

Policy Mandate Export Compliance Control compliance_control Proof deferred
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data: use synthetic or anonymised values only. This tool accepts enums, flags, integers and opaque labels only, and refuses free text, URLs and identifier-like values.
