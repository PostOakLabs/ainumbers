Privacy Coin Regulatory Classifier (MiCA and AMLR)

Place the properties you declare for a privacy-feature coin and for the entity handling it against two pinned provisions of the Official Journal text: Regulation (EU) 2023/1114 Article 76(3) and Regulation (EU) 2024/1624 Article 79(1). Each result cites its paragraph and the application date the text states. It is technical documentation, not legal advice.

MiCA Article 76(3) AMLR Article 79(1) Application Dates From The Text
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ This is a classifier over pinned text and not legal advice. It places the inputs you declare against the paragraphs; it does not decide whether a coin has an inbuilt anonymisation function or whether an account allows anonymisation or obfuscation, and it cannot verify your declarations. The Anti-Money Laundering Regulation paragraph is reported as not yet applicable before 10 July 2027 (10 July 2029 for obliged entities in Article 3(3) points (n) and (o)). Pinned from the Official Journal text on 3 November 2026.
Inputs
Each sample is one of the node's conformance fixtures. Pick one to load it, then edit the JSON below.
Keys: assessment_date (YYYY-MM-DD); coin_label (a short label, no personal data); entity {role, obliged_entity_art_3_3_n_or_o}; declared_properties {inbuilt_anonymisation_function, holders_identifiable_by_platform_casp, transaction_history_identifiable_by_platform_casp, account_allows_anonymisation_or_obfuscation}. Roles: casp_operating_trading_platform, casp_other, credit_or_financial_institution, other. Unknown fields are refused.
