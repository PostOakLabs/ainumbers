Scope & reliance — read before using the number
  • Not tax advice, and not a determination. The tax-free treatment of the housing stipend and M&IE is an assumption you supply, not something this node establishes. Excludability is a facts-and-circumstances question under IRC §162(a)(2) and Reg. §1.162-17; if it fails, the "tax-free" lines become ordinary taxable wages.
  • Zero is not zero. Every input is read as parseFloat(value) || default, exactly as the source tool does. A literal 0 — and any blank or non-numeric field — is falsy, so it reverts to the tool's default instead of computing as zero. Typing 0 into the housing stipend gives $1,200/week; typing 0 into the tax rate gives 22%. The Substituted defaults line in the output names every field this happened to.
  • Gap weeks never touch the travel net. gapWeeks enters only the separately reported opportunity cost, priced at the staff hourly rate. The travel net itself does not subtract unpaid weeks.
  • Benefits are excluded from the comparison. The winner test is travel net against staff net ex fringe, and the staff benefits value is reported but not compared. Benefits are not neutral between the two models.
  • No range validation. The source tool carries no bounds check in its arithmetic, so this node does not add one; negative and absurd values flow through and are flagged, not clamped.
  • No clock. Nothing here reads the date. The artifact envelope takes an explicit timestamp.
