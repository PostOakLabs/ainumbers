Travel Nurse vs. Staff Comp

True net-pay comparison of travel nursing against a permanent staff post: tax-free housing and M&IE stipends stacked against contract gap weeks, per-contract travel cost and out-of-pocket housing, plus the effective all-in travel hourly rate.

CompensationCaller-asserted tax treatmentNo rounding in payloadNo clock readZero PIIClient-side only
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data; use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

Scope and reliance: this node is not tax advice and determines nothing about the tax treatment of the housing stipend or M&IE; that treatment is an assumption you supply, and if it fails the tax-free lines are ordinary taxable wages (IRC §162(a)(2), Reg. §1.162-17). Two mechanical limits to know: every input is read as parseFloat(value) || default, so a literal 0 or blank field silently reverts to the tool default (the Substituted defaults line names each field), and the winner test compares travel net against staff net ex fringe, so the staff benefits value is reported but never compared.

Where the regulatory citations come from

    

      IRC §162(a)(2), travelling expenses while away from home in the pursuit of a trade or business
      (26 U.S.C. §162, law.cornell.edu, fetched 2026-10-15).

      Reg. §1.162-17(b)(4), a per diem in lieu of subsistence on a fixed scale may count as an
      accounting to the employer (law.cornell.edu, fetched 2026-10-15).

      GSA FY2026 CONUS standard rate: lodging $110/night, M&IE $68/day (master rates file fetched
      2026-10-15). Federal agency rates under 41 CFR ch. 301; not binding on a private
      staffing contract, and not the same as this tool's defaults. See CITES.md and DIFFERS.md.
