CSDR Penalty Recompute (Caller Reference Price)

Per-ISIN/day CSDR cash-penalty recompute over your declared open-fails set: selects the RTS asset-class/penalty-type daily rate (CSDR-RTS-2025-10, ESMA Final Report 13 Oct 2025), applies fail duration to your reference price and quantity, credits partial settlement proportionally, and sums forward penalty exposure across the fails set. Fixes ART-78's reachability defect: ART-78 priced off notional with no shipped pack able to feed it a differing reference price; this node's schema instead requires a caller-supplied reference_price per fail. ART-78 keeps its own identity and chain wiring unchanged. Emits a hash-anchored OpenChainGraph v0.4 artifact.

CSDR Reg. (EU) 909/2014 Art 7 ESMA RTS Final Report 13 Oct 2025 Caller-supplied reference_price SPEC.md §25 optional
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ ISIN and counterparty id are optionally private: supply isin_commitment / counterparty_id_commitment (a sha256-salted@1 commitment: sha256: + 64 hex chars, computed by you from a salt you generate and keep) instead of the plaintext field. This tool never receives your salt.
⚠ DECISION-SUPPORT DRAFT, not a regulatory penalty notice. Rate schedule CSDR-RTS-2025-10 only; only the 2025-10-13 ESMA Final Report date is corroborated; no further provision date is asserted here.
