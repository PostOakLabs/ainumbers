OpenChainGraph Suite · ART-165 · Supply-Chain Due Diligence

EUDR DDS Field Validator

Validates the 8 required Due Diligence Statement fields structurally before TRACES NT submission. Covers operator identity, EORI, HS code, trade name, quantity, country of production, and geolocation. Zero network.

EUDR 2023/1115 TRACES NT Mandatory 2026-12-30 Zero PII W3C VC §13.11 v0.5.0
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope
Root node of eudr-due-diligence-statement-validation chain (art-165→166→167). EUDR requires operators to file a DDS in TRACES NT before placing regulated commodities (cattle, cocoa, coffee, oil palm, rubber, soya, wood) on the EU market. This tool validates the 8 required DDS fields structurally before submission. §16 proof candidate (DDS attestation). Feeds geolocation validator (art-166). See eudr-due-diligence-statement-validation chain.
