Wash-Sale Window Guard

Applies the 61-day acquisition window of the declared wash-sale method (sale date minus 30 days through sale date plus 30 days, inclusive) to a declared lot sale and a declared replacement-purchase list: it totals the disallowed loss, flags a replacement account declared tax-deferred, and notes the basis carryforward on the flagged taxable path. The verdict reports the declared arithmetic only: WASH_SALE_FLAGGED or WASH_SALE_CLEAR. Undated lots fail closed. HARD FENCE: this is a deterministic calculator over declared numbers under a named rule; it is never advice, never an optimizer, and it renders no tax position on any real holding.
