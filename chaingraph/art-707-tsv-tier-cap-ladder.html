TSV Tier-Cap Ladder

Replays a tokenized-stock venue’s (TSV’s) daily per-symbol share volume and distinct-symbol counts, aggregated across the TSV and its declared affiliated TSVs, against the SEC TSV Exemption tier caps (FR 2026-19388, 91 FR 60174-76, condition F): Tier 1 is 75 symbols and 0.25 percent of the declared prior-month ADV, Tier 2 is 250 symbols and 2.5 percent. Applies the stepped-compliance ladder to volume breaches only: the first breach needs no action, every later breach forces an immediate three-month pause (affiliates included) whose window is emitted. Symbol-count breaches carry the order’s stated consequence but no pause, because the order states none. Evidence replay only: it never states that a venue is compliant.
