RFP Evidence Desk

Type in your tender's requirement lines, drop the OpenChainGraph artifacts and chain exports that answer them, and get a requirement-by-requirement evidence annex: each row shows its matched artifact, its verified hash and signature state, or an explicit "narrative response, no artifact" marker. Built for government-payments procurement, but the requirement categories are generic; nothing here names a jurisdiction, ministry, or scheme.

⚠ This page assembles artifacts you already hold; it does not parse or extract from your tender document, does not compute a coverage score, and does not host, monitor, or store any transaction data. Every input stays in this browser tab and is gone on reload.
