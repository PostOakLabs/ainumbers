ART-54 · Wave 12 · Digital Trade / eUCP / URDTT · W-B

Digital Trade Rules Compliance Checker

Machine-checks a digital trade presentation (digital LC, collection, or open-account transaction) against the ICC digital trade rulebooks — eUCP v2.1, eURC v1.1, or URDTT v1.0. Produces a discrepancy list with article citations, severity ratings, and remediation actions. Client-side, zero-egress; emits a hash-anchored OpenChainGraph v0.4 artifact.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
