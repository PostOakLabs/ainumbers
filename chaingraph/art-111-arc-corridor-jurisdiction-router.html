Arc Multi-Currency Corridor Compliance Routing

Route a multi-currency Arc corridor to its per-currency home regimes and validate cross-currency settlement and Travel Rule — compliance layer only, no FX economics. Supported: EURC (MiCA), JPYC (JP FSA), BRLA (BCB), MXNB (CNBV), AUDF (ASIC), PHPC (BSP), QCAD (FINTRAC), ZARU (SARB).

Arc Corridor Multi-Currency Zero PII Client-side only MiCA · JP FSA · BCB · CNBV · ASIC · BSP · FINTRAC · SARB iso20022:pacs.008-subset
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope
Compliance routing only — validates home regime mapping, disclousure gap detection, and emits PvP (511) + Travel-Rule (art-104) handoffs. No FX pricing, no liquidity economics. Use ART-106 for economics; ART-107 for paymaster modelling.
