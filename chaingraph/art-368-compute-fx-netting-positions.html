Multilateral FX Netting Calculator

Multilateral FX netting across up to 8 currencies: nets each currency’s payable/receivable exposures in FCY, converts to USD at a spot-plus-forward-points effective rate, and returns gross/net volume, netting efficiency, estimated settlement savings, and per-currency residual position with an approximate 95%-confidence VaR. Provable version of tools/105-fx-netting-simulator.html.
