BoLD Challenge-Window Finality Classifier

Robinhood Chain is Arbitrum Orbit with BoLD interactive fraud proofs. Settlement inside the roughly week-long challenge window is optimistic, not final. This node classifies a finality claim as soft, posted, challengeable, or final, and flags claims of final finality made while the challenge window is still open. Client-side. Zero PII.

This classifier is pinned to one named deployment and to the BoLD interactive fraud-proof model. ART-492 Settlement Finality Classifier is the vendor-neutral equivalent: it takes the challenge window and the settlement parameters as inputs rather than assuming a deployment, and it carries separate tier ladders for optimistic challenge windows, validity proofs and single-slot BFT consensus. Use ART-492 when the deployment is not fixed in advance.
