Rights Record Builder

Builds a structured, hash-anchored rights record for a licensed IP asset. Captures licensor, licensee, license ID, territory, term, and a 9-field rights vector. Produces a deterministic record_hash (SHA-256 over the canonical rights row) suitable for on-chain or off-chain anchoring via the OpenChainGraph anchor pipeline.

Compliance Mandate SHA-256 Record Hash 9-Field Rights Vector Not Legal Advice
Not legal advice. This tool structures and hashes rights data for record-keeping. The published license texts govern all rights. Consult a licensed attorney for your jurisdiction.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
