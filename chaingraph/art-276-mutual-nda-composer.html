Mutual NDA Composer

Assembles a Common Paper Mutual NDA (Version 1.0, CC BY 4.0) from your Cover Page Key Terms: purpose, effective date, MNDA term, term of confidentiality, and governing law and jurisdiction. The Standard Terms body below is vendored verbatim and never modified — only the Cover Page varies. Emits a contract-api.json variable map twin for agent consumption.

Compliance Mandate Fixed Body Verbatim Zero-PII Cover Page Only Not Legal Advice
Not legal advice. This tool assembles a fixed, vendored Standard Terms body with your Cover Page Key Terms only. It does not draft, modify, or advise on the agreement. Consult a licensed attorney for your jurisdiction.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. Party identity, signatures, and notice addresses are never filled by this tool and stay as literal {{placeholder}} tokens for your own off-platform signing flow.
