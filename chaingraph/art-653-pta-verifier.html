OpenChainGraph Suite · ART-653

Pta Verifier

Verifies a plain-text-accounting journal supplied as synthetic text: parses dated transactions and their indented postings, checks each transaction's postings sum to zero (double-entry balance), checks any inline running-balance assertions against computed account balances, builds a Merkle root over the parsed transactions for tamper evidence, and emits Evidence Envelope v0.1 receipt fields (schema, event_type, input/output hashes, result_status). Unsupported journal directives (anything outside dated-transaction and posting lines) are listed, not silently dropped or errored on. Scope limit: single-currency amounts only, no multi-commodity or price-conversion support.

compliance_controlZero PIIClient-side only
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope & reliance
This node has no formal correctness proof yet (compute_proof_ready: deferred). Double-entry balance checking, balance-assertion checking, and Merkle-root construction are mechanical arithmetic over the parsed journal, not standards-mandated content, so standards_basis: not_applicable. Scope limit: single-currency amounts only; multi-commodity or price-conversion journals are not supported and their lines are reported under unsupported_directives rather than silently dropped.
