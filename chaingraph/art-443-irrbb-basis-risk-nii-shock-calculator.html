IRRBB Basis-Risk NII Shock Calculator

Comptroller's Handbook IRR basis-risk convention: sweeps one reference-rate shock across multiple priced indices (Prime, SOFR, Fed Funds, CD portfolio, etc.), each with a caller-declared historical beta vs the reference rate, and isolates the incremental delta-NII attributable to those indices not moving in lockstep. Distinct from ART-369's Rate Shock Ladder Replay, whose parallel-curve convention assumes one shock moves the entire gap schedule uniformly and cannot see basis risk.
