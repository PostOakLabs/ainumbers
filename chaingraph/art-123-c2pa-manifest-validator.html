C2PA Content Credential Manifest Validator

Validate a decoded C2PA 2.x manifest: claim well-formedness (claim_generator, format, instanceID), hard-binding hash assertion (c2pa.hash.data or c2pa.hash.bmff), and claim-signature reference. Agent decodes the JUMBF/binary manifest (e.g. via c2patool) and passes the JSON; this tool validates deterministically. Zero network.
