SWIFT GPI Tracker Lifecycle Simulator

Validates SWIFT GPI pacs.002 payment status code transitions against the GPI state machine and checks the Universal Confirmation SLA: ACCC (credit confirmed) must be sent within 24 hours of ACSP (acceptance). Detects invalid transitions, transitions from terminal states (ACCC, RJCT), and SLA breaches.
