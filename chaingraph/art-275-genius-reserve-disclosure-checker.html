ART-275 · Verify-Rails · Reserve Verification Family

GENIUS Act Monthly Reserve Disclosure Checker

Lints an extracted monthly reserve disclosure (JSON fields, not the source PDF) against GENIUS Act S.394 §4: composition-category eligibility, tenor, custody locations, CEO/CFO certification, registered-examiner presence, month-over-month diff, and an on-chain supply cross-check against a pasted figure. Successor to the pre-issuance GENIUS Act Reserve Attestation Pre-Check: that tool is the pre-issuance readiness gate, this tool is the recurring post-issuance monthly filing check.

GENIUS Act S.394 §4 Monthly Disclosure Deadline ≤ Jan 2027
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Not a PDF verifier
This tool lints extracted disclosure fields you provide. It never claims cryptographic verification of the underlying published PDF filing. PDF-only publishing (no machine-readable extract) is itself the industry gap this tool is built around; see XBRL US comment letters on the GENIUS Act NPRM.
