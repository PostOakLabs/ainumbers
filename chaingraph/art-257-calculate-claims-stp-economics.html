Claims STP Economics Calculator

Models the ROI, NPV, and IRR of straight-through processing automation for insurance claims. Computes annual handling cost savings from STP rate improvement, optional leakage impact analysis, discounted cash flow projections, and payback period. All inputs are aggregate portfolio metrics — no policyholder or claim-level data.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. This tool accepts aggregate portfolio metrics and cost inputs only — no claimant, policyholder, or claim-event personal data.
