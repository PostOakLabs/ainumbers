NAIC RBC Action Level Calculator

Computes the NAIC Risk-Based Capital action level from Total Adjusted Capital (TAC) and Authorized Control Level RBC (ACL). Maps to the five-tier action ladder: No Action (≥200% ACL), Company Action (≥150%), Regulatory Action (≥100%), Authorized Control (≥70%), and Mandatory Control (<70%). Also runs the trend test. Use as step 1 in the insurer RBC workflow.

NAIC RBC NAIC Model Act P&C / Life / Health Regulatory Capital Proofs Deferred
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. This tool accepts aggregate capital and RBC amounts only — no policyholder or personal data.
