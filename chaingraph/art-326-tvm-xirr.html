XIRR (Irregular Dated Cash Flows)

Annualized rate of return for irregular-interval dated cash flows, matching Excel XIRR semantics exactly: fixed actual/365 day count, anchored to the first cash flow date, solved by deterministic bisection over a declared rate bracket. Companion to the IRR primitive for cash flows that do not fall on equal periods.
