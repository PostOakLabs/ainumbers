Payment System MI Pack

Computes a payment system's management-information pack from declared "aggregate" counts only: speed (share of transactions credited within one hour / one business day), average cost, failure rate, availability, fraud shares, complaint resolution, adoption and transparency, each KPI carrying its definition source. The speed percentage is compared against the pinned 75-percent-within-one-hour cross-border retail speed target on the g20_retail and fast_payment_scheme profiles. Missing-input KPIs come back null with a reason code, never zero. This node produces KPIs, not verdicts: it does not reconcile individual records and does not audit statements.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data, use synthetic or anonymised aggregate counts only. This tool accepts aggregate scalars only; it has no field for individual transaction records.

One rounding rule everywhere: round half-up at the final step only, to integer basis points; percent values are basis points divided by 100 (2 decimal places). Channel breakdowns that do not reconcile to the declared totals, credited counts above the declared total and negative declared values raise data-quality flags. Every figure the compute panel returns comes from your own declared counts: this page never supplies real scheme figures, and the prefilled sample is synthetic.

    
Independent work, not an official publication: this node is not endorsed by, and does not speak for, the Financial Stability Board, CPMI, IOSCO, any central bank or any payment scheme. The pinned targets and definitions are cited so the arithmetic is checkable; profile applicability and every design convention are declared above. Jurisdictions and schemes are worked examples, never the product.
