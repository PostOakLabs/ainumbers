openIDS Homeowners Record Validator

Validates homeowners insurance policy records against the openIDS Homeowners Data Standard v1.0 (AAIS / Linux Foundation, November 2025, Apache-2.0 licensed). Checks required sections (policy, insured_location, coverage, premium), required fields within each section, valid policy types (HO-1 through HO-8, DP-1 through DP-3), and flags potential PII fields. This is not an ACORD validator — ACORD XML/AL3 is membership-licensed and is not reproduced here.

openIDS Homeowners v1.0 AAIS / Linux Foundation Apache-2.0 NOT ACORD Proofs Deferred
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. Structural and field validation only — record content is inspected for field presence; personal data is flagged as a warning, not stored or logged.
openIDS v1.0 Required Structure
REQUIRED SECTIONS: policy, insured_location, coverage, premium
policy: policy_number*, effective_date*, expiration_date*, policy_type*
insured_location: street_address*, city*, state*, zip_code*, construction_type*
coverage: dwelling_limit*, other_structures_limit*, personal_property_limit*, liability_limit*
premium: annual_premium*, payment_plan*
Valid policy_type values: HO-1..HO-8, DP-1..DP-3
Record JSON (paste your openIDS Homeowners v1.0 record)
Paste a JSON object with keys: policy, insured_location, coverage, premium. Use synthetic data only.
