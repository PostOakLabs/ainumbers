Examination Readiness Pack

Five stages of regulatory exam management over your own supplied request list. Intake validates a DECLARED as_of date (no runtime clock: the same inputs grade the same on any later day). Roll-up counts delivered, open and overdue requests. The readiness score grades overall READY (all delivered, zero overdue), NOT_READY (nothing delivered) or AT_RISK (between). The optional regime-module annex grades a checklist for the sec-2026 module (AI-use supervision, channel-neutral records preservation, controls-operate evidence) or the amla module (EU AML Authority direct-supervision selection items). The optional evidence handoff emits pointers to the existing binder chain (T574 casefile-binder-composer, T575 casefile-binder-verifier, T576 evidence-handover-bundle): composition, never a rebuilt binder. One pack with regime modules; the module is an input, never a second pack.
