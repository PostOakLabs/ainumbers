Tempo Validator Readiness Scorer

Assess your infrastructure's readiness to operate a Tempo Network validator node across five dimensions: hardware, software/OS, key management, telemetry, and upgrade cadence. Produces a scored readiness grade, per-dimension gap analysis, and a prioritised action checklist. Entry into Tempo's validator set is permissioned — this tool helps you prepare before contacting partners@tempo.xyz.

⚠ Tempo's validator set is currently permissioned. Becoming a validator requires approval from the Tempo team — hardware readiness alone is not sufficient. Current validators include Visa, Stripe, and Zodia Custody (Standard Chartered). Contact partners@tempo.xyz to begin the onboarding process. Bond, stake, and KYC/AML requirements are not publicly published; engage Tempo directly.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
