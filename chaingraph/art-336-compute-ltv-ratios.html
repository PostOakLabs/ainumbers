LTV/CLTV/HCLTV Ratio Calculator

Loan-to-value, combined LTV, and home-equity combined LTV per Fannie Mae Selling Guide B2-1.1-03 and Freddie Mac Single-Family Seller/Servicer Guide 5401.1. Applies the lesser-of-value-or-price rule for purchases and includes the full HELOC credit limit in HCLTV. Feeds ART-222 Agency Eligibility Matrix.
