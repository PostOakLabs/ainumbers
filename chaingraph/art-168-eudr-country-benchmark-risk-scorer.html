EUDR Country Benchmark Risk Scorer

Score EUDR Art. 29 country benchmark risk tier — low, standard, or high — to determine required inspection rates and due-diligence intensity for supply-chain compliance.

v0.5.0
EUDR 2023/1115 Art. 29 Risk Tiers Zero PII W3C VC §13.11 v0.5.0
Root node of eudr-supply-chain-risk-and-traceability chain (art-168→169→170). EUDR Art. 29 classifies countries into benchmark risk tiers determining required inspection rates and due-diligence intensity. Low-risk countries (EU/EEA + strong forest governance): 1% inspection, simplified due diligence. Standard-risk (unclassified): 3% inspection, full due diligence. High-risk: 9% inspection, enhanced due diligence. Commission delegated act pending publication. Feeds traceability linker (art-169). See eudr-supply-chain-risk-and-traceability chain.
