AI System Governance Classifier

Classifies an AI system to its risk tier across EU AI Act (prohibited/high-risk/limited-risk/minimal-risk), NIST AI RMF profile (T1–T3), and ISO 42001 control set (light/standard/enhanced). Terminal node of the ai-management-system-conformance chain. Zero network.

EU AI Act ISO 42001 NIST RMF Zero PII W3C VC §13.11 v0.5.0
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope
Terminal node of ai-management-system-conformance chain (art-171→172→173). Maps a described AI system to governance obligations under all three major frameworks: EU AI Act risk tier (Annex III high-risk mandatory 2 Dec 2027, per Digital Omnibus June 2026), NIST AI RMF profile (T1 basic → T3 enhanced), and ISO/IEC 42001 control set. Covers GPAI provider identification and systemic-risk flag.
