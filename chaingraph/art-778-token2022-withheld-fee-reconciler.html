Token-2022 Withheld-Fee Reconciler

A Token-2022 mint charges a transfer fee and holds it back in two places: a withheld amount on each token account, and an accumulated pot on the mint. This page walks through how the node sums the per-account amounts, reconciles them against the mint's pot and the caller's declared total, and checks where withdrawn fees would land. Every rule comes from the Token-2022 program source pinned at commit b5b7511e.

Presenter mode shows one step per screen. Arrow keys move, A toggles autoplay, Esc exits.
1
Step 1

Withheld fees sit in two places

Every token account carries its own withheld amount, and the mint carries a second pot for fees already harvested up.

The account extension is TransferFeeAmount with one field, withheld_amount, described in the pinned source as the amount withheld during transfers, to be harvested to the mint. The mint extension is TransferFeeConfig, whose own withheld_amount is the pot of fees already moved to the mint for withdrawal. Both are u64 counters in the token's smallest unit, and both are pasted into this node as decimal strings; nothing is read from chain.

2
Step 2

Harvest to the mint is permissionless

Anyone can move account withheld fees up to the mint's pot; an account of a different mint is skipped, not swept.

The pinned processor describes HarvestWithheldTokensToMint as permissionless: it zeroes each harvested account and adds the amount to the mint's pot. An account whose mint differs returns MintMismatch and is skipped, so the node's recomputed sum counts only accounts whose mint matches the declared mint, and lists every skipped one. Each addition is a checked_add: past u64 the harvest fails with Overflow on chain, and the node reports that fact instead of wrapping.

3
Step 3

Withdrawal needs the configured authority

Money leaves the mint's pot only under the withdraw authority named in the mint's config, and lands in an account of the same mint.

The pinned source states that withdraw-from-mint instructions must be signed by the configured withdraw_withheld_authority, and WithdrawWithheldTokensFromAccounts takes a destination, that authority, and the source accounts. The authority is optional: MaybeNull, so a mint may configure none. When a destination is declared, the node reports two plain comparisons: does the destination's mint equal the declared mint, and does the declared withdraw authority equal the config's authority.

4
Step 4

Declared total against the recomputed sum

The caller states a total; the node recomputes it from the accounts and returns the signed difference.

The labels are arithmetic facts: MATCH, DECLARED_ABOVE_RECOMPUTED with a plus-signed difference, or DECLARED_BELOW_RECOMPUTED with a minus-signed one. The node also lists addresses pasted twice, since a repeated entry counts twice in a sum taken as declared, and reports the sum over every declared entry alongside the mint-matching sum so the two numbers can be told apart.

5
Step 5

Sums stay exact past the u64 ceiling

u64 ends at 18,446,744,073,095,516,155; the on-chain addition stops there, and the node keeps counting and flags it.

Every amount is handled as a BigInt from the pasted decimal string to the reported decimal string, the same integer-only discipline as the sibling transfer-fee node. The on-chain harvest uses checked_add and fails with TokenError::Overflow once the mint's pot plus a harvested amount passes u64. The node neither wraps nor refuses: it returns the exact sum and two flags, sum_exceeds_u64 and post_harvest_would_overflow_u64, so the ceiling is a reported fact.

6
Step 6

A placement check stated as booleans

For a declared destination the node answers two yes-or-no questions and stops there.

No destination declared, no placement block: the answer is null and the flags stay empty. The kernel's outputs are values, differences and booleans throughout; it never labels a destination right or wrong, and never guesses intent. Comparisons are string equality on pasted values, stated in the output note, because the node reads nothing from chain.

7
Step 7

Manifest & provenance

One input surface, one output contract, one kernel digest, and the whole thing runs offline.

Kernel: chaingraph/kernels/art-778-token2022-withheld-fee-reconciler.kernel.mjs: pure BigInt sums, subtractions, string equality and boolean comparisons over pasted values; no hashing inside the kernel, no network, no storage. Provenance: solana-program/token-2022 at commit b5b7511e5d4f19a6a118b858d83a7fe3b0017b1e, transfer_fee extension (interface mod.rs and instruction.rs, program processor.rs). Sibling node: art-715 recomputes the fee schedule arithmetic; this node reconciles the withheld amounts that schedule produces.

input  : {mint, transfer_fee_config: {withdraw_withheld_authority, withheld_amount},
          accounts: [{address, mint, withheld_amount}],
          declared_total, destination?: {address, mint, withdraw_authority}}
output : {recomputed_account_sum, mint_withheld_accumulated, declared_total,
          reconciliation, declared_minus_recomputed, sum_over_all_declared_entries,
          sum_exceeds_u64, post_harvest_mint_withheld, post_harvest_would_overflow_u64,
          wrong_mint_accounts[], duplicate_addresses[], fee_receiver_placement}
refuse : MINT_INVALID | TRANSFER_FEE_CONFIG_MALFORMED | ACCOUNTS_MALFORMED
        | DECLARED_TOTAL_INVALID | DESTINATION_MALFORMED
