Fill Quality Summary

Summarise one order’s fill quality from the fills you declare: the average price actually paid with its exact rounding residual, how much of the order filled, how much of the declared market volume it took, and how much came from auctions. Integer arithmetic only.

Exact integer arithmetic Declared fills only Refused fields named
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ A calculation check on the fills and the market volume you declare. It is not investment advice and not a best-execution determination. No market data is fetched; the market volume and its window label are yours. It computes no arrival shortfall and no opportunity cost.
Inputs
Every number is a decimal string, never a JSON number. order_side is buy or sell. currency is one of USD, EUR, GBP, CHF, JPY, BHD, KWD, OMR, TND, JOD, IQD or LYD, and its minor-unit exponent is derived from the code. Prices are integers in units of 10^-price_decimals of the currency; price_decimals is optional (0 to 8) and defaults to the currency exponent, so 100.005 is price "100005" with price_decimals "3". Each fill needs fill_id (unique), side (equal to the order side), currency (equal to the order currency), price, quantity_shares and venue_type (auction or continuous). Up to 5,000 fills. market_volume_shares and market_volume_window (a label that is echoed and never interpreted) are your declaration. orders_placed must be at least the number of fills, and cancels between 0 and orders_placed.
