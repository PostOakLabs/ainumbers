Perp Funding and Carry Calculator

Computes perpetual futures funding rates, compound annual APR, cross-venue funding arb (Hyperliquid vs Binance), and delta-neutral carry (Ethena sUSDe). Includes basis analytics.

gpu:false analytics_mandate groth16-deferred
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Not financial or investment advice. Perpetual funding rates are volatile; liquidation risk, basis risk, and smart contract risk all apply. For informational purposes only. Verify all rates with venue APIs before trading.
Funding Parameters
E.g. 0.01 = 0.01% per hour on HL; 0.03 = 0.03% per 8h on Binance
1 = hourly (HL), 8 = 8-hourly (Binance/Bybit)
HL default 0.035 (3.5 bps); Binance 0.05 (5 bps)
Cross-Venue Arb (optional)
Long venue above / short second venue below for delta-neutral funding arb.
Delta-Neutral Carry (optional)
Hold DeFi yield asset (e.g. sUSDe) + short perp to hedge price risk. Net APR = perp funding + collateral yield.
E.g. sUSDe ~13.5%, leave blank to skip.
