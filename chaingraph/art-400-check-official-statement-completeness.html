Municipal Official Statement Completeness Checker

Checks a municipal-bond Official Statement disclosure-element checklist (present, absent, or incomplete) and continuing-disclosure undertaking presence, per MSRB Rule G-32 and SEC Rule 15c2-12, including the (b)(5)(i)(C) material-event category list.

MSRB G-32 SEC 15c2-12 Record Integrity Family
🔒 All checking runs locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real deal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ ASSERTED-LABELLING NOTE: this checks that declared elements and material-event categories are PRESENT and well-formed, not that the underlying disclosures are true or complete in substance.
