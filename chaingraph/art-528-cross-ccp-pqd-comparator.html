Cross-CCP PQD Comparator

Compares caller-selected CPMI-IOSCO public quantitative disclosure (PQD) fields across FICC and ICE using a manually-transcribed, source-cited fixture dataset. It is never a live feed and never scraped (no CCP publishes these fields machine-readably). FICC and ICE do not disclose the same field set: a field one side does not publish is reported unavailable, never interpolated or defaulted to zero. Optionally flags a caller-declared threshold breach, evaluated per entity. This is comparison arithmetic and delta reporting only, with no ranking, no better/worse scoring language, and no forecasting. Emits a hash-anchored OpenChainGraph v0.4 artifact.

FICC and ICE only (CME, LCH named, not built) Field-level availability, never fabricated Caller-declared threshold flags
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ This tool does not assess CCP soundness and expresses no ranking or better/worse judgment. It reports declared-field deltas and threshold flags over a fixture dataset refreshed by hand each quarter. It computes once when you press the button, stores nothing, runs on no schedule, and retains nothing.

    What this does not do
    
    
    It does not assess CCP soundness, capital adequacy, or resolvability, and produces no ranking or better/worse judgment: AINumbers is not positioned to make that call. It does not pull live data from any CCP; every figure is a manually-transcribed public disclosure, refreshed by hand per quarter. It does not cover CME or LCH, which are both named in the CCP-core spec but not built into this fixture; selecting either as an entity yields an unknown-CCP rejection, never a fabricated figure. It does not recompute a waterfall, size a default fund, or track a margin call: other nodes in the CCP-core family do that.
