Receipt Export Envelopes

One receipt in, two wire envelopes out : deterministic by construction. This node re-expresses an OpenChainGraph execution receipt as a lineage RunEvent (so lineage stacks ingest receipts without a custom adapter) and as a structured-mode event envelope (so eventing stacks do the same). The mapping is a pure function of the receipt: no clock read, no randomness, no network, and a fail-closed refusal on any missing or malformed hash, timestamp or URI-reference. Same receipt in, byte-identical envelopes out.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

The required-field surface each envelope must carry is pinned: the RunEvent mapping validates against the vendored JSON Schema (every emitted event is schema-validated in the node's fixture test), and the envelope always carries the four required context attributes of its core spec, id, source, specversion, type , plus the structured-mode members time, datacontenttype and data.

The runId derivation

A pure function of the receipt

The RunEvent needs a run identifier. This node does not mint one at export time, because that would make the identifier a function of the moment, not of the receipt. Instead the runId is derived from the receipt's own execution_hash in three deterministic steps:

StepOperationResult
1Take the first 32 hex characters of execution_hash (its first 16 bytes)16 raw bytes
2Set byte 6's version nibble to 8: b[6] = (b[6] & 0x0f) | 0x80version bits 1000 in the high nibble; the receipt's own low nibble preserved
3Set byte 8's two variant bits to 10: b[8] = (b[8] & 0x3f) | 0x80variant bits 10x in the two high positions; the receipt's own six low bits preserved
4Format the 16 bytes as canonical lowercase 8-4-4-4-12 hexthe runId

Every other byte of the runId is byte-identical to the first 16 bytes of the receipt's hash. That makes the runId a commitment device: whoever reads the envelope can re-derive it from the receipt and detect a substituted receipt immediately. Example: hash 656e6fd6… yields runId 656e6fd6-3469-85d1-a93a-6dcffbebdb8d (bytes 6 and 8 carry the two bit-sets; nothing else moves).

Fail-closed refusals

Refused exports carry no envelopes

Any missing or malformed member refuses the export whole. A refusal emits no envelopes at all (both envelope members are null), so a downstream stack can never ingest a half-mapped receipt.

event_time not a well-formed timestamp (calendar-checked: month, day, leap-year February, hour ≤ 23, minute ≤ 59, second ≤ 60, offset present)event_time

Hard fence: the receipt is caller-supplied and caller-asserted. This node performs no verification of the hash values themselves; this node is an exporter, not a verifier. Correctness of the underlying execution is scored by the estate's recompute nodes, never by this receipt's envelopes.
