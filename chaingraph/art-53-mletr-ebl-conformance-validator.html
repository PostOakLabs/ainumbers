MLETR / eBL Conformance & Enforceability Validator

Validates an electronic transferable record (eBL or ETR) against MLETR functional-equivalence tests (Arts. 10–12) and scores cross-corridor legal enforceability. Answers the key question: will this eBL hold up at both ends? Client-side, zero-egress; emits a hash-anchored OpenChainGraph v0.4 artifact.
