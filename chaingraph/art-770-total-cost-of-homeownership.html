Total Cost of Homeownership

The mortgage payment is usually less than two thirds of what a house actually costs. This node ports Apex #153 (AL-167): it amortizes the loan month by month, grows each recurring cost year by year, and returns the gross and net cost of ownership over the hold, a break-even monthly rent, and a cost-composition breakdown. Every rate is user-supplied; nothing is fetched.

HousingPITI + CompositionDeterministicZero PIIClient-side only
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data; use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope & reliance
Deterministic port of Apex #153: standard amortization, MI while post-payment LTV is strictly above the drop threshold (default 80%, Homeowners Protection Act borrower-cancellation leg; the 78% automatic-termination leg is not modeled), property tax on purchase price grown yearly, insurance/maintenance/HOA/utilities grown yearly, opportunity cost as annual compounding of cash at close, deduction benefit (interest + SALT-capped property tax) × marginal rate. The SALT-cap default shown ($10,000) is the Apex fallback; the official 2026 figure is $40,400 (26 USC 164(b)(7)). Enter marginal rate 0 if you take the standard deduction (2026 single $16,100). No clock is read: the computation is deterministic and zkVM-promotable. The 1%-maintenance panel value is display-only; the maintenance % input below drives the math.
