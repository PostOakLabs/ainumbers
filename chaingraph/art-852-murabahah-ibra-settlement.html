Murabahah Ibra' Settlement

A deferred-price sale discloses a schedule of instalments, profit and principal split, outstanding cost and selling price, and deferred profit. On early settlement the rebate is the deferred profit at that point less any permitted recovery cost. This page reproduces that arithmetic in integer minor units, including the variable-rate and non-delivery cases. Outputs are arithmetic and rule checks against the cited regulator paragraphs, not a fatwa, Shariah certification, legal opinion or regulatory approval. Buy-now-pay-later and card rebate treatments are out of scope, and late-payment amounts are pass-through only.

Basis and declarations

    
Profit for a period is the outstanding cost multiplied by the contracted rate over twelve, rounded half to even to a whole minor unit. The principal portion is the instalment less that profit. Outstanding selling price falls by one instalment each period, and deferred profit is the difference between outstanding selling price and outstanding cost. The published illustration states a selling price of 365,136.00 while its schedule uses 365,135.97; both are emitted and the three-minor-unit difference is a named field, never hidden. Some printed outstanding-cost cells in that illustration carry cent-level drift; this node emits the internally consistent recurrence and discloses the residual rather than copying an inconsistent cell.

    
Early settlement: the rebate is the deferred profit at the settlement point less only permitted recovery costs. Lost profit and marketing costs are refused by name. Late-payment amounts are added separately and never accrue as interest. The non-delivery case adds rebate on undisbursed principal and stops profit at the first sign that delivery would fail.

    
Nothing here is a legal or religious ruling. The node does not assess whether a contract was validly formed, whether an asset was possessed, or whether a rebate clause is enforceable. It checks arithmetic and the decision branches named in the cited paragraphs. Values are synthetic and no personal data is accepted.
