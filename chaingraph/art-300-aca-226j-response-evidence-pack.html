226J Response Evidence Pack Builder

Assembles a replayable evidence pack responding to an IRS Letter 226J proposed Employer Shared Responsibility Payment assessment: recomputes the exposure position against the IRS-asserted figure, computes the response-window deadline from the supplied letter date (DRAFT-PINNED at 30 days pending a confirmed citation), and records a named-HR/benefits-officer attestation closure. Recompute and respond before the window closes -- not a guarantee of abatement.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
