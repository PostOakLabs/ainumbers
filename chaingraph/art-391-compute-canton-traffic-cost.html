Canton Synchronizer Traffic-Cost Calculator

Computes Canton Network synchronizer traffic cost under the CIP-0042/CIP-0084 regime: message megabytes × the Tokenomics-Committee-set USD/MB rate, converted to Canton Coin burned at your CC/USD price. Applies the CIP-0119 transfer-preapproval free-traffic window when applicable.

Canton Network CIP-0084 Traffic Fee CIP-0119 Free Period
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ The traffic rate, CC/USD price, and protocol version are your own inputs, source-cited in the output — this tool never hard-codes the CIP-0084 rate or a CC market price as a silent constant. Both move: the rate already changed once (CIP-0042 → CIP-0084) and CC/USD is a market price.
Computes traffic COST for one message/envelope. For Canton settlement and counterparty structure, see diagnose_canton_readiness → or the app-reward side at compute_canton_app_reward_estimate (ART-392) →
