Scope
Step 2 of eudr-supply-chain-risk-and-traceability chain (art-168→169→170). EUDR single-DDS rule (Art. 4): only the first operator placing goods on the EU market files the DDS; all downstream operators reference the upstream DDS reference number. Validates single-DDS compliance, TRACES NT reference format, plot geolocation coverage, and custody-chain completeness. Feeds readiness diagnostic (art-170).
