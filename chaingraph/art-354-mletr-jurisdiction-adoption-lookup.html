MLETR Jurisdiction-Adoption Lookup

Pick an origin and destination jurisdiction and look up the UNCITRAL MLETR (Model Law on Electronic Transferable Records) adoption status for each — statute, scope, and effective date, cited and data_version stamped — plus a corridor verdict for whether an electronic bill of lading (eBL) is legally effective end-to-end across that corridor. Static citation table, update-on-touch, not live data. Answers the "is an eBL faster paper in corridor X→Y" practitioner question that sits upstream of the MLETR control-evidence chain (OpenChainGraph Suite).
