TSV Stoppage Concurrency

Checks a tokenized-stock venue (TSV) against condition H of the SEC TSV Exemption (FR 2026-19388, 91 Fed. Reg. 60176, Sept. 22, 2026): a TSV must stop trading in a Tokenized NMS Stock concurrently with any stoppage of trading in the underlying NMS stock on the primary listing exchange, which includes a halt or a suspension, and must immediately notify its TSV Participants. Stoppage intervals are half-open (start-inclusive, end-exclusive). Every trade inside an interval beyond the declared concurrency tolerance is flagged; the venue's own halt record must span each stoppage; participant-notice latency is reported as a measured value in milliseconds and is never graded pass/fail, because the order says "immediately" without a number. Evidence only, zero network.
