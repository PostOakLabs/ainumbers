Regulatory Obligations Register

A regime-agnostic rule-to-obligation-to-evidence register verdict. You supply the register (each obligation as a rule_id, an owner, control_ids, evidence_refs); the node computes owner and evidence coverage percentages, the control-linkage count, the unassigned list, one FAIL finding per failing check, and an overall COVERED or GAPS_FOUND determination. Five stages in one deterministic pass: intake with fail-closed shape validation, owner coverage, control linkage, evidence linkage, verdict. This is the spine the exam-readiness and attestation packs hang off. The estate's BaaS control mapper (tools/152-baas-provider-comparator.html) is the pattern source for one scoped framework; this register is the regime-agnostic build the gap scan called for.

Scope & reliance
Owner coverage and evidence coverage are the gated checks (both must reach 100 percent for COVERED). Control linkage is measured but never gates the verdict: an obligation with an owner and evidence but no control link still counts as covered here. Thresholds are internal design, not any regime's rule; a reader holding a regulator's rulebook uses this as the register arithmetic, not as a statement of what that regime requires. All inputs are synthetic identifiers. No formal correctness proof exists for this node yet (compute_proof_ready: deferred); see the proof-status note below the results.
