Event Fee Recompute

Recomputes event-contract trading fees per fill and per order from an effective-dated venue fee profile, and diffs the recompute against a reported fee. Venue-neutral: the fee family and its parameters are data, with shipped effective-dated profiles for Kalshi (per-fill trade fee ceiled to $0.000001 plus the pinned per-order rounding accumulator, reproduced from both pinned worked examples), Polymarket global (pinned category rate table with 5-decimal rounding and the 0.00001 USDC minimum), Polymarket US (CFTC 40.6(d) taker coefficient history), and a caller-declared generic profile. Every fee path is integer BigInt arithmetic; conservation (order fee = fill trade fees + net carried rounding) is checked on every order. This tool recomputes declared terms and reports arithmetic only: no endorsement by any venue, no trading advice, no account or order identifiers by schema, values that look like identifiers are refused and never echoed.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data: use synthetic or anonymised values only; order labels are opaque synthetic strings and identifier-like values are refused.
Fee recompute input (aggregate values only)


    

  


  

    
What this node computes

    

      
FamilyRuleOutcome

      
parabolic / multipliertheta x C x p x (1-p), times the effective series multiplier; prices are integer micro-dollars and every product is BigIntper-fill fee

      
linear / flat / rebate_sharetheta x C per contract; a fixed micro amount per contract; a maker rebate equal to a declared share of the taker-equivalent fee (never a negative theta)per-fill fee

      
Kalshi accumulatorper-fill trade fee ceiled to $0.000001; rounding overpayment carries across the order's fills and is rebated in $0.01 (non-direct) or $0.0001 (direct) increments, capped so a fill's net fee never goes negative - the pinned worked examples are golden vectorsorder fee + carry

      
Polymarket globalpinned category table (crypto 0.07 ... combos 0.05), 5-decimal rounding, fees under 0.00001 USDC round to zeroper-fill fee

      
Polymarket UStaker theta 0.06 from 2026-07-07 and 0.0695 from 2026-09-17 (CFTC 40.6(d) letters); combo curve C x p x [0.0695(1-p) + 0.04(1-p)^4] from 2026-09-25per-fill fee

      
fee_checkper order, the recompute is diffed against the caller-reported fee: MATCH, OVERCHARGE (reported above the recompute) or UNDERCHARGEverdict

      
conservationevery order checks order fee = fill trade fees + net carried rounding; the flag is set on every completed runcheck

      
refusalsunknown profile, malformed as_of, identifier-like order or outcome labels (never echoed), malformed fields, out-of-domain inputsrefusal

    

    
Adjacency: art-211 and art-212 compute whole-order fees inside their analyses; this node is the standalone per-fill recompute and edits neither. Rates and rounding rules come from pinned venue documentation only; any rule without a pinned source ships caller-declared. Profiles for Robinhood, Crypto.com, ForecastEx and Coinbase are deferred.
