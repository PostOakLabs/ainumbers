TLS / X.509 PKI Migration Planner

Sequences TLS and PKI migration from RSA/ECDSA to post-quantum algorithms. Builds a phased plan (root CAs → intermediates → leaf certificates), models payload impact per NIST FIPS 203/204, and flags interoperability risks. Reuses CBOM inventory from tool 499. Part of the pqc-tls-pki and pqc-hndl-protocol-plan chains.
