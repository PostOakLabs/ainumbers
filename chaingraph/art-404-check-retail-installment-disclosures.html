Retail Installment Contract TILA Disclosure Checker

Ties Amount Financed, Finance Charge, and Total of Payments on a retail installment contract (e.g. auto financing) against an amortization schedule REUSED from Amortization Schedule Builder (art-332). This tool does not amortize: paste that tool's exported totals and schedule_digest below.

12 CFR 1026.18 Composes art-332 Collections Compliance Family
🔒 All checking runs locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real consumer data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ COMPOSITION NOTE: this checks the tie-out ARITHMETIC against the schedule totals you supply: it does not independently recompute or verify the amortization schedule. The dealer-participation record below is ASSERTED and receipt-bound; it is a fair-lending adjacency note, not a discrimination determination.

Disclosed figures (as printed on the contract)
