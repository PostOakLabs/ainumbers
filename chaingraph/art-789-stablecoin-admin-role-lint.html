Stablecoin Admin Role Lint

Lint a stablecoin token's declared admin-role configuration for separation of duties, key concentration and issuance-policy fit. Profiles follow the published role models of the Circle FiatToken contracts and the Paxos USDP contracts; every input is a role label such as "org-treasury-msig". Addresses, public keys and private keys are refused by design. This is an independent compliance tool: it is affiliated with no issuer, implies no endorsement by Circle or Paxos, and its output is configuration guidance, not legal advice.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data. Use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Role Configuration (role labels only)
Profile Determines the role set, critical roles, default distinctness pairs and capability mapping. Use of a profile name describes the role model only and implies no endorsement.
Holders: one per line: label, kind, threshold, signers_count kind is one of: eoa, multisig, timelock, hsm_service. Threshold and signers_count apply to multisig holders.
Roles: JSON map of role to holder label fiattoken roles: owner, proxy_admin, master_minter, pauser, blacklister, rescuer. usdp roles: owner, pauser, asset_protection, supply_controller.
Minters: JSON array of {holder_label, allowance_minor} Minor-unit allowances per minter label. Compared against the policy ceilings.
Policy JSON: max_single_minter_allowance_minor, max_total_allowance_minor, require_multisig_for, require_distinct Caller-declared issuance policy and control requirements. Distinctness pairs are [roleA, roleB] arrays.
