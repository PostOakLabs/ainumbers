Audit Recalculation Suite

Independently recomputes depreciation (straight-line, double-declining-balance, units-of-production), interest accrual, EPS (basic and diluted), straight-line intangible amortization, and prepaid-expense roll-forwards, then diffs each figure against the client's stated figure under a tolerance gate you declare. A category only runs when you add at least one item to it.

Independent Recalculation Variance / Threshold Gate 5 Schedule Types
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ EPS diluted uses the simplified NI-less-preferred-dividends-over-diluted-shares form — it does not model if-converted or treasury-stock adjustments for specific convertible instruments.
