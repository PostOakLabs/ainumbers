Cross-License Rights Comparator

Compare any two licenses from the CC, CBE (a16z Cant Be Evil), and Story PIL families on a 9-dimension rights vector. Returns a diff array and partial-order permissiveness result. Informational Rosetta stone for cross-framework selection. Not legal advice.

Creative Commons a16z CBE Story PIL 9-Dim Rights Vector Selection Only
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ Rights vectors encode the stated terms of published license texts as a canonical boolean matrix for comparison purposes. This tool provides an informational analysis only and is not legal advice. Consult the canonical license texts and a licensed attorney before relying on any comparison for commercial or legal decisions. Sources: creativecommons.org · a16z CBE · Story PIL
