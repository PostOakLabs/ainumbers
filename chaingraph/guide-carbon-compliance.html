Carbon & Climate Computational Compliance

CBAM-anchored carbon compliance computational layer. Nine tools (ART-68–76) across 8 cbm-* chains cover the full CBAM declarant lifecycle (embedded-emissions calculation, default-value resolution, certificate-cost modeling, precursor aggregation) and extend into EU Taxonomy alignment scoring, EuGB factsheet validation, and NGFS Phase V climate-scenario application. All regulatory reference data (CBAM factor schedule 2026–2034, default values per country×sector, Taxonomy objectives/DNSH criteria, NGFS scenarios) is baked into the kernels citing primary sources. Zero PII.

✅ DO NOW: CBAM definitive period started 1 January 2026. Importers of CBAM goods (cement, iron/steel, aluminium, fertilisers, electricity, hydrogen) above the 50-tonne CO₂e threshold must track embedded emissions NOW. The CBAM factor schedule begins at 2.5% (2026) and reaches 100% in 2034. Default values carry a mandatory mark-up: +10% (2026), +20% (2027), +30% (2028+). EU Taxonomy alignment obligations and EuGB Regulation (21 Dec 2024) are fully in force. Run cbam-fit first to classify your exposure.
⚠ PREPARE-AHEAD: First CBAM declaration due 30 Sep 2027 (covering calendar year 2026). Embedded-emissions verification requirements will tighten as CBAM transitional period data accumulates. NGFS Phase V scenarios are the current reference; verify current edition from NGFS publications.
EDUCATIONAL: All outputs are decision-support drafts. Not regulatory filings. EuGB criteria against Reg. (EU) 2023/2631 and Commission technical standards.

CBAM: embedded emissions & certificate cost (cbm-* chains)

The four CBAM-specific tools cover the full declarant workflow: embedded emissions calculation → default-value resolution → certificate cost modeling → precursor aggregation.
ART-69 · compliance_mandate
CBAM Embedded Emissions Calculator
Flagship CBAM tool. Calculates total embedded emissions (direct + indirect) per goods batch using actual SEE or sector defaults. Baked: default specific embedded emission (SEE) values per CBAM goods category, indirect electricity emission factor (0.376 tCO₂e/MWh default). 50-tonne annual threshold check.
calculate_cbam_embedded_emissions IN FORCE · CBAM 1 Jan 2026
ART-70 · compliance_mandate
CBAM Default Value Resolver
Resolves sector default SEE values per origin country×sector combination with mandatory mark-up: +10% (2026), +20% (2027), +30% (2028+). Reference version CBAM-IR-v1.0-2024-Q4 baked. Flags when actual verification is possible.
resolve_cbam_default_value IN FORCE · CBAM 1 Jan 2026
ART-71 · compliance_mandate
CBAM Certificate Cost Engine
Models total CBAM certificate cost: embedded emissions × EUA carbon price × CBAM factor schedule (2.5% 2026 → 100% 2034). Quarterly holding threshold (80% default). Flags CBAM_ABOVE_THRESHOLD and SIGNIFICANT_CBAM_LIABILITY. Full factor schedule baked.
model_cbam_certificate_cost IN FORCE · CBAM 1 Jan 2026
ART-72 · compliance_mandate
CBAM Precursor Emissions Aggregator
Aggregates precursor-embedded emissions into complex-goods CBAM calculation. 2028 pre-consumer-scrap rule (50% credit proxy for eligible scrap inputs). Routes precursor emissions into the cbam-liability chain.
aggregate_cbam_precursor_emissions IN FORCE · CBAM 1 Jan 2026

EU Taxonomy, EuGB & Climate Scenario

Taxonomy alignment scoring, GAR/KPI aggregation, EuGB factsheet validation, and NGFS Phase V scenario application for Taxonomy-adjacent climate risk.
ART-73 · compliance_mandate
EU Taxonomy Alignment Scorer
Three-gate test: Substantial Contribution (SC) + Do No Significant Harm (DNSH) + Minimum Safeguards. Scores across all 6 Taxonomy objectives. Omnibus I revisions (28 Jan 2026) reflected. Flags NOT_ALIGNED, PARTIAL_ALIGNMENT_DNSH_FAIL, MINIMUM_SAFEGUARDS_FAIL.
score_taxonomy_alignment IN FORCE · Taxonomy Reg.
ART-74 · compliance_mandate
Taxonomy KPI / GAR Aggregator
Aggregates revenue, CapEx, and OpEx Taxonomy-eligible and -aligned exposures. Computes Green Asset Ratio (GAR) for financial undertakings per Taxonomy Disclosures Delegated Regulation. Flags LOW_GAR, BELOW_EU_BENCHMARK.
aggregate_taxonomy_kpi_gar IN FORCE · Taxonomy Reg.
ART-75 · compliance_mandate
EuGB Factsheet Validator
Validates European Green Bond factsheet against the 8 Annex I disclosure sections required by Reg. (EU) 2023/2631. Checks 100% Taxonomy-aligned proceeds requirement, pre/post-issuance templates, and external review obligations. In force 21 Dec 2024.
validate_eugb_factsheet IN FORCE · 21 Dec 2024
ART-76 · model_governance
NGFS Climate Scenario Applicator
Applies NGFS Phase V climate scenarios (orderly, disorderly, hot-house, Fit-for-55) to a portfolio. Reference version NGFS-Phase-V-2025 baked with transition risk, physical risk, and carbon-price trajectories. Verify current scenario edition from NGFS publications.
apply_climate_scenario NGFS Phase V · verify edition

Who runs these chains

The CBAM compliance and sustainable-finance disclosure value chain.
Tier 1: EU importers & customs agents
The declarants
Any EU importer of CBAM goods above 50 tCO₂e/year. Primary chains: cbam-fit, cbam-liability, cbam-precursor. First declaration due 30 Sep 2027 (calendar year 2026 data). Act now to establish embedded-emissions data flows.
Tier 2: ESG & climate risk teams at financial institutions
The disclosers
Banks, asset managers, and insurers with Taxonomy disclosure (GAR), SFDR PAI, and EuGB issuance obligations. Primary chains: taxonomy-align, taxonomy-kpi, eugb-conformance, climate-scenario.
Tier 3: Carbon & sustainability RegTech vendors
The embedders (M&A-relevant)
ESG data providers, carbon accounting platforms, and sustainable-finance disclosure vendors embedding cbm-* chains as MCP tools. CBAM is a recurring declarant obligation; annual renewal creates durable SaaS attach.
Tier 4: Tax authorities & customs agencies
The verifiers
National customs authorities implementing CBAM declarations and embedded-emissions verification. Hash-anchored carbon audit packs (carbon-audit-pack) provide tamper-evident evidence trails.
CBAM Reg. (EU) 2023/956: Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism. Definitive period 1 Jan 2026; 50 tCO₂e threshold; first declaration 30 Sep 2027. Verify against Official Journal and CBAM registry guidance.
CBAM Implementing Reg. (EU) 2024/3238: Default embedded emission values by country×sector. Mark-up rates: +10% (2026), +20% (2027), +30% (2028+). Verify current version.
EU Taxonomy Reg. (EU) 2020/852 + Delegated Regulations: SC criteria per Taxonomy objective, DNSH criteria, minimum safeguards. EU Omnibus I revisions in force 28 Jan 2026; verify simplified DNSH criteria.
EuGB Reg. (EU) 2023/2631: European Green Bond Standard in force 21 Dec 2024. 100% Taxonomy-aligned proceeds. 8 Annex I factsheet sections. Verify Commission technical standards.
NGFS Phase V (2025): Network for Greening the Financial System: orderly, disorderly, hot-house, Fit-for-55 scenarios. Verify current edition from ngfs.net.
