CO SB 21-169 BIFSG Bias Threshold Tester

Tests aggregate regression outputs against Colorado SB 21-169 / Reg. 10-1-1 (effective Dec 1, 2023) BIFSG proxy bias detection thresholds. Statistical prong: p<0.05 AND marginal effect ≥5 percentage points. Premium prong: model-predicted premium ≥5% above group average. Mandatory annual attestation due Dec 1 each year. ZERO PII — aggregate statistics only; never enter individual applicant data.
