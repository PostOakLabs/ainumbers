Ijarah IMBT Rental Schedule

Computes a month-by-month rental schedule for an ijarah lease that ends with the lessee owning the asset, against one pinned authority profile: the Malaysian regulator's Ijarah policy document, paragraphs 14, 16 and 22. Fixed, benchmark and hybrid rental bases with required floor and cap clamps, lessor maintenance and takaful cost lines, the two total-loss branches, and the post-lease ownership transfer event. Outputs are arithmetic and rule checks, not a fatwa, Shariah certification, legal opinion or regulatory approval.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
What it checks

Rental fixed at inception on a fixed, benchmark or hybrid basis; a benchmark-determined rental requires a floor and a cap and every benchmark-determined component is clamped into them; a rental revision without a declared mutual agreement is refused as a unilateral increase. Maintenance and takaful cost lines sit with the lessor, takaful is required unless a declared conventional-insurance circumstance is supplied with its own cost line. A total loss with a proven lessee fault keeps rent running and puts restoration on the lessee; without proven fault the rent stops from the loss month and takaful proceeds belong to the lessor. Ownership transfer is output as a separate post-lease event: sale at a declared price, conditional hibah, or wa'd to hibah; a wa'd to purchase triggered by anything other than default or breach of terms is refused.

Out of scope in v1: forward ijarah, sub-lease, leaseback, advance or deferred rental timing, and any authority other than the pinned profile, which is refused by name.

Try it locally

Paste a policy_parameters object. Amounts are integer minor units; the schedule is capped at 360 monthly rows.
