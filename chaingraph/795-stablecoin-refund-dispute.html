Stablecoin Refund Dispute Check

Recomputes the expected refund and dispute outcome of stablecoin payments held under an escrowed refund protocol (a lock period with release, an arbiter refund that is all-or-nothing unless partial refunds are declared, an early withdrawal carrying a basis-point fee, and partial refunds with release of the remainder) from aggregate, synthetic-label payment records only, and flags every break between the merchant reported state or amounts and the recomputation with a named rule and a conservation identity over integer minor units. Everything runs locally on aggregate values only, so nothing leaves the machine.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data: use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
The pinned profile re-implements the behaviour of the Circle Refund Protocol (open source, Apache-2.0) at pinned commit a7ae494 from its published source; this is an independent re-computation tool and implies no endorsement by, affiliation with, or warranty from Circle. All example values on this page are synthetic. Fee rounding is floor(amount times fee_bp over 10000) in integer minor units.
Payment set input (aggregate values only, synthetic labels)


    

  


  

    
What this node checks

    

      
RuleCheckOutcome

      
lock periodA payment sits HELD in escrow until paid_at plus the caller-declared lock period seconds; after the lock the full remaining amount releases to the recipientexpected state

      
refund_is_all_or_nothingAn arbiter refund returns the full payment amount; a decision below the full amount only counts when the caller declares partial refunds allowed, otherwise the decision is treated as the full refund and the break is namedbreak

      
payment_still_lockedA merchant report of RELEASED (or a released value) while the lock is still open at the audit clock is a break; the recomputed state is HELDbreak

      
early_withdrawal_fee_bpAn early withdrawal inside the lock carries fee = floor(amount_minor times fee_bp over 10000), integer minor units, round-down; the remainder releasesexpected state + fee

      
terminal_state_mismatchReported state differs from the recomputed terminal state (HELD, RELEASED, REFUNDED, PARTIALLY_REFUNDED, EARLY_WITHDRAWN)break

      
reported_amount_mismatchReported refunded or released minor amounts differ from the recomputationbreak

      
conservationPaid equals refunded plus released plus fee plus still held, per payment and in totalidentity

      
verdictCONSISTENT (no breaks), CONSISTENT_WITH_BREAKS (breaks but the reported figures are internally conservative), INCONSISTENT (breaks and reported figures violate conservation)verdict

      
identifier refusalA payment_ref resembling an address (0x plus 40 hex), a transaction hash (0x plus 64 hex) or a 40-plus-character identifier-like blob is refused with no echo of the valuerefusal

    

    
The pinned profile re-implements the escrow, refund, arbiter and early-withdrawal behaviour of the Circle Refund Protocol (Apache-2.0) from a pinned source snapshot; it is an independent re-implementation in plain arithmetic and implies no endorsement by or affiliation with Circle. Payment settlement evidence lives in the estate x402 and CCTP nodes; this node consumes none of their outputs.
