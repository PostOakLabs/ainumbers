AI Training-Data Lineage Record

Composes a hash-chained ML training-data lineage record — dataset identity, upstream source datasets, a declared collection/governance method, and an OPTIONAL reference to an existing OCG receipt for the training-run compute (tool identity, execution hash, kernel digest — never re-embedded).

Data Governance EU AI Act Art 10 / Annex IV 2(d) SR 11-7 Data Lineage
Supersession notice (added 2026-08-02): On 17 April 2026 the Federal Reserve issued SR 26-2, Revised Guidance on Model Risk Management, which supersedes and replaces SR 11-7. Any reference to SR 11-7 on this page describes the framework as it stood before that date.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ dataset_id and source_dataset_ids are STRUCTURAL catalog identifiers only. This kernel never ingests, stores, or echoes dataset contents. It documents dataset lineage and governance metadata — it does NOT validate dataset quality, bias, or representativeness.
Not build_ai_decision_log_record (art-236, per-inference decision chain) and not build_ai_workpaper_record (art-380, audit workpaper over a receipt). All three may reference the same underlying receipt hash without re-embedding it.
