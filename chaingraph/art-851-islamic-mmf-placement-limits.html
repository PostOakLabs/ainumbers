Islamic MMF Placement Limits

Checks a money market fund's placements against one pinned authority profile: the Saudi Capital Market Authority Investment Funds Regulations 2025, Article 56(a) to (j). It reports used percentage, cap, headroom and breach flag for each numeric limit, a maturity-bucket ladder, and a stress-test frequency reminder. Shariah eligibility of each holding is supplied by the caller; the node does not assess it. Outputs are arithmetic and rule checks, not a fatwa, Shariah certification, legal opinion or regulatory approval.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
What it checks

Permitted placement types; hedging derivatives at 5% of net asset value; securitisation at 10%; single issuer at 10% and single counterparty at 25%; same group at 25%; fund units at 25%; government-debt exemption from those concentration tests; weighted average life at 180 calendar days; liquidity of 10% of net asset value or maturities within seven days. Stress-test frequency is shown as a reminder only.

Only the Saudi CMA profile is pinned. Any other authority, including SC Malaysia money market fund limits, is refused by name rather than guessed.

Try it locally

Paste a policy_parameters object. Amounts are integer minor units. Issuer, counterparty and group keys must be opaque lowercase tokens, not identifiers.
