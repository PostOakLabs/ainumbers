Wolfsberg Payment Transparency & LEI Binding Linter

Scores a cross-border payment against the Wolfsberg Payment Transparency Standards (6 fields, 110-point scale: HIGH 80+, MEDIUM 50–79, LOW below 50) and validates ISO 17442 LEI check-digit bindings for originator and beneficiary. LEI absence is not an error; LEI presence with a bad check digit is a binding failure.

Wolfsberg Group ISO 17442 LEI BIS CPMI d218 Proofs Deferred
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. LEIs are public identifiers; all scoring uses structural fields only, never real party PII.
