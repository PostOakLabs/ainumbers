Workforce Board ROI Report

A workforce program ROI report from a program budget, enrollment and WIOA outcome data: a five-indicator benchmark scorecard against the WIOA PY 2023 target sets for adult, dislocated-worker and youth programs, cost-per-outcome metrics, and a taxpayer return model with a break-even horizon and a three-year return per dollar.

Scope & reliance
The scorecard compares the entered outcome rates against the WIOA PY 2023 national target sets carried in the kernel; a percentage indicator exceeds at target + 2 points and meets at target − 1 point, the earnings indicator exceeds at target × 1.05 and meets at target. The wage gain is measured against the official BLS QCEW Q3 2023 private-sector weekly wage × 52 for the selected sector. Tax recapture applies a fixed 30% effective-rate assumption (not a statutory rate) to the positive wage gain, annualizes Q2 median earnings × 4, and grades the program on the three-year return per dollar. The PY 2023 target values are carried pending an official DOL ETA publication and are flagged accordingly; participants exited defaults to enrolled when left blank.
