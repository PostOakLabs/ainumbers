Digital Trade / Electronic Trade Documents (MLETR)

OpenChainGraph tools for the electronic trade document ecosystem: MLETR (UNCITRAL Model Law on Electronic Transferable Records), ICC eUCP v2.1 / eURC v1.1 / URDTT v1.0, and the DCSA eBL standard. As of 2026, 10+ economies have adopted MLETR or are aligned (UK, Singapore, UAE, France Decree 2025-811, Bahrain, Japan, India, US/Germany aligned). DCSA's nine ocean carriers have committed to 100% electronic bills of lading by 2030. The ADB trade-finance gap sits at $2.5T, concentrated in SMEs where documentary friction is a primary cause.

These tools validate eBL legal enforceability, check digital trade-rule compliance, verify document-set integrity, screen counterparties for TBML, model trade-finance eligibility, and emit a Merkle-root audit pack, agent-native end-to-end via OpenChainGraph v0.4 Compute Binding.

France: Decree 2025-811 (first EU MLETR transposition) Japan: FY2026 implementation DCSA: 100% eBL by 2030 ADB gap: $2.5T (2025)

Cross-validates a full trade-document set for internal consistency and computes a SHA-256 Merkle provenance root (ICC DSI KTDDE field model). Flags TBML red flags: over/under-invoicing, phantom shipments, circular trade.

Key citations & standards

No content on this page or the linked tools fabricates regulatory requirements. All rules cited below trace to published primary sources.

UNCITRAL MLETR: Model Law on Electronic Transferable Records (2017); Arts. 10 (singularity/integrity), 11 (exclusive control), 12 (reliability)
UK ETDA 2023: Electronic Trade Documents Act 2023; first major economy to adopt MLETR-equivalent legislation
France Decree 2025-811: First EU member state to fully transpose MLETR
ICC eUCP v2.1 / eURC v1.1 / URDTT v1.0: ICC digital trade rulebooks; electronic documentary credit, electronic collections, open-account digital transactions
FATF TBML typologies: Financial Action Task Force Trade-Based Money Laundering (2006, 2020 update); over/under-invoicing, phantom shipments, circular trade
ICC DSI KTDDE: Key Trade Documents & Data Elements (ICC Digital Standards Initiative) field model for cross-document consistency
ADB Trade Finance Gap Survey 2025: $2.5T gap (~10% of global trade), concentrated in SMEs; DCSA 100% eBL by 2030 target
