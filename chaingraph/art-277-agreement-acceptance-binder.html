Agreement Acceptance Binder

Binds a party's acceptance to a specific assembled agreement artifact — a specific template, at a specific vendored body hash, filled with a specific variable map — referenced by its execution_hash, never re-embedded. An optional §16 eddsa-jcs-2022 signature on the emitted artifact turns this into a countersignable "I accepted THESE exact terms" receipt.

Compliance Mandate Zero-PII Receipt Countersignable via §16 Not Legal Advice
Not legal advice. Records a stated acceptance of a specific referenced artifact; it does not itself establish legal effect, identity, or enforceability.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only. This receipt carries no party identity — only the accepting role and the referenced artifact hashes.
Referenced Artifact
The execution_hash of the assembled agreement artifact being accepted (e.g. from ART-276).
The vendored, immutable body's provenance hash (from the template's contract-api.json).
Optional. References a first party's acceptance execution_hash, forward-compatible with a future §16.x proof-chain endorsement.
