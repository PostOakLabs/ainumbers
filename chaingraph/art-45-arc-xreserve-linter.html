Arc xReserve Config Linter

Lint an Arc xReserve reserve configuration against GENIUS Act §4 1:1 eligible-asset backing requirement (S.394, enacted 18 Jul 2025), MiCA Article 54 EMT reserve rules, and Circle Arc xReserve eligible-asset composition guidelines. Produces A–F graded checks with remediation guidance. Client-side. Deterministic. Zero PII.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
