OpenChainGraph Suite · ART-174 · AI Governance

NIST AI RMF Function Mapper

Maps supplied AI controls and evidence to NIST AI RMF Govern/Map/Measure/Manage functions and sub-categories, returning per-function coverage scores (0–100) and gap lists. Root node of the ai-governance-framework-crosswalk chain. Zero network.

NIST AI RMF Govern·Map·Measure·Manage Zero PII W3C VC §13.11 v0.5.0
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope
Root node of ai-governance-framework-crosswalk chain (art-174→175→176). NIST AI RMF (2023) organises AI risk management into four core functions: GOVERN (policy, roles, culture, transparency, accountability), MAP (context, categorisation, risk identification, stakeholders), MEASURE (analysis, monitoring, testing, benchmarking), and MANAGE (response, prioritisation, treatment, residual risk). This tool maps 17 controls across these functions and returns per-function coverage and gaps. Feeds GPAI Code of Practice checker (art-175).
