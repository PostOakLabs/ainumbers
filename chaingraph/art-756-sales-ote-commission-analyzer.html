Sales OTE & Commission Analyzer

Gross W-2 model for an On-Target-Earnings offer: base plus variable as a function of quota attainment, with an optional accelerator tier and an optional recoverable draw. Ported from Apex #89 (AL-96).

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data; use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope & reliance
This node models gross pay only. It computes no tax liability: after_tax_at_target is gross W-2 times a single flat rate you supply, which ignores the standard deduction and the 2026 marginal bracket schedule (Rev. Proc. 2025-32 §4.01), and it is not a take-home figure. It computes no FLSA regular rate and no overtime: the commission this node prices is "a percentage of total sales", which 29 CFR 778.117 says "must be included in the regular rate" if overtime is owed; that leg belongs to art-340-compute-flsa-regular-rate, and whether overtime is owed turns on the duty test at 29 CFR 541.500, not on the job title (29 CFR 541.2). The risk labels (high at ≥60%, moderate at ≥40% of OTE in variable pay), the 6-month clawback threshold and the 25-point highlight bucket are the tool's own editorial constants with no cited source; they are not regulatory. The draw figures are arithmetic on the draw you supply, not a reading of any offer letter. Not tax advice. Not wage-hour legal advice. Not compensation advice. The port reproduces the Apex page faithfully, including its parseFloat(x) || default quirk, which silently replaces an explicit 0; see apex_zero_coercion_applied in the output.
