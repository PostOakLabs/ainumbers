FRIA & Post-Market Monitoring Plan Builder

The flagship deployer lifecycle tool: builds an Art 27 Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA), an Art 72 post-market monitoring plan, and assesses Art 12 logging + Art 14 human oversight for banks and insurers using a high-risk AI system. Also maps the Art 73 serious-incident reporting path. Distinct from tool 451 (SR 11-7 model-risk gap assessor — that is the model-risk evidence this FRIA cites, not the FRIA itself). Part of the ai-governance-fria-monitoring and ai-governance-credit-ai-conformity chains.
