EMIR Reconciliation Break Ageing

Diffs this cycle's break set (e.g. from art-482 emir-recon-adjudicator) against the prior cycle's sealed break set by stable break_key: newly-opened / persisting / newly-closed split, an ageing bucket, a recurrence count, and an escalation-clock status — all against policy-supplied ageing limits, reusing the art-428 cyber-incident-clock deadline-vs-evaluated_at pattern. Final step of the EMIR Inter-TR Reconciliation Cycle, where a break past its policy ageing limit routes to a §27 escalate gate.
