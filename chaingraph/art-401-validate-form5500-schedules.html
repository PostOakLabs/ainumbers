ERISA Form 5500 Schedule Validator

Validates the Form 5500 schedule-applicability matrix (plan type and size determine required schedules H/I/A/C/G/MB/SB/R), the Schedule H cross-schedule arithmetic tie, and the filing-deadline calculation (plan-year end plus seven months, plus the Form 5558 extension).

ERISA Sec 103-104 Form-Lint, Not Advice Record Integrity Family
🔒 All validation runs locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real plan data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
⚠ SHELF-ROW NOTE: this is FORM-LINT (structural schedule applicability and arithmetic), not retirement or plan-design advice — it sits in the compliance-mechanics lane, not the advice lane the options-shelf constraint fences off.
