Cross-Network Atomic Settlement Validator

Validate atomic settlement across two or more networks: cash leg final on the money ledger, asset leg delivered on the asset ledger, FX leg PvP where present. Detects finality mismatches between legs, non-atomic cross-network risk, and PvP gaps per CPMI-IOSCO PFMI Principles 8 & 12. Models the BIS Agorá unifying-ledger, ECB Pontes TARGET-link, and DTCC Collateral AppChain coordination patterns. Distinct from Wave 8 Canton single-network DvP — this checks the cross-network seam.

Educational estimator · not a live settlement instruction or legal advice · Verify BIS Agorá / ECB Pontes / DTCC mechanics against current primary sources
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
