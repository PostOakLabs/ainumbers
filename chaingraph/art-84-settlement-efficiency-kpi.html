ART-84 · Wave 17 · Settlement Discipline · Efficiency KPIs

Settlement Efficiency KPI Aggregator

Aggregates batch-level settlement data into CSDR/T+1-relevant KPIs: settlement rate (graded A–F), fail rate, on-time allocation, SSI golden-source coverage, CSDR penalty cost, buy-in triggered count, and fail-duration distribution. Benchmarked against ESMA 97.5% settlement rate. Part of the sd-audit-pack chain.

ESMA Benchmark 97.5% T+1 Industry Target ≥99.5% CSDR Reg. (EU) 909/2014 Art 7 EU T+1 Go-Live 11 Oct 2027
