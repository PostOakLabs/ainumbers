ZK Compliance Proof Generator

Generate synthetic zero-knowledge compliance proofs for AML/Travel Rule predicates. Select a predicate (transaction below threshold, counterparty not on sanctions list, account KYC-complete) and generate a synthetic ZK proof token that asserts the predicate is satisfied — without revealing the underlying data. Client-side NTT simulation (Number Theoretic Transform) models the proof computation. GDPR Article 25 data-minimisation principle. Explicitly educational — no production cryptographic security. Chains into ART-10.

AP2 Export Chains: ART-10 ZK Proof · NTT Simulation Educational Only Zero PII GDPR Art. 25
⚗️ Educational simulation only. This tool simulates the structure and workflow of ZK compliance proofs. The "proofs" generated are synthetic cryptographic tokens — they are NOT cryptographically secure or verifiable by a real ZK verifier. The NTT computation models the number-theoretic transform used in real ZK-SNARK/STARK backends (e.g., Plonk, FRI, Halo2) but does not produce a sound proof. Use for learning, demos, and architecture planning only.
🔒 Private witness data (transaction amounts, counterparty IDs) is never stored or transmitted. The proof output contains only the synthetic proof token and public inputs. Zero PII.
