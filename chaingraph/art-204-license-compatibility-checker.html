License Compatibility Checker

Checks whether a child work licensed under B can legally derive from a parent asset licensed under A. Returns a compatible flag, reason codes, and the required child license when ShareAlike or reciprocal terms force a specific choice. Also runs an SPDX-satisfies check for Creative Commons families.

Compliance Mandate CC / CBE / PIL / Embedded SPDX Satisfies Not Legal Advice
Not legal advice. This tool checks published license compatibility rules. The published license texts govern all rights. Consult a licensed attorney for your jurisdiction.
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.

License of the parent/source asset.
