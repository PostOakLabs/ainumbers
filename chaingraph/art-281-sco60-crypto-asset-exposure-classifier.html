SCO60 Crypto-Asset Exposure Classifier

Classify a crypto-asset position into Basel SCO60 Group 1a, 1b, 2a, or 2b (BCBS d545, Prudential treatment of cryptoasset exposures), apply the Group 1 infrastructure-risk capital add-on, and check the Group 2 exposure limit of 1% of Tier 1 capital. National implementation timelines are still fragmenting; this tool never asserts a jurisdiction has adopted the standard. Zero network.

SCO60 BCBS d545 Group 1 / 2 Zero PII W3C VC §13.11
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope
Basel Committee SCO60 (BCBS d545, Dec 2022 standard). Group 1a: tokenized traditional asset meeting classification conditions. Group 1b: asset-referenced token / stablecoin meeting classification conditions. Group 2a: Group 2 exposure with an effective, recognised hedge. Group 2b: unhedged Group 2, conservative treatment (1250% risk weight). Net long and short Group 2 exposure must not exceed 1% of Tier 1 capital (SCO60.14): a breach requires the WHOLE Group 2 book to receive Group 2b treatment. National implementation status varies by jurisdiction; re-verify before citing as adopted.
