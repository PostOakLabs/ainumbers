SE Tax and Deductions

Recomputes US federal self-employment tax from values you already hold: the Schedule SE regular-method tax split into its 12.4 percent social security part, capped at the year contribution and benefit base less social security wages, and its 2.9 percent Medicare part, uncapped; the 0.9 percent Additional Medicare Tax on net earnings over the filing-status threshold reduced by wages; and the above-the-line deduction of one half of the SE tax. Supported years are 2025 and 2026. A recompute and cross-check, not return preparation and not tax advice.

🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
What it computes

Net earnings enter at 92.35 percent of profit (26 USC 1402(a)(12); Pub 334 ch.10). The 12.4 percent social security part applies only up to the year base less social security wages (26 USC 1402(b)(1): base 176100 dollars for 2025 and 184500 for 2026 per the SSA table). The 2.9 percent Medicare part has no maximum. The 0.9 percent Additional Medicare Tax applies to net earnings over a 250000 dollar joint, 125000 dollar married-filing-separately or 200000 dollar other threshold, reduced by wages (26 USC 1401(b)(2); Form 8959 threshold chart). One half of the SE tax, excluding the Additional Medicare Tax, is deductible (26 USC 164(f)(1)).

Floors are applied separately: no tax on non-church net earnings under 400 dollars; church employee income under 108.28 dollars is excluded; church net earnings under 100 dollars contribute nothing. A loss is not considered and is flagged. Farm and nonfarm optional methods refuse by name (method: "farm" or "nonfarm" is a named refusal). A self-employment loss in one business offsets another inside the combined profit you supply.

Try it locally

Paste a policy_parameters object. tax_year (2025 or 2026), filing_status (single, mfj, mfs, hoh, qss), net_se_profit in dollars (a loss may be negative), w2_wages_ss in social-security wages, and the optional church_employee_income. Values only: no identity fields exist on the input surface.
