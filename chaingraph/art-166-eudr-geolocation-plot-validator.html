EUDR Geolocation Plot Validator

Validates EUDR Art. 9(1)(d) plot-level geolocation requirements: Point geometry for plots under 4 ha, Polygon with closed ring for 4 ha and above. Checks coordinate ranges, size rule, and polygon closure. Zero network.

EUDR 2023/1115 GeoJSON Art. 9(1)(d) Zero PII W3C VC §13.11 v0.5.0
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
Scope
Step 2 of eudr-due-diligence-statement-validation chain (art-165→166→167). EUDR Art. 9(1)(d) requires plot-level geolocation: Point for plots <4 ha, Polygon with closed ring for ≥4 ha. Micro-operators (<10 employees, <€2M) may use postal address. Validates GeoJSON type, coordinate ranges, size rule, polygon closure. Feeds commodity scope classifier (art-167). See eudr-due-diligence-statement-validation chain.

For Polygon/MultiPolygon: enter a representative centroid. The validator checks type eligibility and closure rules. Provide actual polygon coordinates via MCP for full ring validation.
