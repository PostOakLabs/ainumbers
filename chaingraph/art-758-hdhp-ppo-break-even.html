HDHP vs. PPO Break-Even

Model the annual cost of an HDHP+HSA against a PPO at any spending level, including the HSA tax shield, and find the medical-spend level where the HDHP flips cheaper. Client-side, zero PII. Port of ApexLogics #105 (AL-112).

Scope & reliance
Plan-cost model only: premium + in-network cost sharing up to the out-of-pocket maximum, minus the HSA tax shield on contributions up to the 2026 coverage limit (individual $4,400; family $8,750; IRS Rev. Proc. 2025-19 §2.01(1)). The break-even is the first $50 grid point in [0, 20000] at which the HDHP total is ≤ the PPO total. Out of scope: FSA/HRA interactions, out-of-network and balance billing, prescription tiers, premium tax credits, state HSA deduction rules, HSA investment growth, and the age-55+ catch-up. as_of is an input; the node reads no clock.
