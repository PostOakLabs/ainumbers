AI Addendum Assembler

Assembles a Common Paper AI Addendum (Version 1.0, CC BY 4.0) from your Cover Page Key Terms: whether Training is permitted, non-training improvement, data retention, output ownership, and AI sub-processor disclosure. The Standard Terms body below is vendored verbatim and never modified — only the Cover Page varies. Emits a contract-api.json variable map twin for agent consumption.

Compliance Mandate Fixed Body Verbatim Zero-PII Cover Page Only Not Legal Advice
