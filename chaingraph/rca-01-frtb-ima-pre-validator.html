FRTB IMA Expected Shortfall Pre-Validator

Educational simulator for FRTB Internal Models Approach (IMA) Expected Shortfall computation across a synthetic trading book. Covers liquidity horizons LH1–LH5 (10–120 days), NMRF surcharge estimation, PLA test status, and IMA capital vs SA floor comparison. For pre-validation and educational scenario analysis ahead of UK IMA go-live January 2028. All computation is client-side — no trading book data transmitted.
