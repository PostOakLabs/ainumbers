CBAM Embedded-Emissions Calculator

Flagship importer tool. Computes embedded emissions (direct + indirect, tCO₂e) for a consignment of CBAM goods from actual installation data or Commission default values, applying the system boundaries of the Implementing Regulation. Deduct any carbon price already paid in the country of origin. Handles precursor emissions from ART-72. Part of the cbam-liability and cbam-precursor chains.
