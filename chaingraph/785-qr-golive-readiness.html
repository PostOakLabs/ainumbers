QR Go-Live Readiness Assessor

Scores a QR (or small-market instant payment) scheme's readiness for a go or no-go call at three phases (pilot, production, cross-border linkage) over weighted gates: participant certification, UAT completion, configuration sign-off, operating procedures, incident and support cover, communications, and cross-border link agreements. Any blocking gate not met is a NO-GO; a UAT summary with an open severity-1 defect or a failed pass count fails the UAT gate; otherwise the weighted score sets GO or GO-WITH-CONDITIONS. A Level One principles checklist rides along as advisory only. Decision-support draft: not a supervisory assessment, and no real participant or scheme operator's readiness state is named anywhere on this page.
