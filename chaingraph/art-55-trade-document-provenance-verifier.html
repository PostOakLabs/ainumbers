Trade Document Provenance & Consistency Verifier

Cross-validates a trade-document set (eBL, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin, insurance certificate) for internal consistency and computes a Merkle provenance root. Flags TBML red flags — over/under-invoicing, phantom shipments, mismatched goods/value. Client-side, zero-egress; emits a hash-anchored OpenChainGraph v0.4 artifact.

Educational TBML screen — not a SAR determination · FATF typologies · ICC DSI KTDDE
🔒 All inputs are processed locally in your browser. No data is transmitted. Do not enter real personal data — use synthetic or anonymised inputs only.
