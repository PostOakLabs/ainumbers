Threat models

One short model per public surface. Written from the code and the CI gates as of 5 November 2026.

How to read these pages

Each page names what the surface protects, where the trust boundaries sit, who we assume might attack it, which threats are in and out of scope, and the control that addresses each in-scope threat. Every control cites a CI gate or a code path. Where nothing enforces a property mechanically, the page says UNTESTABLE and does not claim it. The residual risk section lists what is left after the controls.

These pages describe design intent that the cited files back up. They are not an audit, a certification or a guarantee.

Surfaces

  • Site pages: the static tool, guide and hub pages on ainumbers.co.
  • MCP endpoint: the stateless compute service at mcp.ainumbers.co.
  • Verification ledger: the in-browser receipt ledger.
  • Anchor relay: the timestamping service at anchor.ainumbers.co.
  • Data handling: what each surface receives and records, retention, and the service provider behind it.

The local Helm daemon has its own threat model, kept with its source in the Helm repository (docs/THREAT-MODEL.md). It is a separate liability class and is not summarised here.
