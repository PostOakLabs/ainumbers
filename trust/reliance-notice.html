Reliance notice

Audience: anyone relying on the output of a tool on this site that references a regulation, standard, or supervisory framework
Applies to: every tool page linking to this notice, and the equivalent notice carried inside a tool's exported Policy Mandate
This tool runs in your browser and is provided for information. It is not legal, tax, accounting, or investment advice, and it is not a regulatory determination. Regulations change, and any reference shown here may not match the text currently in force. Check the source instrument and take professional advice before relying on an output.

Why this notice travels with an export

A tool's Policy Mandate export can carry this same notice as a dated, attributed field: a statement, the date it was current as of, and who issued it. That dating is deliberate and differs from the page copy above, which carries no date. An export held for months or years is exactly the situation this notice is written for: the artifact should say, on its own, what it claimed and as of when, without depending on this page still saying the same thing when someone opens the file later.
